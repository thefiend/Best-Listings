## 1. Executive Summary

**Current state:** 72 articles live, averaging 1.7 clicks/day (Aug 2026).

**Target:** 100 clicks/day by Aug 2027 — a 58x increase in 12 months.

**Strategy:** Three phases of deliberate content expansion across nine thematic clusters. The core insight driving this plan: every "best X singapore" article with 10 listed companies automatically generates 5–15 branded review queries ("Company X review", "Is Company X good?") that compound over time without additional effort. At scale, branded review traffic becomes the primary growth engine.

**Three phases:**
1. Foundation (Weeks 1–12, Aug–Oct): Fill the massive food/restaurant gap, add healthcare
2. Volume (Weeks 13–28, Nov 2026–Feb 2027): Complete clusters, add location variants
3. Authority (Weeks 29–52, Mar–Aug 2027): Guide articles, niche expansion, article refreshes

**Key insight:** The site currently has ZERO food articles outside halal-restaurants and brunch-cafes — yet Singapore food keywords represent 60%+ of total addressable search volume at LOW-to-MEDIUM competition. This is the single biggest opportunity.

### Maturity Math

At 250 articles with an average 12 clicks/month per article:
- 250 × 12 = **3,000 clicks/month = 100 clicks/day**

Average 12 clicks/month is conservative — articles covering 4,400/mo keywords (ramen, chicken rice, katong food) will far exceed this. Articles covering 300/mo keywords will be below it. The blend is achievable.

### Branded Review Query Multiplier

Each article lists ~10 companies. Each company generates roughly 0.5–1.5 branded queries/month once the article ranks.
- 250 articles × 10 companies = 2,500 company names indexed
- At 0.8 branded queries/company/month avg = **2,000 additional clicks/month from branded tail alone**

This branded tail is invisible in keyword research tools but becomes the majority of traffic at scale — it is the compounding flywheel.

## 3. Content Clusters

### Cluster 1: Food & Restaurants (Target: 40 articles)
Singapore has among the highest food search volumes in Southeast Asia. The site has a near-total gap here despite halal-restaurants and brunch-cafes being live. Food articles earn strong engagement signals (dwell time, return visits) that help all other articles rank.

**Interlinking:** All food articles link to a central "best hawker food singapore" hub. Location variant food articles link back to the cuisine-type articles (e.g., best food in katong links to best ramen and best zi char).

**Featured snippet opportunities:** "What is the best ramen in Singapore?" — numbered list format captures Position 0.

**Branded review potential:** Very high. Restaurant names are searched frequently by locals vetting options.

### Cluster 2: Education & Tuition (Target: 12 articles)
Foundation is good (math, english, science, swimming) but misses: primary-specific, secondary-specific, enrichment classes, music, arts, coding. Parents are high-intent searchers who bookmark and return.

**Interlinking:** All tuition articles link to a "best tuition centres singapore" hub (existing). Music and arts articles link to enrichment hub.

### Cluster 3: Healthcare & Wellness (Target: 10 articles)
Has dentists, plastic surgeons, physiotherapy. Missing: TCM, orthodontist, dermatologist, chiropractor, eye specialist, GP clinics. Healthcare queries have moderate competition and high branded review potential — patients actively search clinic names.

### Cluster 4: Home & Living (Target: 8 articles)
Good coverage (renovation, cleaning, aircon) but missing: curtains, mattress, wardrobe, kitchen cabinets, smart home, CCTV.

### Cluster 5: Beauty & Personal Care (Target: 8 articles)
Has hair, nails, massage/spa. Missing: facials, eyelash extensions, waxing, brow services, laser hair removal, slimming centres.

### Cluster 6: Finance & Legal (Target: 6 articles)
Has divorce lawyers, corporate lawyers, mortgage brokers, audit firms, insurance. Missing: personal loans, money lenders, financial advisors. High-intent searches, good CPM potential.

### Cluster 7: Location Variants (Target: 20 articles)
Area-specific "best food in [area]" articles capture long-tail with lower competition than generic Singapore terms. Katong (4,400/mo, LOW) is the #1 priority. Also: Tiong Bahru, Holland Village, Dempsey, Tampines, Bedok, Jurong, Little India, Kampong Glam, Woodlands, Yishun, CBD, Orchard.

**Interlinking:** Each location article links to relevant cuisine-type articles. Location cluster links to each other via a "Singapore food by area" navigation strategy.

### Cluster 8: Guides & Comparisons (Target: 15 articles)
Informational "how to choose" and cost-guide articles rank for featured snippets and attract inbound links. They also support existing articles (e.g., "HDB renovation cost guide" supports home-renovation article).

### Cluster 9: Events & Seasonal (Target: 8 articles/year)
CNY, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Christmas dining guides. High intent at time of search. Publish 6–8 weeks before the event for indexing time.

## 5. Cluster Deep-Dives

### Food & Restaurants
**Why it works:** Singapore is a global food city. Local searches for hawker dishes and restaurant types are evergreen, high-frequency, and low-competition relative to volume. Food content earns natural backlinks from travel blogs, food Instagrams, and local media.

**Interlinking strategy:** Hub-and-spoke. `best-hawker-food-singapore` is the hub. Cuisine articles (ramen, dim sum, zi char) link to each other via "you might also like" sections and inline contextual links. Location articles (katong, tiong bahru) link to relevant cuisine articles.

**Featured snippet opportunities:** "What is the best chicken rice in Singapore?" — numbered list (Position 0). "Where to eat dim sum in Singapore" — table format with location and price.

**Branded review potential:** Very high. Diners actively search "[restaurant name] review" before booking. An article listing 10 ramen restaurants generates 10 branded review query opportunities, each compounding monthly.

### Education & Tuition
**Why it works:** Singapore parents are extremely active searchers during enrollment windows (Oct–Jan for primary, Mar–May for secondary). Once an article ranks, it re-ranks every year during the same window.

**Interlinking:** All tuition articles → tuition-centres hub. Music/arts articles → enrichment-classes hub. Subject-specific articles (math, english, science) cross-link by level (primary vs secondary).

**Featured snippets:** "What is the best math tuition in Singapore?" — numbered list. "How much does tuition cost in Singapore?" — cost table for featured snippet.

### Healthcare & Wellness
**Why it works:** Healthcare searches have high commercial intent (patients are ready to book) and strong branded follow-up. A patient who finds a clinic through an article will search that clinic's name multiple times over months.

**Interlinking:** Healthcare articles cross-link by specialty type. TCM, chiropractor, and physiotherapist link to each other. Dentists, orthodontists, and oral surgeons link cluster. Eye specialist links to plastic-surgeons and dermatologist.

**Featured snippets:** "What is the difference between TCM and physiotherapy?" — paragraph snippet opportunity.

### Location Variants
**Why it works:** "[area] food" queries are systematically lower competition than generic Singapore queries because large review sites (HungryGoWhere, TimeOut) focus on citywide lists. Area-specific articles capture the resident who wants options near home.

**Interlinking:** All location articles link to relevant cuisine articles. The katong article links to seafood, zi char, and ramen articles. A planned location-index page ("best food by area in singapore") acts as a second hub.
