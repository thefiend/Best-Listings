Eyelash extensions in Singapore have evolved far beyond a niche beauty treatment: they are now a mainstream grooming choice for working professionals, brides, and anyone who wants to wake up looking polished without a daily mascara routine. The challenge is not finding a studio, it is finding one that applies extensions safely, uses quality materials, and delivers results that last a full refill cycle without premature shedding. We evaluated over 50 eyelash extension studios across Singapore, scoring each on Google rating, review volume, range of techniques offered (classic, volume, mega-volume, lash lift), aftercare quality, hygiene standards, and the absence of aggressive package-selling tactics. Our minimum threshold was 4.8 stars and at least 100 verified Google reviews. Duplicate brand locations were consolidated, ranked on the best-performing outlet's data. The result is a shortlist of 10 studios that consistently deliver beautiful, long-lasting extensions across a range of styles, budgets, and locations. Whether you are new to extensions or a regular looking for a better studio, this guide gives you an objective, data-backed answer.

## How We Ranked These Eyelash Extension Studios

We applied a systematic scoring framework to every eyelash extension studio we considered. To qualify for inclusion, a studio needed a minimum Google rating of 4.8 stars and at least 100 verified Google reviews at the time of evaluation. Studios with a pattern of complaints about premature lash shedding within one week, allergic reaction incidents attributed to poor product quality, or aggressive package upselling were excluded regardless of their overall score.

We weighted Google rating and review volume most heavily because these represent aggregated, independently verified client experiences at scale. We then assessed: the breadth of techniques offered (classic, Russian volume, mega-volume, hybrid, lash lift and tint), the quality of the pre-application consultation process, evidence of hygiene practices such as single-use application tools and patch testing availability, pricing transparency, and location accessibility via public transport.

Where a brand operates multiple outlets, we included the brand as a single entry and based the ranking on the best-performing outlet. This prevents large chains from dominating the list simply by accumulating reviews across many locations. Our goal was to produce a list that reflects genuine quality of experience, not just the size of a brand's marketing operation.

## What to Look for When Choosing Eyelash Extensions in Singapore

**Understand the different extension techniques before booking.** Classic extensions apply one extension per natural lash and deliver a clean, defined look. Volume sets apply a handmade fan of lightweight extensions per lash for more fullness. Mega-volume uses even denser fans for maximum impact. Lash lifts curl your natural lashes without adding extensions. Knowing which technique suits your natural lash density and your desired level of drama prevents disappointment and helps you ask the right questions when consulting a stylist.

**Ask about lash material quality.** Most reputable studios use synthetic mink or silk lash extensions. Real mink lashes are rarely used in Singapore due to ethical concerns and import regulations. The quality of the synthetic material matters: good-quality extensions feel soft and lightweight, hold their curl over time, and do not look plasticky or stiff. Ask the studio what brand of extensions they use and whether they can show you the material before application.

**Verify that patch testing is available.** Eyelash extension adhesive contains cyanoacrylate compounds that can cause allergic reactions in some people. A reputable studio will offer a patch test at least 24–48 hours before a first-time full application. This is particularly important if you have sensitive skin, existing eye conditions, or known adhesive allergies. Skipping the patch test is a risk that the best studios will discourage.

**Assess retention claims critically.** Healthy natural lash retention for quality extensions is two to four weeks before a refill is needed, with shedding accelerating as lashes reach the end of their natural growth cycle. Studios claiming six-week retention without a refill are overstating typical outcomes. The studios on this list are noted for honest retention expectations and refill cycles that match what clients actually experience.

**Look for transparent pricing with no hidden add-ons.** Eyelash extension pricing in Singapore is frequently quoted at a base rate that excludes aftercare products, lash sealing treatments, or removal fees. Before booking, confirm what is included in the quoted price and whether there are any standard add-ons that the studio considers part of a complete service. The best studios include a clear price breakdown on their website or booking page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much do eyelash extensions cost in Singapore?

Eyelash extension prices in Singapore range from around SGD 60 for a basic classic set at a budget studio to SGD 250 or more for a premium volume or mega-volume set at a top-rated studio. Mid-range quality studios typically charge SGD 90 to SGD 160 for a full classic set and SGD 130 to SGD 220 for a volume set. Refill pricing is generally 50 to 70 percent of the full-set price. Lash lifts and tints typically cost SGD 60 to SGD 120. Always confirm whether the quoted price is inclusive or exclusive of aftercare products and removal.

### How long do eyelash extensions last in Singapore?

In Singapore's humid climate, most clients can expect their extensions to last two to four weeks before a refill is needed, with individual lashes shedding at the rate of the natural lash growth cycle. Humidity and sebum production can affect retention: clients with oilier skin may need refills closer to the two-week mark, while those with drier skin and a slower natural lash cycle may get close to four weeks. Extensions do not come out all at once: they shed gradually as the natural lash sheds, so the full set thins progressively until a refill restores it.

### Will eyelash extensions damage my natural lashes?

Properly applied eyelash extensions by a qualified stylist using appropriate weight and length for your natural lash density should not cause permanent damage. Problems arise when extensions are too long or too heavy for the natural lash, causing stress on the hair follicle over repeated cycles. A skilled stylist will assess your natural lash condition at each appointment and recommend extensions that do not exceed the load your natural lashes can support. If you notice your natural lashes getting shorter or sparser after repeated extension sets, it is a signal to take a break or switch to a lighter technique.

### What is the difference between classic, volume, and mega-volume extensions?

Classic extensions apply one synthetic extension per one natural lash, creating a look similar to mascara but more defined and longer-lasting. Volume extensions use a fan of two to six very thin extensions per natural lash, creating more fullness and density without significantly more weight because each individual extension is much thinner than a classic extension. Mega-volume uses fans of seven or more ultra-thin extensions per lash for maximum dramatic effect. Hybrid sets mix classic and volume techniques for a textured, layered look. The right choice depends on your natural lash density and the level of drama you want.

### How should I look after eyelash extensions?

For the first 24 hours after application, avoid water, steam, and oil-based products near the eye area to allow the adhesive to fully cure. After that, clean your lashes daily using a lash-safe, oil-free cleanser applied with a soft brush to remove makeup residue and sebum, which are the primary cause of premature shedding. Avoid oil-based eye creams and makeup removers near the lash line. Sleep on your back where possible to avoid friction on the extensions. Brush your lashes gently each morning with a clean lash wand to keep the extensions aligned.

### Can I wear mascara with eyelash extensions?

Most lash stylists advise against mascara with extensions, especially on the extension portion of the lash. Mascara residue is difficult to remove completely from extension material, builds up over time, and accelerates shedding. If you want more drama between refills, a clear lash serum applied by your stylist can add temporary shine without the adhesive residue issue. Waterproof mascara is particularly problematic as its removal requires an oil-based remover that will dissolve the lash adhesive.

### What is a lash lift and how does it differ from extensions?

A lash lift uses a chemical perming process to curl your natural lashes upward from the root, creating an open-eyed effect without adding any synthetic material. It is combined with a lash tint to darken the natural lash colour, mimicking the effect of mascara for two to three months with no daily maintenance required. Lash lifts are ideal for clients with naturally long but straight lashes who want low-maintenance enhancement, or for those taking a break from extensions to allow their natural lashes to recover. Extensions add material and volume; a lash lift works entirely with what you already have.

### How often should I get a refill for eyelash extensions?

Most clients need a refill every two to four weeks to maintain a full, even look. A refill replaces the extensions that have shed on naturally shed lashes and fills in gaps. If you wait longer than four weeks between refills, the set thins significantly and you may need a fuller infill at close to full-set pricing. Most studios charge a lower refill rate if you come within three weeks and a higher rate for fills done after four or more weeks because more extensions need to be applied. Regular, timely refills are more cost-effective over time than allowing the set to become very sparse and requiring a near-full rebuild.

### Are eyelash extensions safe for people with sensitive eyes?

They can be, with appropriate precautions. Sensitivity reactions to eyelash extensions are usually caused by the adhesive rather than the extension material itself. A patch test done 24–48 hours before a full application can identify whether you have a reaction to the specific adhesive a studio uses. Some studios offer a lower-formaldehyde or sensitive-formula adhesive for clients with a history of mild reactions. If you have active eye conditions, recent eye surgery, or severe allergies, consult your ophthalmologist before getting extensions.
