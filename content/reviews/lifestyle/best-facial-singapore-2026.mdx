Facials in Singapore cover a wide spectrum, from deep-cleansing extraction sessions at neighbourhood beauty salons to clinical medical-grade procedures at licensed medispas and aesthetic clinics. Finding the best facial in Singapore is not simply a matter of following the highest price point or the trendiest brand: the key variables are the therapist's skill level, the quality of the skincare products used, the facility's standards of hygiene, and whether the treatment is tailored to your specific skin type and concerns. We evaluated over 50 facial providers across Singapore, applying a systematic scoring framework that weighted Google review rating, review volume, treatment specialisation, location accessibility, and the absence of high-pressure upselling, to identify the 10 best facial providers in 2026. Whether you are looking for a thorough extraction facial to address congested pores, a Korean-style hydration treatment, or a medical spa experience with clinical-grade results, this guide gives you an objective, data-backed shortlist.

**Key Takeaways**

- **Best overall:** Caring Skin (4.9 stars, 2,482 Google reviews at Clementi), Singapore's most reviewed facial specialist with a personalised skin-coaching approach and consistent results for acne and sensitive skin

## How We Ranked These Facial Providers

We evaluated each facial provider using the following criteria: Google Maps rating (minimum 4.8 stars to qualify for consideration), Google review count (minimum 100 verified reviews required), treatment specialisation and service range, evidence of staff training and certification, hygiene and facility standards based on customer feedback, pricing transparency, and the absence of high-pressure sales practices. Providers with a pattern of complaints about aggressive upselling or misleading package terms were excluded regardless of their overall rating. We also excluded providers with no direct booking mechanism, those operating without a traceable business address, and outlets with fewer than 100 individual reviews. Multiple outlets of the same chain were treated as a single entry, with ranking based on the best-performing outlet's data. Our final 10 represent the best combination of quality, trust, and verifiable customer satisfaction across different facial types, price tiers, and locations in Singapore.

## What to Look for When Choosing a Facial in Singapore

**Understand your primary skin concern.** The best facial for congested skin with blackheads is not the same as the best facial for dehydrated, dull skin, and both differ from what is optimal for sensitive or reactive skin. Before booking, identify whether your main concern is extraction, hydration, brightening, anti-ageing, or acne management, and look for a provider that specialises in that area rather than one that offers every treatment type generically.

**Verify the therapist's qualifications.** In Singapore, the beauty industry does not require nationally standardised certification for facial therapists, which means skill levels vary significantly across salons. Look for providers whose therapists have completed recognised training programmes such as CIDESCO, ITEC, or equivalent certifications, or who have formal training with specific product ranges such as Dermalogica, Laneige, or other professional-grade brands.

**Ask about product quality before you book.** The products used during a facial make a significant difference to both the immediate experience and the post-treatment condition of your skin. Professional-grade ranges such as Dermalogica, Guinot, Comfort Zone, and Sothys are formulated for use under therapist supervision and are meaningfully different from retail skincare products. A salon willing to discuss what they use and why is a good signal of transparency.

**Be sceptical of aggressive package offers.** Singapore's beauty industry has a well-documented history of high-pressure package selling. Legitimate high-quality providers let their results and reviews speak for themselves and do not rely on scare tactics or limited-time pressure to sell long-term commitments. Every provider on this list has reviews specifically noting the absence of hard selling as a positive attribute.

**Check whether the space is clean and professional.** Facial treatments involve opening pores and working directly with skin. Hygiene standards for tools, linens, and treatment areas are non-negotiable. Any reputable provider will use fresh linen for each client, sterilised or single-use tools, and maintain a visibly clean treatment room.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a facial cost in Singapore?

Facial prices in Singapore range from around SGD 60 for a basic express session at a Korean express chain to SGD 300 or more for a premium medical spa or clinical treatment. Mid-range quality facials at reputable beauty salons typically cost between SGD 100 and SGD 180 per session. Package pricing, if you choose to purchase, generally offers a lower per-session cost but requires an upfront commitment: always read the package terms carefully before signing, including refund and transfer policies.

### How often should I get a facial in Singapore?

Most skincare professionals recommend a facial every four to six weeks, which aligns with the skin's natural cell turnover cycle of approximately 28 days. In Singapore's humid climate, clients with oily or congested skin may benefit from monthly sessions, while those with normal or dry skin may find every six weeks sufficient. Clients with specific concerns such as acne or hyperpigmentation being actively treated may need more frequent sessions as recommended by their therapist.

### What is the difference between a beauty salon facial and a medical-grade facial?

A beauty salon facial uses cosmetic-grade skincare products applied by a trained therapist, focusing on cleansing, extraction, hydration, and relaxation. A medical-grade facial at a medispa or aesthetic clinic uses clinical-grade products and may incorporate medical devices such as chemical peels at higher concentrations, laser or light therapy, or microdermabrasion, typically under clinical supervision. Medical-grade facials can address more significant skin concerns but cost more and may require a consultation rather than a direct booking.

### What should I avoid before getting a facial?

Avoid exfoliating your skin at home in the 48 hours before a facial, as over-exfoliation combined with professional extraction can cause sensitivity and irritation. Do not use retinol, AHA, or BHA products for at least two days before your session. Arrive with a clean face if possible, or at minimum without heavy makeup. Inform your therapist of any active breakouts, skin conditions, or recent treatments such as laser or injectables so they can adapt their approach accordingly.

### What should I do after a facial in Singapore?

In the 24–48 hours following a facial, avoid heavy makeup, direct sun exposure without SPF protection, saunas, and intense exercise that causes significant sweating. Your skin will be more receptive to skincare products after a professional cleanse and treatment, so apply your moisturiser and any active serums as directed by your therapist. Do not pick or squeeze any areas that were treated during the session, even if mild purging occurs in the days after extraction.

### Is a facial worth it for men?

Yes. Men's skin often has more active sebaceous glands and tends to experience more congestion, particularly in the T-zone, making extraction facials especially effective. Men's facials have grown significantly in popularity in Singapore, and several providers on this list including SGFACE Medispa and faceofman are specifically noted for their male client experience. There is no physiological reason why facials are less effective for men's skin, and regular professional facials can meaningfully improve skin clarity and reduce shaving-related irritation.

### How do I choose between an extraction facial and a hydration facial?

If your primary concern is blackheads, congested pores, or mild acne, an extraction facial will address the root cause more directly. If your main issue is dull, tight, or flaky skin, particularly common in air-conditioned environments in Singapore, a hydration facial will deliver more noticeable improvement. For most people, the ideal is alternating between extraction and hydration treatments depending on what their skin is doing at each point in their cycle and the season. A good therapist will advise you on which approach suits your skin at each visit rather than applying the same treatment every time.

### What is a Korean facial and why is it popular in Singapore?

Korean facials are based on Korean beauty (K-beauty) philosophy, which prioritises skin health, hydration, and a methodical, layered approach to skincare over heavy coverage or quick fixes. The treatments typically involve a multi-step cleansing process, a thorough but gentle extraction technique that minimises trauma to the skin, and an intensive hydration phase using sheet masks or ampoules. They are popular in Singapore because they deliver noticeable clarity and glow improvements that many clients find superior to Western-style facials at a similar or lower price point. CHEONGDAM and Kskin are the best-established Korean facial providers on this list.
