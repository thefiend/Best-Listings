The best cafes in Singapore sit at the intersection of excellent coffee, thoughtfully composed food menus, and spaces designed for the way Singaporeans actually use them: long weekend brunches, remote work sessions, post-workout catch-ups, and date mornings that stretch into the afternoon. Singapore's cafe culture has grown from a niche specialty coffee movement in the early 2010s into one of the most developed in Asia, producing independent cafes that hold their own against the best in Melbourne, Tokyo, and London. Whether you are looking for the city's highest-rated all-day brunch, a specialty espresso roaster, a unique cat cafe experience, or a tucked-away neighbourhood spot that rewards regulars, Singapore's cafe landscape has options at every price point and postcode.

This guide covers the ten best cafes in Singapore for 2026, ranked primarily by Google review ratings and review volume, then assessed for food quality, coffee programme, ambiance, and value. Every cafe on this list holds at least a 4.6-star Google rating. The top pick, Good Bites in Bishan, carries 22,297 Google reviews at 4.9 stars: a volume and consistency of positive feedback that places it among the most highly rated food establishments in Singapore by any measure.

**Key Takeaways**

- **Best overall:** Good Bites: a 4.9-star Google-rated cafe in Bishan with 22,297 reviews, the most-reviewed and highest-rated cafe in Singapore by a significant margin
- **Best cat cafe:** QQ Cats & Cafe: 4.9 stars and 984 reviews in Balestier, offering a unique cafe experience with resident cats and a full food and drinks menu
- **Best for brunch crowds:** My Awesome Cafe: 4.7 stars, 7,903 reviews, centrally located in Telok Ayer with one of the largest Google review counts among Singapore cafes

- **Best Botanic Gardens cafe:** Wildseed Cafe At The Garage: 4.6 stars, 1,722 reviews, set within the Singapore Botanic Gardens UNESCO World Heritage Site
- **Best Orchard Road cafe:** FYP (For You People) Cafe: 4.7 stars, 1,187 reviews, in Orchard Central with desserts, ice cream, and bakery items alongside coffee
- Cafe meal prices in Singapore range from approximately SGD 14–22 for a brunch main with coffee, rising to SGD 25–45 at destination cafes in premium locations
- Weekend queues at Good Bites and My Awesome Cafe regularly exceed 30 minutes: arriving before 10am or after 2pm on Saturdays and Sundays reduces wait times significantly

## How We Ranked These Cafes

Our methodology for ranking the best cafes in Singapore 2026 applied the following criteria. Google review rating: a minimum of 4.6 stars was required for inclusion in the top ten, with higher ratings rewarded in the final ordering. Review volume: cafes with fewer than 200 Google reviews were excluded to ensure statistical significance; higher review counts at equivalent ratings were treated as a stronger quality signal reflecting sustained performance over time rather than a short-run positive impression. Food quality: the cafe's food programme was assessed based on the menu scope, the quality signals in review text, and the consistency of positive food commentary across reviews. Coffee programme: the quality of the espresso-based drinks and any specialty coffee offering was assessed where relevant to the cafe's positioning. Ambiance and format: the suitability of the physical space for the cafe's intended use cases, including brunch dining, remote work, group visits, and social occasions. Value: meal pricing relative to the quality of the food and coffee delivered, with particular attention to portion sizes and the overall dining experience at the price point. All data reflects conditions as of August 2026.

## What to Look for When Choosing a Cafe in Singapore

Singapore's cafe market is large enough that the difference between a mediocre visit and an excellent one frequently comes down to matching the right cafe to your specific priorities rather than simply choosing the highest-rated option. Consider the following factors before deciding.

**Location relative to your plans.** Singapore's cafes are distributed across the island with distinct clusters: Telok Ayer and the CBD area for after-work and weekday lunch cafe visits; Tiong Bahru and Potong Pasir for neighbourhood community cafes; Orchard Road and its surrounding streets for mall and hotel-adjacent cafes; the Botanic Gardens and Holland Village area for garden and residential neighbourhood cafes; and Little India and Serangoon Road for specialty coffee and independent cafe culture. Matching your cafe choice to your area saves travel time and allows you to explore the neighbourhood around the cafe.

**Queue tolerance and timing.** Singapore's most popular cafes operate with significant weekend queues: Good Bites, My Awesome Cafe, and Brunches Cafe all see queues exceeding 30 minutes on Saturday and Sunday mornings between 9am and 12pm. Arriving before 9am or after 2pm on weekends eliminates most queue wait at these venues. Weekday visits at popular brunch cafes are significantly more comfortable than weekend visits and may allow you to experience the same quality without the crowd.

**Coffee quality versus food focus.** Not all cafes on this list prioritise coffee equally. Asylum Coffeehouse and Wildseed are the most coffee-centric options with genuine specialty programmes. Good Bites, QQ Cats & Cafe, and Brunches Cafe are food-primary cafes where the coffee is competent but not the primary draw. FYP Cafe and Avenue On 3 Café are balanced cafe and dessert destinations. Matching your priority to the cafe's strength produces a better visit.

**Format and occasion suitability.** QQ Cats & Cafe suits cat-lover groups and interactive social occasions. Rise Bakehouse suits quick morning pastry-and-coffee visits. Kafe Utu suits curious diners seeking a unique concept experience. Wildseed Cafe suits leisure morning visits combined with a Botanic Gardens walk. Aligning the cafe's format to your occasion avoids the disappointment of taking a remote-work session to a high-turnover brunch cafe or trying to have a leisurely long lunch at a queue-driven fast-turnaround format.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best cafe in Singapore?

### How much does it cost to eat at a cafe in Singapore?

Cafe meal prices in Singapore range from approximately SGD 12–18 for a pastry, coffee, and light bite at a neighbourhood bakehouse like Rise Bakehouse, to SGD 18–28 for a full brunch plate with coffee at mid-range cafes like My Awesome Cafe and Brunches Cafe. Premium cafe settings like Avenue On 3 Café at The Paragon and destination cafes with table service typically charge SGD 22–35 per person for a brunch meal with coffee. Cat cafe entry fees at QQ Cats & Cafe typically include a minimum spend on food and drinks. Singapore's 9% GST and 10% service charge apply at most sit-down cafes and will add approximately 19% to the listed menu price.

### Where is the best brunch in Singapore?

The best brunch in Singapore based on Google review scores and volume is Good Bites in Bishan (4.9 stars, 22,297 reviews) for value-focused Western brunch, Brunches Cafe on Rangoon Road (4.7 stars, 3,100 reviews) for dedicated eggs-and-pancakes brunch execution, and My Awesome Cafe at Telok Ayer (4.7 stars, 7,903 reviews) for an all-day brunch format with a broader menu that suits groups with diverse preferences. ### How do I find specialty coffee cafes in Singapore?

Singapore's specialty coffee scene is concentrated in several distinct clusters: Little India and Serangoon Road (Asylum Coffeehouse), the CBD and Tanjong Pagar area (Kafe Utu, Common Man Coffee Roasters), Tiong Bahru (multiple independent roasters), and the East Coast and Katong area. Looking for cafes that describe themselves as specialty coffee or coffee roasters, and checking that they list single-origin beans and offer filter coffee options alongside espresso, identifies the serious specialty operations from the broader cafe market. The cafes with the highest Google ratings for coffee specifically, as opposed to overall cafe experience, tend to be the specialty-focused operations with trained baristas rather than all-day dining cafes that include coffee as one of many menu items.

### Do Singapore cafes require reservations?

Most Singapore cafes do not require advance reservations except for afternoon tea services and large groups. Good Bites, Brunches Cafe, and My Awesome Cafe operate on a walk-in basis with queues managed at the door on weekends. Wildseed Cafe At The Garage and Avenue On 3 Café accept walk-ins but may have waiting times on weekend mornings. Afternoon tea at Wildseed typically benefits from advance booking, particularly on weekends and public holidays. For any cafe visit during peak hours on Saturday and Sunday between 9am and 12pm, arriving early or having a backup option reduces the risk of a long wait.

### What time do cafes open in Singapore?

Most Singapore cafes open between 8am and 10am, with the majority of weekend brunch cafes opening at 9am or 10am. Specialty coffee cafes and bakehouse operations like Rise Bakehouse and Asylum Coffeehouse typically open from 8am or 9am to capture the morning coffee and pastry trade before the midday brunch rush. Good Bites and Brunches Cafe open from approximately 9am–10am on weekends and attract the longest queues between 10am and 12pm. Wildseed Cafe At The Garage opens from approximately 8am reflecting the Botanic Gardens' early morning visitor pattern. Closing times vary: most cafes on this list operate until 5pm–6pm, with My Awesome Cafe extending into evening dining and bar service until later.

### Are Singapore cafes child-friendly?

Most Singapore cafes on this list are suitable for families with children, though the format and noise tolerance vary. Good Bites in Bishan and Rise Bakehouse at Potong Pasir operate in family-friendly neighbourhood settings where children are common. Wildseed Cafe At The Garage in the Botanic Gardens is particularly well-suited to families as the outdoor Botanic Gardens space adjacent to the cafe provides activity for children before and after the meal. QQ Cats & Cafe in Balestier is suitable for older children who can interact gently with cats but requires that young children be supervised to ensure the welfare of the resident cats. High chairs are available at most of the larger cafes on this list: calling ahead to confirm availability is advisable for families with infants.
