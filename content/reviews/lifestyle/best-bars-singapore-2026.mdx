The best bars in Singapore sit within one of the most competitive and creative drinking scenes in Southeast Asia. Singapore's cocktail culture has matured significantly over the past decade, producing bars that regularly appear on Asia's and the World's 50 Best Bars lists, drawing serious drinkers from across the region specifically for the quality of the craft on offer. Whether you are looking for a storied whisky specialist, a boundary-pushing cocktail programme, a rooftop bar with a view of the skyline, or a neighbourhood haunt with genuine character, Singapore's bar scene delivers at every price point and ambiance. This guide covers the ten best bars in Singapore for 2026, ranked by Google review ratings, review volume, speciality, and the overall drinking experience they consistently deliver. Each bar on this list has been evaluated against a transparent set of criteria covering cocktail quality, bartender expertise, ambiance, value, and the consistency of the experience across visits.

Singapore's bar landscape is concentrated in a handful of distinct drinking districts: Tanjong Pagar and the Duxton Hill area for cocktail bars and intimate speakeasies, Boat Quay and the CBD waterfront for rooftop and view bars, Orchard Road for hotel bars and high-design lounges, and Kampong Glam and Haji Lane for creative, neighbourhood-driven bar culture. Understanding which district suits your preference is the first step in navigating a bar scene that punches well above Singapore's size on the global stage.

**Key Takeaways**

- **Best overall:** Quaich Bar Wanderlust: a whisky specialist with a 4.9-star Google rating and 586 reviews, the highest-rated bar in Singapore by Google score
- **Best cocktail bar:** Jigger and Pony: the most-reviewed cocktail bar in Singapore with 1,814 Google reviews and a sustained track record on Asia's 50 Best Bars list
- **Best for atmosphere:** ATLAS: housed in a stunning Art Deco building at Parkview Square with over 4,700 Google reviews, the most-reviewed bar on this list
- **Best hotel bar:** Manhattan at Conrad Singapore Orchard: a celebrated American bar programme with 1,100+ reviews and a reputation for technically precise cocktails
- **Best for Asian spirits:** Native at Amoy Street: a sustainability-focused bar using foraged and locally sourced Asian ingredients in its cocktail programme
- **Best rooftop bar:** Republic Bar at Marina Bay: waterfront views of Marina Bay Sands and the Singapore skyline, 451 Google reviews averaging 4.7 stars
- Cocktail prices in Singapore range from approximately SGD 22–38 per drink at independent bars, rising to SGD 28–50 at hotel and destination bars
- Reservations are strongly recommended at Jigger and Pony, Manhattan, Native, and ATLAS on weekends: walk-ins at peak hours face long waits at these venues

## How We Ranked These Bars

Our methodology for ranking the best bars in Singapore 2026 applied the following criteria in order of weight. Google review rating: a minimum of 4.3 stars was required for inclusion, with higher ratings rewarded in the final ranking. Review volume: bars with fewer than 50 Google reviews were excluded to ensure statistical significance; higher review counts at strong ratings were weighted as a measure of sustained quality over time rather than a short-run positive impression. Drinks quality: the cocktail programme, spirits selection, and bartender technique were assessed based on available documentation, bar awards, and guest review patterns. Ambiance and concept: the distinctiveness, coherence, and execution of the bar's concept and physical environment. Value: cocktail pricing relative to the quality of the drinking experience delivered. Consistency: evidence from review patterns that the quality of the experience is reliable across visits and bartenders, rather than contingent on a single star employee. Award recognition: inclusion on Asia's 50 Best Bars or World's 50 Best Bars lists was noted as an additional credibility signal but did not override the Google review-based primary ranking. All data reflects conditions as of August 2026.

### 1. Quaich Bar Wanderlust, Best Overall

<img src="/images/places/best-bars-singapore/quaich-bar-wanderlust.jpg" alt="Quaich Bar Wanderlust" className="w-full rounded-lg my-4 object-cover" style={{height: "300px"}} />

<CompanyRating name="Quaich Bar Wanderlust" rating={4.9} address="80 Middle Rd, Lobby Level Frasers House" postalCode="188966" phone="+65 9818 0885" />

Quaich Bar Wanderlust holds a 4.9-star Google rating across 586 reviews, making it the highest-rated bar in Singapore by any meaningful statistical measure. Located at the lobby level of Frasers House on Middle Road, adjacent to the Wanderlust Hotel, it operates as an accessible neighbourhood whisky bar with the depth of programme and quality of hospitality that places it among the finest whisky-focused drinking establishments in Southeast Asia. The bar's name references the Scottish quaich, a shallow two-handled drinking cup traditionally used for whisky, and the Wanderlust suffix signals the global curiosity that defines both the spirits selection and the bartender's approach to whisky education.

The whisky programme at Quaich Bar Wanderlust covers single malts from every major Scottish distilling region: Highlands, Speyside, Islay, Campbeltown, and Islands: alongside Japanese expressions from Nikka, Yamazaki, and independent bottlings, Irish whiskeys, American bourbons and ryes, and Taiwanese single malts from Kavalan. The depth of the Scotch selection in particular distinguishes Quaich from the broader cocktail bar circuit: bartenders are knowledgeable enough to guide guests through a tasting progression suited to their flavour preferences rather than defaulting to the familiar entry-level expressions. Cocktails using whisky as a base are crafted with the same care, and the bar's willingness to engage guests in genuine conversation about the spirits on offer creates an experience that rewards return visits.

The compact, intimate room format and the quality of service consistency evident in 586 reviews averaging 4.9 stars together produce what is, by the data, the most reliably excellent bar experience currently available in Singapore.

## What to Look for in a Singapore Bar

Choosing the right bar in Singapore depends on understanding what kind of experience you are optimising for. Singapore's bar scene is large, competitive, and varies significantly across format, price point, and neighbourhood character.

**Cocktail programme depth.** The strongest Singapore bars operate distinct, original cocktail programmes that cannot be replicated elsewhere. Bars like Jigger and Pony, Native, and Nutmeg and Clove invest in bartenders who are genuinely creative rather than producing standard pours from a purchased recipe list. When evaluating a bar for a first visit, checking whether the cocktail menu is house-developed or generic is a reliable quality indicator.

**Bartender engagement.** Singapore's best bars distinguish themselves through bartenders who engage guests as collaborators in the drinking experience: asking about flavour preferences, explaining the provenance of spirits, and occasionally crafting bespoke drinks for guests who signal interest. This level of hospitality is not universal: at high-volume venues and rooftop bars prioritising throughput, bartender engagement is necessarily more transactional. Know which experience you are seeking before choosing.

**Reservations policy.** Several of Singapore's top bars require or strongly recommend advance reservations: Jigger and Pony, Manhattan, and Native in particular have limited seating and fill quickly on Thursday through Saturday evenings. Arriving without a reservation at these venues during peak hours is likely to result in a significant wait or no entry. Build the reservation step into your bar planning for any serious drinking evening.

**Price expectations.** Singapore bar prices reflect the city-state's high operating costs, the quality of the spirits programmes at serious bars, and the value of the bartending talent that the best venues maintain. Expect to pay SGD 22–30 for cocktails at independent neighbourhood bars, SGD 28–38 at destination cocktail bars, and SGD 35–50 at hotel bars and waterfront venues with premium location costs. Minimum spend or cover charge policies apply at some venues: confirm in advance for special occasions or large groups.

**Location and neighbourhood character.** Tanjong Pagar and Duxton Hill concentrate the highest density of quality cocktail bars in Singapore, making them the optimal base for a bar-focused evening. The CBD and Marina Bay area offer waterfront views and high-end hotel bars. Orchard Road is convenient for hotel guests and provides polished hotel bar experiences. Amoy Street, Keong Saik, and the Ann Siang Hill area form a cluster of intimate, character-rich bars in Singapore's conserved shophouse belt.

### How much do cocktails cost at Singapore bars?

Cocktail prices at Singapore bars range from approximately SGD 22–30 per drink at independent neighbourhood cocktail bars like Nutmeg and Clove, Tess Bar, and 28 HongKong Street. Destination cocktail bars and hotel bars including Jigger and Pony, Native, and Manhattan typically charge SGD 28–38 per cocktail. Waterfront and rooftop venues like Republic Bar and hotel bars with premium views charge SGD 35–50 or above. Beer at most Singapore bars ranges from SGD 12–18 per pint. Singapore's Goods and Services Tax (GST) of 9% and service charge of 10% apply on top of listed prices at most licensed establishments: the final bill will be approximately 19% above the menu price.

### What time do bars close in Singapore?

Most Singapore bars operate until 1am–2am on weeknights (Monday to Thursday) and until 3am–4am on weekends (Friday and Saturday). Some licensed establishments hold extended hour licences that permit operation until later. Last orders are typically called 30–45 minutes before closing time. Singapore's Public Entertainment licence framework regulates bar closing hours, and enforcement is consistent. Bars near residential areas in conserved shophouse districts: Duxton Hill, Keong Saik, Amoy Street: may have earlier closing times due to noise management conditions attached to their licences. Checking the specific bar's operating hours before a late-night visit is advisable as hours vary by venue and day of the week.

### What drinks are Singapore bars known for?

Singapore's bar scene is internationally known for cocktail innovation: the quality of the city-state's bartending talent and the creative ambition of bars like Native, Jigger and Pony, and 28 HongKong Street have made Singapore cocktails a benchmark for the Asia-Pacific bar world. The Singapore Sling, invented at the Long Bar at Raffles Hotel in the early 20th century, is Singapore's most iconic cocktail: a blend of gin, Cherry Heering, cointreau, pineapple juice, lime juice, Benedictine, grenadine, and Angostura bitters. However, Singapore's contemporary bar scene has long moved beyond the Singapore Sling, producing original cocktail programmes based on Asian spirits, foraged local ingredients, and cross-cultural flavour references that reflect the city-state's multicultural identity.
