Finding the best burger in Singapore means navigating dozens of spots across the island, from plant-based smash burgers in Jalan Eunos to gourmet wood-fired patties at Dempsey Hill. Singapore's burger scene has matured significantly over the past decade, producing independent joints and chef-driven concepts that rival the best American-style burger bars in any major city. We evaluated 20 burger restaurants across Singapore, assessing Google ratings, review volume, menu differentiation, patty quality, and overall value. This list covers the top ten burger restaurants in Singapore for 2026, ranked by overall quality and customer satisfaction as reflected in verified Google reviews.

**Key Takeaways**

- **Best overall:** VEGANBURG Jalan Eunos, a 4.6-star rated plant-based burger restaurant at 44 Jalan Eunos with 5,005 Google reviews, the most reviewed burger spot in Singapore and a globally recognised name in vegan fast food
- **Best smash burger:** One Fattened Calf Burgers at Fusionopolis, 4.6 stars across 1,369 Google reviews, delivering consistently crispy-edged smash patties from their one-north location
- **Most atmospheric:** Burgs Singapore on Bussorah Street in Kampong Glam, 4.6 stars and 559 reviews, set in a heritage shophouse neighbourhood that makes it one of the most characterful burger spots on the island

- Avoid burger restaurants that lack a verifiable physical address or show a cluster of five-star reviews posted within a short time window, both are common signals of manipulated ratings in Singapore's F&B market

## How We Ranked These Burger Restaurants

We ranked each burger restaurant in Singapore using the following criteria. Google Maps rating: a minimum of 4.2 stars was required for inclusion, with restaurants rated 4.6 stars and above prioritised. Review volume: restaurants with fewer than 30 Google reviews were excluded to ensure statistical reliability. Menu quality: the distinctiveness of the burger offering, the quality of patty sourcing and preparation technique (smash, wagyu, plant-based, wood-fired), and the consistency of positive commentary in reviews. Value: meal pricing relative to portion size and the overall dining experience. Location accessibility and dine-in suitability were assessed as secondary factors. Businesses with review patterns suggesting manipulation were excluded regardless of headline rating. All data reflects conditions as of August 2026, sourced from Google Maps and verified against restaurant websites and social media.

## What to Look for in a Great Burger Restaurant

**Patty sourcing and preparation technique.** The quality of a burger starts with the beef blend and how the patty is cooked. Smash burgers use a thinner patty pressed hard against a flat-top to create a Maillard crust; classic burgers use a thicker, loosely packed patty cooked to medium. Neither is superior; they are different eating experiences. Know which you prefer before choosing a restaurant, and look for menus that specify the beef blend or preparation method rather than generic descriptions.

**Google rating and review volume together.** A 4.8-star rating from 12 reviews means far less than a 4.5-star rating from 600 reviews. Look for restaurants with at least 100 Google reviews at 4.4 stars or above. A review count above 500 at 4.4 stars or above is a strong signal of genuine and sustained quality. BestThingReview.com applied this threshold to every restaurant on this list.

**Bun quality.** The bun is the most underrated element of a great burger. It needs structural integrity to hold the patty and toppings, enough softness to eat comfortably, and enough flavour not to be neutral filler. Brioche buns are the standard at quality Singapore burger restaurants; potato buns are a softer, slightly sweeter alternative worth seeking out. If a restaurant uses commercial supermarket-style buns, the overall experience will suffer regardless of patty quality.

**Value relative to portion.** Expect to pay SGD 15–25 for a smash burger meal with fries and a drink at a quality standalone restaurant in Singapore. Chef-driven or fine-dining adjacent options like Burnt Ends sit at SGD 28–45 or above for the burger alone. Anything priced above SGD 30 at a casual counter-service restaurant without a clear justification in sourcing or technique represents poor value by Singapore market standards.

**Consistency across visits.** The restaurants on this list earn their ratings through consistency, not one-off performance. Before committing to a restaurant for a group meal or a special occasion, check whether recent reviews (within the past three months) reflect the same quality as older reviews. A sharp drop in recent ratings often signals a change in ownership, kitchen staff, or sourcing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a burger cost in Singapore?

A standard smash burger with fries at a quality standalone restaurant costs SGD 15–25. ### What is a smash burger and why is it popular in Singapore?

A smash burger is made by placing a loose ball of ground beef on a very hot flat-top griddle and pressing it flat with a spatula, which maximises the contact surface area and creates a highly caramelised Maillard crust. The result is a thinner patty with crispy, lacy edges and a juicy interior. Smash burgers have dominated Singapore's burger scene since around 2020 because the technique consistently produces intense beef flavour in a format that suits the tropical climate.
