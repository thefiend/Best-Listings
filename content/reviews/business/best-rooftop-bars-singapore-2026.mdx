## What to Look for When Choosing a Rooftop Bar in Singapore

**View orientation and elevation.** Not all rooftop views in Singapore are equal. Marina Bay-facing venues like VUE, HighHouse, and CÉ LA VI offer the iconic city skyline. River-facing venues like Southbridge offer quieter, more intimate settings. Sunset-oriented terraces like Artemis Grill benefit from west-facing aspects. Choose based on what you are trying to experience, not just the height.

**Weather resilience.** Singapore's rain arrives without warning and can end a rooftop evening instantly. Venues with covered or semi-covered terraces, such as VUE and Artemis Grill, are significantly more reliable for planned events. Fully open terraces like CÉ LA VI's pool deck are spectacular in good weather but risky for weddings or important dinners.

**Reservation policy.** Singapore's top rooftop bars are reservation-only on weekends, and Friday and Saturday evening slots at VUE, HighHouse, and LAVO frequently fill weeks in advance. Weekday evenings at most venues allow same-day bookings. Plan accordingly and book early for special occasions.

**Drinks versus dining focus.** Some rooftop venues are primarily bars where food is secondary (Southbridge, LeVeL33). Others are full restaurants with rooftop terraces where the menu is as important as the view (VUE, Artemis Grill, Verandah). Choose based on whether you want a full dining experience or drinks and light bites.

**Price transparency.** Most Singapore rooftop bars do not impose a cover charge on weeknights, but weekend and public holiday covers of SGD 25 to SGD 50 are common at Marina Bay Sands venues. Confirm the cover policy when booking, and check whether the amount is redeemable against drinks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best rooftop bar in Singapore?

VUE at OUE Bayfront on Collyer Quay is the best-rated rooftop bar in Singapore, with 4.7 stars from 1,714 Google reviews. For the most iconic view, CÉ LA VI at Marina Bay Sands has the most reviews (10,132) and the most recognisable setting. For sunset cocktails, Artemis Grill at CapitaGreen is the top recommendation.

### Do Singapore rooftop bars require reservations?

Yes, most top rooftop bars require reservations on weekends. VUE, HighHouse, and LAVO at Marina Bay Sands are reservation-only on Friday and Saturday evenings. Southbridge and LeVeL33 have more walk-in flexibility on weeknights. Book at least one week ahead for weekend evening sessions.

### How much do drinks cost at Singapore rooftop bars?

Cocktails at premium rooftop bars range from SGD 22 to SGD 38. House pours start from SGD 15 to SGD 20. Some venues, particularly at Marina Bay Sands, impose a minimum spend of SGD 20 to SGD 40 per person during peak hours. LeVeL33's house-brewed beers start from SGD 12 to SGD 14 per pint, making it the best-value option.

### Is there a dress code at Singapore rooftop bars?

Smart casual is the standard across all venues on this list. Flip-flops, shorts, and singlets are generally not accepted at venues like VUE, HighHouse, and CÉ LA VI, particularly in the evening. Artemis Grill and LAVO have stricter dress codes due to their fine dining positioning. Check individual venue websites when planning formal occasions.

### What is the best rooftop bar in Singapore for a date?

VUE and HighHouse are the top picks for a date, combining views, cocktails, and an intimate atmosphere. Southbridge is an excellent lower-key alternative with a riverside setting that feels more personal than the Marina Bay circuit. Book a window or terrace seat specifically when making the reservation.

### Which rooftop bars in Singapore are best for groups?

LAVO at Marina Bay Sands and LeVeL33 both accommodate larger groups more comfortably than the boutique sky bars. LAVO offers private dining rooms for groups of 10 to 30. LeVeL33 has flexible table configurations and a corporate events team. CÉ LA VI also handles large group bookings but fills quickly during peak periods.

### What is the best time to visit a rooftop bar in Singapore?

The golden hour before sunset, approximately 6:30pm to 7:30pm, is the best time to visit for views. The city lights become dramatic from 8pm onward. Weekday evenings from Monday to Thursday offer the best combination of quality service and availability without the weekend premium pricing or crowd levels.

### Are there rooftop bars in Singapore without a reservation?

Southbridge at Boat Quay accepts walk-ins on weeknight evenings reliably. LeVeL33 also has walk-in capacity on weeknights. For weekends, walk-ins at top venues are unlikely to secure seats during prime hours. Arriving at opening time (usually 5:30pm or 6pm) on a weekend evening may result in walk-in availability before the reservation rush fills the venue.

## Verdict

VUE at OUE Bayfront is the best rooftop bar in Singapore for most occasions, combining the highest review scores in its category with a panoramic view position, strong cocktails, and the rare advantage of equally strong food from the restaurant below. For the most iconic Singapore experience, CÉ LA VI at Marina Bay Sands remains unmatched in recognition and review volume. HighHouse is the strongest choice for a cocktail-focused evening without a dining component. If budget is a consideration, Verandah Rooftop Rotisserie on Alexandra Road delivers comparable ambiance and a 4.7-star rating at meaningfully lower prices than the CBD venues. Book your preferred venue one to two weeks ahead for weekends, and arrive at sunset for the most memorable version of Singapore's skyline.
