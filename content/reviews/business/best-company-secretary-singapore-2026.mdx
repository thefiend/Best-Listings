## What to Look for When Hiring a Company Secretary Service in Singapore

**Residency requirement compliance.** Singapore law requires the company secretary to be a natural person ordinarily resident in Singapore: a foreign resident company secretary is not permitted. Verify that the individual named as your company secretary satisfies this requirement, not just the corporate secretarial firm. This is a common source of inadvertent non-compliance for companies incorporating remotely.

**ACRA filing responsiveness.** The most important operational metric for a company secretary is their speed and reliability in processing ACRA filings. Late annual returns, delayed director change notifications, and missed filing deadlines result in financial penalties that are directly attributable to poor secretarial management. Ask providers for their average turnaround time on standard ACRA filings and whether they proactively alert you before deadlines or wait to be instructed.

**Service bundling vs specialisation.** Firms that offer company secretarial services bundled with accounting and payroll can simplify your administrative vendor management and may offer coordinated pricing. However, ensure that the secretarial component of a bundled service maintains quality standards independently: some bundled providers prioritise the accounting revenue and treat secretarial compliance as a low-margin add-on that receives inadequate attention.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a company secretary do in Singapore?

A company secretary in Singapore is responsible for ensuring the company's compliance with the Companies Act and ACRA requirements. Core responsibilities include maintaining the statutory registers (shareholders, directors, charges), preparing and filing annual returns, drafting board and shareholder resolutions, notifying ACRA of changes to directors, shareholders, and registered address, managing the Annual General Meeting (for companies required to hold one), and keeping the company's constitutional documents current. The company secretary is the company's primary point of contact with ACRA.

### Is a company secretary mandatory for Singapore companies?

Yes. Under Section 171 of the Companies Act, every Singapore company is required to appoint at least one company secretary. The company secretary must be a natural person ordinarily resident in Singapore. If there is only one director, that director cannot simultaneously act as company secretary. The appointment must be made within 6 months of the company's incorporation.

### How much does a company secretary cost in Singapore?

Annual company secretarial fees for standard private limited companies typically range from SGD 300–1,200 per year, depending on the level of service, complexity of the company structure, and number of ACRA filings required. Company incorporation (not including government fees) typically costs SGD 300–1,500. ACRA's government incorporation fee is SGD 300. Additional fees apply for non-standard filings such as share allotments, capital reductions, and name changes.

### What is ACRA and why does it matter for my company?

ACRA stands for the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority: Singapore's national regulator of business entities, public accountants, and corporate service providers. ACRA administers the Companies Act, maintains the public register of Singapore companies (BizFile+), and enforces compliance with filing requirements. Companies and directors that fail to comply with ACRA requirements face financial penalties and potential disqualification from acting as directors. A competent company secretary ensures your company remains compliant with all ACRA obligations.

### How do I incorporate a company in Singapore?

Company incorporation in Singapore is managed through ACRA's BizFile+ portal. You will need to provide the proposed company name (subject to ACRA approval), company constitution, details of shareholders and directors, registered Singapore address, and share structure. ACRA typically approves straightforward incorporations within 1–3 working days. A company secretary service can handle the complete incorporation process on your behalf, including name reservation, document preparation, and BizFile+ submission.

### What is an annual return filing in Singapore?

Every Singapore private limited company must file an annual return with ACRA each year. The annual return contains updated company information including current directors, shareholders, share capital, and registered address, along with the company's financial statements or a certificate of solvency (for small companies exempt from audit). The annual return must be filed within one month of the company's financial year-end. Late filing attracts financial penalties starting from SGD 300.

### What happens if I miss the AGM or annual return deadline in Singapore?

Missing the annual return filing deadline results in late filing penalties from ACRA, starting at SGD 300 and increasing with the length of delay. Persistent non-compliance can result in ACRA striking off the company from the register, which would create significant complications for ongoing business operations. Directors who are found responsible for persistent non-compliance may be disqualified from acting as directors of any Singapore company for up to five years.

### Can a foreigner be a company secretary in Singapore?

No. The Companies Act requires the company secretary to be a natural person ordinarily resident in Singapore. A foreigner who is not ordinarily resident in Singapore: including an Employment Pass or S Pass holder who does not have permanent residence: cannot act as company secretary. All the company secretary firms on this list provide a professional resident company secretary as part of their service package.

### What is the difference between a company secretary and an accounting firm in Singapore?

A company secretary is responsible for corporate governance and statutory compliance: maintaining registers, filing with ACRA, managing resolutions and constitutional documents, and ensuring Companies Act compliance. An accounting firm handles financial recording, statutory audit, tax filing, and financial reporting. These are distinct functions with different regulatory requirements. Some firms on this list provide both, but the statutory obligations require that each function is competently and independently performed.

### When should I change my company secretary in Singapore?

Consider changing your company secretary if they repeatedly miss ACRA filing deadlines, fail to notify you of upcoming compliance obligations in advance, are unresponsive to queries about your company's corporate status, make errors in statutory documents that require ACRA corrections, or are unable to provide clear written confirmation of fees for non-standard work. Before changing providers, ensure you obtain all original statutory registers, corporate documents, and ACRA filing history from the departing secretary.
