Every company incorporated in Singapore is legally required to appoint a qualified company secretary within six months of incorporation, and to maintain that appointment on an ongoing basis. The company secretary is responsible for ensuring the company meets its statutory obligations under the Companies Act — ACRA annual return filings, minutes and resolutions, maintenance of statutory registers, director and shareholder changes, and AGM administration. Choosing the right company secretary service matters: a competent firm keeps you compliant and flags upcoming obligations before they become late filings; an incompetent or unresponsive firm creates regulatory exposure that directors are personally responsible for. We evaluated Singapore's leading company secretarial services firms by assessing Google ratings, review volume, service scope, pricing transparency, responsiveness, ACRA accreditation, and the range of value-added services offered alongside core secretarial work.

- **Best overall:** HeySara, 4.8 stars across 262 Google reviews, tech-enabled company incorporation and ongoing secretarial services with a transparent fee structure and strong platform experience
- **Best for startup incorporation:** Profectant Corporate Services, 5.0 stars across 131 reviews, highly rated for fast, accurate company registration at competitive pricing

- Company secretarial fees in Singapore typically range from SGD 300 to SGD 800 per year for a standard private limited company with straightforward filing needs
- For companies with complex structures, multiple directors, frequent resolutions, or significant changes during the year, all-in annual fees can range from SGD 800 to SGD 2,000
- All persons acting as company secretary must be a natural person resident in Singapore — a corporate body cannot serve as company secretary for a Singapore-incorporated company

## What to Look for When Hiring a Company Secretary Service

**ACRA knowledge and filing accuracy.** The fundamental requirement of a company secretary is accurate, timely ACRA filings. A single missed annual return or incorrectly filed director change can result in late penalties and ACRA enforcement exposure that directors are personally liable for. Ask prospective providers about their filing accuracy record and late-filing rate, and look for review patterns that confirm proactive deadline management.

**Proactive communication.** The best company secretarial firms don't wait for clients to chase them — they send reminders before deadlines, flag upcoming obligation dates, and keep clients informed of regulatory changes that affect their company. This is the quality that most clearly differentiates an excellent secretarial firm from an adequate one, and it is well-evidenced in Google review content.

**Range of services and scalability.** As a company grows, its secretarial and compliance requirements become more complex: share issuances, director changes, capital restructuring, employee stock option plans, and subsidiary incorporation all require professional secretarial work beyond the annual return. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a company secretary in Singapore?

A company secretary in Singapore is a qualified person appointed by the company's directors to ensure compliance with the Companies Act. The company secretary is responsible for ACRA filings, maintaining statutory registers (directors, shareholders, charges), preparing board and shareholder resolutions, administering AGMs, and advising directors on their statutory obligations. Under the Companies Act, a company secretary must be a natural person ordinarily resident in Singapore.

### Is it mandatory to appoint a company secretary in Singapore?

Yes. Every company incorporated in Singapore must appoint a company secretary within six months of incorporation and must maintain that appointment at all times. The sole director cannot also serve as the company secretary. Failure to appoint or maintain a company secretary is a criminal offence under the Companies Act.

### What qualifications must a company secretary have in Singapore?

For public companies, the company secretary must hold a specific professional qualification (such as a membership of the Chartered Secretaries Institute of Singapore, a law degree, or an accounting qualification). For private companies, the requirement is less prescriptive — the company secretary must be a natural person ordinarily resident in Singapore who is capable of performing the required duties. Most private companies use professional secretarial service firms that employ qualified practitioners.

### How much does company secretarial services cost in Singapore?

Annual fees for standard company secretarial services for a private limited company range from SGD 300 to SGD 800 per year, covering ACRA annual return filing, maintenance of statutory registers, and standard resolutions. Companies with more complex requirements — multiple director changes, share issuances, or frequent resolutions — can expect annual fees of SGD 800 to SGD 2,000. Company incorporation services (as a one-time fee) typically cost SGD 300 to SGD 1,000 depending on the firm and the complexity of the initial structure.

### What does a company secretary do in Singapore?

Core responsibilities include: filing the annual return with ACRA on time, maintaining the statutory registers of directors and shareholders, preparing and circulating notices and minutes of board and shareholder meetings, lodging changes to directors, shareholders, and company particulars with ACRA within the statutory timeframe, advising directors on their statutory duties, and maintaining the company's registered address. Many secretarial firms also provide complementary accounting and tax services.

### Can I use a virtual office address as my company's registered address?

Yes. A virtual office address can be used as a Singapore company's registered address, provided the address is a physical Singapore address capable of receiving ACRA correspondence. Most professional secretarial firms include a registered address service in their retainer packages, or offer it as an add-on. You must be able to receive official government correspondence at the registered address, so ensure the virtual office provider manages mail forwarding or notification reliably.

### What is the ACRA annual return deadline in Singapore?

Private limited companies must file their annual return with ACRA within five months of their financial year end (for companies with a share capital). The annual return includes confirmation of the company's financial statements and updated particulars of directors and shareholders. Late filing attracts penalties of SGD 300 for companies with revenue below SGD 5 million and SGD 600 for companies above that threshold. ACRA may also take enforcement action against directors of companies with persistent non-compliance.

### What happens if my company misses an ACRA filing deadline?

Missing an ACRA filing deadline results in a late filing penalty, currently SGD 300 to SGD 600 per filing depending on company size. Persistent failure to file can result in ACRA issuing a notice of default and, in severe cases, initiating action to strike off the company or prosecute directors. Late filings also appear on the company's ACRA profile, which can raise concerns for banks, investors, and counterparties doing due diligence. A proactive secretarial firm will ensure this never happens.
