## What to Look for When Choosing a Halal Restaurant in Singapore

**MUIS halal certification.** In Singapore, the Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura (MUIS) administers halal certification for food establishments. A current MUIS halal certificate is the definitive confirmation of halal status: it confirms that ingredients, preparation methods, and kitchen practices all comply with halal requirements. Certificates are renewed annually, so confirm the current certification status directly with the restaurant rather than relying on an outdated listing.
