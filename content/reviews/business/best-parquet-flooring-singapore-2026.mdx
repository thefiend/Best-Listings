The best parquet flooring contractors in Singapore combine three skills that rarely come together: sourcing the right timber for a humid tropical climate, laying it on a properly prepared subfloor, and finishing it so it survives years of foot traffic. This guide is for HDB, condo, and landed homeowners who want real timber underfoot, whether that means a new herringbone floor, an engineered oak upgrade, or reviving 20-year-old teak parquet that has gone dull. We wrote it because parquet quotes in Singapore vary enormously, and many "parquet" listings turn out to be vinyl specialists selling wood-look products. BestThingReview.com reviewed 49 flooring businesses from Google Maps, removed vinyl-only, epoxy, and unrelated listings, and ranked the remaining timber specialists on verified Google ratings, review volume, depth of parquet services, and pricing transparency. The ten businesses below cover new installation, engineered and solid timber supply, sanding and varnishing, and repair.

**Key Takeaways**

- **Best overall:** SinHao E&C PTE. LTD.: 5.0★ across 1,299 Google reviews, covering parquet supply, installation, hacking of old floors, sanding, varnishing, and repairs under one contractor

## How We Ranked These Parquet Flooring Contractors

We started with 49 Google Maps listings returned for parquet flooring in Singapore and applied four filters. First, relevance: every business had to offer genuine timber parquet work, meaning solid or engineered wood supply and installation, or parquet sanding, varnishing, and repair. Vinyl-only suppliers, epoxy overlay specialists, general handymen, laundries, and furniture makers were excluded even where their ratings were high. Second, credibility: a minimum Google rating of 4.7 stars and at least 90 verified reviews, so every pick has a meaningful sample of customer feedback. Third, transparency: extra weight went to businesses whose websites clearly state their timber species, product lines, and services, and to those that publish indicative pricing. Fourth, scope: contractors covering the full job, from removing old flooring to final varnish, ranked above single-step providers with similar ratings. Within those filters, businesses were ordered by rating and review volume together, with each score calculated as Google rating multiplied by two. SinHao E&C PTE. LTD. holds the top spot as our featured contractor. If you need vinyl, SPC, or tiles rather than real timber, see our separate guide to the <a href="/business/best-flooring-contractors-singapore">best flooring contractors in Singapore</a>.

## What to Look for When Hiring a Parquet Flooring Contractor in Singapore

**Match the specialist to the job.** Supplier-installers such as Floorrich and floorxpert are strongest on new engineered floors, while DW Floor Polishing, Mr Wood Varnish, and MCL Flooring focus on reviving and fixing existing parquet. SinHao covers both.

**Insist on a specified quote.** It should name the species, block or board size, wear layer, pattern, adhesive or click system, finish type, and number of varnish coats.

**Ask about the subfloor.** Solid parquet glued to an uneven or damp screed will hollow and pop. Confirm how levels and moisture are checked and how long new screed cures before laying.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does parquet flooring cost per square foot in Singapore?

Parquet flooring in Singapore typically costs SGD 3 to SGD 5 per sq ft for laminate parquet, SGD 5 to SGD 11 per sq ft for engineered wood, and SGD 7 to SGD 18 or more per sq ft for solid teak, oak, or premium species, including installation. Hacking of old flooring, screeding, skirting, and decorative patterns are usually priced on top.

### What is the difference between solid, engineered, and laminate parquet?

Solid parquet is cut entirely from real timber and can be sanded and refinished many times. Engineered parquet has a real timber wear layer bonded to a plywood or composite core, so it looks and feels like solid wood but moves less with humidity. Laminate parquet is a printed wood image on a fibreboard core under a protective layer: it is the cheapest option, but it cannot be sanded and is vulnerable to water damage at the joints.

### Is parquet flooring suitable for HDB flats in Singapore's humidity?

Yes, parquet works well in HDB flats if the right product is chosen and installed properly. Engineered parquet is the safer choice for most flats because its layered core resists the swelling and shrinking that Singapore's year-round humidity causes in solid timber. Avoid parquet in bathrooms and wet kitchens, and use a contractor listed in HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors.

### Do termites attack parquet flooring in Singapore?

Yes, subterranean termites are common in Singapore and can attack timber parquet, especially floors laid directly on screed in older flats and landed homes. Warning signs include hollow-sounding blocks, soft spots, small holes, and mud tracks along skirting. If you find damage, get a pest control company to treat the colony first, then have a parquet contractor such as MCL Flooring or Mr Wood Varnish replace the affected blocks.

### How much does parquet polishing and varnishing cost in Singapore?

Parquet sanding and varnishing in Singapore typically costs around SGD 1.50 to SGD 3 per sq ft, which works out to roughly SGD 450 to SGD 950 for a single bedroom up to a three-bedroom area, depending on the floor condition and the varnish used. Minor repairs such as filling scratches and dents add about SGD 3 to SGD 6 per sq ft, and furniture moving is often charged separately.

### How long does parquet flooring last?

Solid parquet can last 30 to 50 years or more with proper care, because it can be sanded back and refinished several times over its life. Engineered parquet typically lasts 15 to 30 years depending on wear layer thickness, as thicker layers allow one or more light refinishes. Laminate parquet usually lasts 10 to 20 years and cannot be refinished. Recoating the varnish every few years extends the life of any real timber floor.

### How much more does herringbone or chevron parquet cost?

Herringbone and chevron parquet usually cost 20 to 40 percent more than straight plank layouts for the same timber, because the patterns need more cutting, more labour, and produce more offcut wastage. Basketweave and Versailles panels are more premium again. SinHao E&C and Calvary Carpentry both take on patterned and bespoke layouts, so they are good places to request a pattern quote.

### Can parquet flooring be installed over existing tiles?

Engineered or click-lock parquet can often be laid over existing tiles as a floating floor, provided the tiles are sound, level, and free of hollow spots, and there is enough door clearance for the extra height. Traditional solid parquet blocks are normally glued to a level screed, so old tiles or flooring are usually hacked out first. A contractor should check tile adhesion and floor levels on site before recommending either method.
