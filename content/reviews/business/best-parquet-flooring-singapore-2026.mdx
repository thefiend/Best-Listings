## What to Look for When Hiring a Parquet Flooring Contractor in Singapore

**Google reviews and recency.** A 4.5-star rating with 50 or more reviews is the minimum credibility threshold for parquet flooring contractors in Singapore. Review recency matters more than the aggregate: a contractor with 150 five-star reviews but nothing posted in the past six months may have lost key staff or declined in quality. Prioritise contractors with a consistent stream of recent reviews from clients describing project types similar to yours.

**Supply-and-install vs. installation only.** Some parquet flooring contractors supply the materials themselves; others expect you to source materials and pay them only for labour. Supply-and-install contractors take responsibility for material quality, meaning defects in the product are their problem to resolve. Installation-only contractors can be cost-effective if you have a reliable material source, but the accountability split creates complexity when problems arise. For most homeowners, supply-and-install is lower risk.

**Subfloor preparation capability.** Parquet flooring installed on an inadequately prepared subfloor will creak, shift, and lift within months. Ask whether the contractor assesses and prepares the subfloor as part of their scope. Levelling compounds, moisture barriers, and adhesive selection depend on substrate type — a contractor who skips this step is taking a shortcut that affects long-term performance. Request confirmation of their subfloor preparation process before signing.

**Material specification in quotations.** Reputable parquet contractors provide quotations that specify timber species, board dimensions, wear layer thickness for engineered products, finish type, and adhesive or fastening method. A quote that says only "parquet flooring — SGD X" with no further specification makes it impossible to compare with competing quotes and often leads to disputes once cheaper materials are substituted on-site. Insist on full material specification in writing.

**Restoration vs. replacement assessment.** For properties with existing parquet floors showing wear, dulling, or minor surface damage, restoration through sanding and refinishing is often the more cost-effective option compared to full replacement. A knowledgeable parquet contractor should be able to assess board condition and advise on whether restoration or replacement is the appropriate approach. Be wary of contractors who always recommend replacement without first assessing whether the boards are structurally sound.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does parquet flooring installation cost in Singapore?

Engineered parquet flooring installation in Singapore typically costs SGD 5 to SGD 12 per square foot for materials and installation combined, depending on timber species, wear layer thickness, and board width. Solid timber parquet ranges from SGD 8 to SGD 18 per square foot. For a standard 1,000-square-foot HDB flat, total flooring costs typically fall between SGD 5,000 and SGD 18,000 depending on material choice and scope.

### What is the difference between solid parquet and engineered parquet flooring?

Solid parquet is cut entirely from a single piece of timber and can be sanded and refinished multiple times over its lifespan. Engineered parquet has a real timber wear layer bonded to a plywood or HDF core, making it more dimensionally stable in Singapore's humid climate. Engineered parquet is generally recommended for Singapore homes due to its resistance to expansion and contraction with humidity changes, particularly in HDB and condo environments.

### How long does parquet flooring installation take in Singapore?

A standard HDB 4-room flat parquet installation typically takes 1 to 2 days for experienced teams. Larger landed properties can take 3 to 5 days depending on floor area and complexity. Solid parquet installations that include sanding and finishing on-site require additional time, typically 2 to 4 days for the finishing process after laying.

### Do I need to remove existing flooring before installing parquet?

In most cases, yes. Installing parquet over existing tiles or flooring raises floor levels, affects door clearance, and creates adhesion problems. Contractors should hack and remove existing flooring, assess the subfloor condition, carry out any necessary levelling, and then install the parquet. Some engineered floating-floor systems can be installed over existing surfaces in specific conditions, but this requires a proper assessment.

### Can parquet flooring be repaired rather than replaced?

Yes, in many cases. Boards with surface scratches, dullness, or minor staining can be restored through sanding and refinishing at a fraction of replacement cost. Individual boards that are cracked or lifted can often be replaced without disturbing the surrounding floor. A qualified parquet flooring contractor should assess the floor condition and advise on whether repair, restoration, or replacement is the appropriate solution before proceeding.

### What timber species are commonly used for parquet flooring in Singapore?

Common species for parquet flooring in Singapore include teak, merbau, chengal, and oak. Teak is prized for its durability and natural oil content. Merbau is popular for its reddish-brown colour and hardness. Chengal is exceptionally dense and durable, commonly used in landed properties. Oak is widely available in engineered format and offers a consistent European aesthetic at competitive pricing.

### Are there restrictions on parquet flooring in Singapore HDB flats?

HDB flats require impact noise insulation beneath hard flooring to meet sound transmission requirements for neighbours below. Parquet contractors working in HDB flats must install an HDB-approved underlayment beneath the flooring. Failure to do so can result in complaints, fines, and mandatory rectification. Confirm with your contractor that they include approved acoustic underlayment in their scope for HDB projects.

### How far in advance should I book a parquet flooring contractor in Singapore?

For well-reviewed parquet flooring contractors, book 4 to 6 weeks in advance. During peak renovation periods, particularly the first and last quarters of the year when BTO flat handovers are concentrated, the best firms fill their project slots 6 to 8 weeks ahead. Engaging early also allows time for material selection, delivery lead times for imported species, and any subfloor preparation that needs to precede installation.

### What questions should I ask before hiring a parquet flooring contractor?

Ask: What timber species and grade will be used? What is the wear layer thickness for engineered products? How will the subfloor be prepared? Is acoustic underlayment included? What finishing product is applied and how many coats? What is your warranty on workmanship and materials? Can you provide an itemised quotation covering materials, installation, subfloor preparation, and disposal of existing flooring separately?

### What are red flags when hiring a parquet flooring contractor in Singapore?

Red flags include: lump-sum quotes with no material specification; contractors who do not ask about subfloor condition before quoting; requests for full payment before work commences; inability to provide an itemised breakdown; no verifiable physical business address; very low pricing without explanation of material grade; and verbal agreements rather than a written contract covering scope, timeline, and payment schedule.
