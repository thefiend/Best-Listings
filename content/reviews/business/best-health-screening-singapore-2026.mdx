## What to Look for When Choosing a Health Screening Centre in Singapore

**Panel comprehensiveness relative to your age and risk profile.** A basic package covering complete blood count, lipid profile, fasting glucose, and urinalysis is sufficient for healthy individuals under 35. From age 40 onwards, or earlier with family history of cancer or heart disease, look for centres offering cancer markers (CEA, CA-125, PSA), cardiac stress testing, and optionally CT coronary calcium scoring. Always confirm what is included before booking — "comprehensive" means different things at different centres.

**Google review score and volume.** For medical providers, a minimum of 4.5 stars and at least 50 reviews is the baseline. More important is the combination of score and volume: Mediway Medical at 4.9 stars across 7,559 reviews carries far more statistical weight than a 5.0 score from eight reviews. For health screening specifically, recency also matters: confirm there are reviews from the past 3 months before booking.

**Result turnaround and doctor consultation.** A health screening result without clinical interpretation is of limited value. Confirm whether the centre includes a doctor consultation to explain your results, and ask how results are delivered. Centres that send a PDF by email with no follow-up call are not providing a complete service. Same-day or next-day results are now achievable at most reputable providers.

**Teleconsult availability.** For busy professionals, centres that offer teleconsult for result consultations save a significant amount of time. Digital Health Clinic leads on this dimension. Confirm whether teleconsult is included in the package price or billed separately.

**Corporate and insurance billing.** If your employer provides a health screening benefit, confirm whether your chosen centre bills directly to corporate accounts or insurance panels. HOP Medical and Mediway Medical both have established corporate billing relationships. This detail is often not advertised on websites and is worth confirming by phone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does health screening cost in Singapore?

Basic health screening packages in Singapore cost SGD 80–200 and cover essential blood tests, urine analysis, and BMI measurements. Mid-tier packages with cancer markers and cardiac screening range from SGD 250–500. Premium executive packages including CT scans, cardiac stress tests, and full hormonal profiling range from SGD 600–1,500. Prices vary by centre and panel tier.

### What is included in a standard health screening package in Singapore?

A standard health screening package typically includes: complete blood count (CBC), fasting blood glucose, lipid profile (cholesterol), kidney function tests, liver function tests, urinalysis, and BMI and blood pressure measurement. Some mid-tier packages add cancer markers such as PSA (for men) or CA-125 (for women), hepatitis B and C serology, and thyroid function.

### How often should I go for health screening in Singapore?

The Health Promotion Board recommends annual health screening for adults aged 40 and above for core chronic disease markers. For younger adults aged 18–39, biennial screening for blood pressure and cholesterol is the baseline recommendation. Individuals with family history of cancer, heart disease, or diabetes should discuss more frequent or expanded screening with their doctor.

### How do I prepare for a health screening appointment?

Most blood-based health screenings require a 8–12 hour fast (water is permitted). Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before the test. Bring your NRIC or passport and any relevant medical history documents. Wear comfortable clothing if the package includes a physical examination. Confirm fasting requirements with your chosen centre at the time of booking.

### Can I use MediSave or insurance for health screening in Singapore?

MediSave can be used for selected health screening under the Screen for Life programme at CHAS GP clinics, covering subsidised rates for eligible Singaporeans and PRs. Private health screening packages at the centres on this list are typically self-pay unless covered by employer insurance. Check your specific insurance policy for outpatient screening benefits.

### What is the Screen for Life programme in Singapore?

Screen for Life (SFL) is a national programme by the Health Promotion Board that subsidises screening for chronic diseases including diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, and cervical cancer at CHAS-accredited GP clinics. Singaporeans and PRs aged 18 and above can access subsidised rates. The programme does not cover premium health screening packages at private specialist centres.

### What is the difference between a basic and executive health screening package?

Basic packages cover core chronic disease markers through blood and urine tests. Executive packages add layers of diagnostic depth: cancer tumour markers, cardiac risk profiling, advanced imaging (CT scans, ultrasound), hormonal profiling, audiometry, optometry, and sometimes a cardiovascular stress test. Executive packages take longer — typically half a day — and cost significantly more, but provide a substantially more complete clinical picture.

### Are health screening results confidential in Singapore?

Yes. Under the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and the Private Hospitals and Medical Clinics Act, your medical records and screening results are confidential and cannot be shared without your consent, except in specific circumstances required by law. Always request your results in writing and retain them for your own records.

### How do I choose between a hospital-based and standalone health screening centre?

Hospital-based centres such as HealthConnexion at Farrer Park offer the advantage of immediate specialist referral if a result requires follow-up — without leaving the building. Standalone and clinic-based screeners are often faster, more affordably priced, and more convenient for straightforward annual checks. Choose hospital-based screening if you have complex health history or are seeking premium comprehensive packages; choose standalone centres for efficient, cost-effective annual wellness checks.
