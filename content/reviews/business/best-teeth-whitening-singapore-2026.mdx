## What to Look for When Choosing a Teeth Whitening Clinic in Singapore

**MOH-registered dental clinic, not an aesthetic salon.** In Singapore, teeth whitening using peroxide-based bleaching agents above certain concentrations is a dental procedure that must be performed by a licensed dentist in a registered dental clinic. Always verify that the clinic is listed on the Singapore Dental Council register before booking. Aesthetic salons and nail studios offering "whitening" typically use non-peroxide cosmetic products with limited effect.

**In-chair versus take-home options.** In-chair professional whitening uses higher-concentration gel activated by a light source for results in 60–90 minutes. Take-home custom tray kits use lower-concentration gel worn daily over 2–4 weeks. The best clinics offer both, and will recommend one based on your sensitivity levels, existing dental work, and desired timeline.

**Google reviews and recency.** For dental services in Singapore, a 4.8+ rating with 100+ reviews is a reliable quality signal. More important than the average is the recency: check that reviews from the past 3 months are consistently positive. A clinic with many old positive reviews but recent complaints about new dentists or changed ownership is a yellow flag.

**Sensitivity management.** Post-whitening sensitivity is one of the most common complaints. Reputable clinics pre-assess your enamel health, adjust peroxide concentration accordingly, and provide desensitising gel for at-home use after treatment. Ask specifically what their protocol is for patients with sensitive teeth before booking.

**Pricing transparency.** Legitimate clinics quote a clear fee for whitening before treatment begins. Avoid any clinic that quotes a low entry price and then adds charges for items like the custom tray, consultation, or desensitising gel as separate line items without disclosing them upfront.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does teeth whitening cost in Singapore?

Professional in-chair teeth whitening in Singapore costs between SGD 600 and SGD 1,500 depending on the clinic location, the system used (Zoom, Pola, BEYOND), and whether a take-home maintenance kit is included. Heartland clinics tend to charge SGD 600–900 while Orchard Road and CBD clinics typically charge SGD 900–1,500. Take-home custom tray kits alone cost SGD 200–500.

### Is teeth whitening safe in Singapore?

Yes, when performed by a licensed dentist using clinically approved peroxide gel at regulated concentrations. Hydrogen peroxide and carbamide peroxide are both proven safe for enamel at the concentrations used in professional dental whitening. The Singapore Dental Council regulates which concentrations are permissible. The main side effect is temporary post-treatment sensitivity, which typically resolves within 24–48 hours.

### How long does teeth whitening last?

In-chair professional whitening results typically last 12–24 months depending on your diet and oral hygiene habits. Coffee, tea, red wine, and dark-coloured foods accelerate re-staining. Most dentists recommend a take-home maintenance kit to extend results. A touch-up session once a year is common among regular whitening patients.

### Can I get teeth whitening if I have crowns or veneers?

Whitening gel does not bleach porcelain, ceramic, or composite restorations. If you have crowns, veneers, or bonding on your front teeth, your dentist will need to assess whether whitening is appropriate and plan for colour-matching any restorations afterwards if the natural teeth lighten significantly. Always disclose existing dental work during your whitening consultation.

### What is the difference between Zoom, Pola, and BEYOND whitening?

All three are established light-activated in-chair whitening systems using hydrogen peroxide gel. Zoom (Philips) and BEYOND are more commonly available in Singapore private clinics. Pola Office is widely used due to its sensitivity-minimising formula. The brand matters less than the concentration used and the dentist's technique. Ask your dentist which system they use and why they prefer it.

### How many shades whiter can I expect from professional whitening?

Most patients achieve 4–8 shades lighter on the Vita shade guide from a single in-chair session. Results depend on your starting shade, tooth structure, and the degree of intrinsic versus extrinsic staining. Intrinsic staining (from tetracycline, fluorosis, or trauma) responds less well to peroxide whitening. Your dentist should set realistic expectations during the consultation.

### Is teeth whitening covered by Medisave or insurance in Singapore?

No. Teeth whitening is classified as a cosmetic procedure in Singapore and is not covered by Medisave, MediShield Life, or most integrated shield plans. CHAS subsidies apply to basic dental treatments such as scaling and polishing but not cosmetic whitening. Some employer dental benefit plans may include a cosmetic allowance; check your policy terms.

### What should I do before and after a teeth whitening treatment?

Before: brush and floss thoroughly, avoid staining foods and drinks 24 hours prior, and inform the dentist of any tooth sensitivity or existing restorations. After: avoid coffee, tea, red wine, soy sauce, and coloured beverages for at least 48 hours (the "white diet"). Use the desensitising gel provided if sensitivity occurs. Brush with a soft toothbrush and sensitive toothpaste for the first week.

### Are at-home whitening strips or kits from pharmacies as effective as professional whitening?

No. Over-the-counter whitening products sold in Singapore pharmacies and online contain very low concentrations of hydrogen peroxide (typically 0.1–0.5%), far below the levels used in professional in-chair treatments. They can remove surface stains but will not achieve the same degree of colour change or lasting results. They are best used as maintenance between professional sessions rather than as a substitute.

### How do I choose between in-chair and take-home whitening?

In-chair whitening is best if you want fast results for a specific event (wedding, job interview, reunion). Take-home custom tray whitening is better if you have high sensitivity, prefer gradual change, or want more control over the final shade. Many dentists recommend combining both: an in-chair session for immediate lift, followed by take-home trays for maintenance and fine-tuning.
