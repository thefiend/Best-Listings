## What to Look for When Hiring a Website Redesign Service

**A formal SEO migration audit in the project scope.** The single most dangerous oversight in a website redesign is URL structure changes without a corresponding 301 redirect plan. Every page that has accumulated inbound links or organic ranking position is at risk the moment its URL changes without a redirect. Ask any agency you are evaluating to describe their SEO migration process specifically: how they audit existing rankings, how they map redirects, and how they monitor for ranking loss after launch. Agencies that cannot answer this question in detail are telling you that ranking loss is a foreseeable outcome of working with them.

**Core Web Vitals performance built into the build specification.** Google uses Core Web Vitals (Largest Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift, Interaction to Next Paint) as direct ranking signals. A redesign that launches with poor Core Web Vitals scores is trading ranking strength for visual refresh. Require that the agency provides a post-launch PageSpeed Insights score target in writing and include it in the delivery acceptance criteria.

**Post-launch monitoring with a defined coverage period.** Every live website behaves differently in production compared to the staging environment. Issues with tracking implementation, redirect chains, mobile rendering, and server performance only surface under real traffic conditions. A 30-day post-launch monitoring period with a defined response time for issue resolution is the minimum acceptable support commitment for a professional website redesign engagement.

**A portfolio with your industry or a directly adjacent one.** A web design agency that has redesigned professional services firms understands the content priorities, trust signals, and compliance considerations that sector requires. Ask for two or three redesign portfolio examples specifically in your industry or a closely adjacent one before committing to a briefing.

**Fixed-scope pricing or a clearly defined change management process.** Redesign projects routinely expand when clients add requirements mid-project. Before work begins, confirm whether the quoted price is fixed scope or estimate-based, what triggers a change request, and how additional work is priced. Ambiguity on this point is the most common source of budget overruns and project relationship breakdowns in website redesign engagements.

### Will a website redesign affect my Google rankings?

A website redesign can both improve and damage Google rankings depending on how the migration is managed. Rankings are at risk when URLs change without 301 redirects, when page content is removed or significantly changed, when internal linking structure is disrupted, or when Core Web Vitals scores worsen at launch. Rankings improve when the redesign includes proper SEO migration, better content structure, faster page load times, improved mobile experience, and cleaner technical implementation. The agencies on this list include SEO migration planning in their redesign scope. Agencies that do not have a formal SEO migration process are the ones most likely to cause ranking loss.

### How long does a website redesign take in Singapore?

A standard SME website redesign in Singapore takes 4 to 8 weeks from briefing to launch. Corporate redesigns with custom UX research, multiple stakeholder approval stages, and CMS integration typically require 8 to 12 weeks. E-commerce redesigns with product data migration, payment gateway integration, and multi-device testing take 8 to 16 weeks. The most common cause of delays is content: if the client provides final text and images late, every subsequent stage shifts. Agree a content submission deadline with the agency at the start of the project and hold to it.

### What is the difference between a website redesign and a website refresh?

A website refresh involves updating the visual style, typography, colours, and imagery of an existing site without changing the underlying structure, content, or technology. A redesign involves reconstructing the site architecture, content strategy, user journey, and often the technology stack. Refreshes are lower cost and lower risk but address surface appearance only. Redesigns resolve structural problems that refreshes cannot: poor conversion flow, mobile usability issues, Core Web Vitals failures, and outdated CMS platforms. For sites with conversion or performance problems, a refresh will not resolve the underlying cause.

### Should I redesign my website or build a new one from scratch?

Redesigning preserves the SEO equity your current site has accumulated: domain age, inbound links, indexed pages, and any existing rankings. Building from scratch on a new domain means starting that equity accumulation from zero, which can set organic search performance back by 12 to 24 months. Redesign is the correct choice for most Singapore businesses with established websites unless the existing domain has been penalised by Google, the technology platform is unsalvageable, or the business has undergone a fundamental change in positioning that makes the existing URL structure irrelevant. In ambiguous cases, a discovery audit from an agency with SEO migration expertise will give you the data to make the decision correctly.

### What should I prepare before briefing a website redesign agency?

Provide a complete list of your current pages and their URLs, your Google Analytics and Google Search Console data showing traffic and ranking performance, your brand guidelines if they exist, examples of competitor or aspirational sites that demonstrate the visual direction you want to move toward, and a clear statement of the business outcome you are trying to achieve with the redesign. The more specific you are about measurable objectives: enquiry rate, organic rankings, bounce rate, conversion rate: the more accurately the agency can scope the work and the more accountable they are for delivering against it.

### How do I avoid losing SEO rankings during a website redesign?

Insist on a formal SEO migration audit before design work begins. This should cover: a full crawl of all existing URLs and their ranking positions, a redirect map for every URL that will change, preservation of all title tags and meta descriptions into the new build, structured data migration, and a 30-day post-launch monitoring period with ranking position tracking. Ask any agency you are evaluating to describe this process specifically. If they cannot outline these steps, budget for ranking recovery as a post-launch cost.

### Can a website redesign improve my conversion rate?

Yes, when the redesign includes conversion rate optimisation as a design objective. This means analysing where current visitors drop off, what content or calls-to-action are missing or mispositioned, whether the primary action (enquiry form, phone call, purchase) is visible and frictionless on mobile, and whether the page content addresses the specific concerns of the target customer at each stage of their decision process. Cosmetic redesigns that update the visual style without addressing conversion architecture may improve first impression without improving business outcomes. Insist that the agency includes conversion performance metrics in the project success criteria, not just delivery milestones.

### What questions should I ask a website redesign agency before signing?

Ask: How do you handle SEO migration for URL changes? What are your Core Web Vitals targets for the redesigned site? What is your post-launch monitoring and support period? How do you handle change requests and what do they cost? Can you show me two or three redesign projects in my industry or a similar one? What is the content submission deadline for the project to launch on time? What analytics and tracking setup is included in the scope? The answers to these questions will reveal whether the agency is treating this as a technical delivery project with measurable outcomes or as a visual production exercise.

### How do I measure whether my website redesign was successful?

Measure performance against the specific business objectives agreed at the briefing stage. For conversion: enquiry form submissions, phone call volume, and direct sale completions before and after launch. For organic search: keyword ranking positions and organic traffic via Google Search Console. For user experience: bounce rate, average session duration, and pages per session via Google Analytics. For technical performance: Core Web Vitals scores via PageSpeed Insights and Google Search Console. Establish baseline measurements before the redesign launches and compare at 30, 60, and 90 days post-launch. If an agency cannot define these metrics at briefing, ask them to do so before scope is agreed.
