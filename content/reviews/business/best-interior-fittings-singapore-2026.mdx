This guide is for homeowners collecting BTO keys, tenants taking over a Cat A office, and retailers racing a mall fit-out deadline. We wrote it because most "best interior" lists rank design studios on renders, while the hard part of any project is execution on site. We evaluated 72 unique Singapore firms from Google Maps searches for "interior fitting out contractor singapore" and "interior fittings singapore", scoring each on verified rating, review volume and evidence of hands-on fit-out capability. ## How We Ranked These Interior Fit-Out Contractors

We started from two Google Maps datasets ("interior fitting out contractor singapore" and "interior fittings singapore"), merged them and removed duplicates by Google place ID, leaving 72 unique firms. Every firm had to meet two hard thresholds: a verified Google rating of at least 4.5 stars and at least 15 Google reviews. We then prioritised firms whose name, Google category or published services signalled hands-on fit-out work: carpentry and built-ins, false ceilings, partitions, kitchen fit-out, A&A, M&E or contract furniture. Pure design studios and lead-generation platforms were deprioritised because our separate interior design and renovation rankings already cover them. We ranked by rating, weighted by review volume: a 4.9-star firm with 270+ reviews ranks above 5.0-star firms with under 70 reviews, because a larger sample is harder to sustain. Each firm's score is its Google rating multiplied by two. Firms without a verifiable Singapore address were excluded.

## What to Look for When Hiring an Interior Fit-Out Contractor in Singapore

**Licensing that matches your property.** For HDB flats, the contractor must be on HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors; check the registration number before signing. For condos, confirm the firm handles MCST applications. For commercial units, ask about experience with building management and fire safety submissions.

**In-house carpentry versus subcontracted.** Carpentry is the biggest cost and most visible quality marker in any fit-out. Ask where cabinets are fabricated and who installs on site. Firms with their own workshop or factory have more control over lead times and finish consistency.

**Itemised quotations by foot run and square foot.** A credible quote lists every built-in by foot run (for example "kitchen bottom cabinet, 10 ft, HPL laminate, Blum soft-close"), every ceiling and partition by square foot, and the board and hardware brands. Lump-sum quotes make it impossible to compare contractors or challenge variation orders.

**A payment schedule tied to milestones.** Avoid contractors that ask for 50 percent or more upfront. A fair schedule ties payments to verifiable progress: deposit, start of works, carpentry delivery, and final payment after defect inspection.

**Review volume, recency and photos.** A 4.5-star rating with 15 reviews is our floor; 50 or more recent reviews with customer-uploaded photos is far stronger evidence. BestThingReview.com weighs review volume heavily for exactly this reason.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does interior fit-out cost per square foot in Singapore?

In 2026, a Cat B office fit-out in Singapore typically costs SGD 50 to SGD 150 per sq ft depending on partitions, ceilings, M&E and joinery, with high-end corporate offices exceeding SGD 200 per sq ft. Retail fit-outs usually run SGD 80 to SGD 200 per sq ft, and F&B outlets cost more due to exhaust, grease traps and wet works. Home carpentry is quoted per foot run: roughly SGD 150 to SGD 250 for laminate kitchen cabinets and SGD 200 to SGD 350 for full-height wardrobes, so a carpentry-focused 4-room HDB fit-out commonly lands between SGD 20,000 and SGD 45,000.

### What is the difference between interior fit-out, interior design and renovation?

Interior design is the planning stage: layouts, concepts, materials and drawings. Interior fit-out is the build stage: carpentry, built-ins, false ceilings, partitions, flooring, lighting and finishes installed into a space. Renovation is the broader term for altering an existing space, often including hacking, wet works, re-plumbing and structural A&A. If design is your priority, compare the [best interior design companies in Singapore](/business/best-interior-design-companies-singapore); for heavy hacking or structural works, see the [best renovation contractors in Singapore](/business/best-renovation-contractor-singapore).

### What is the difference between Cat A and Cat B fit-out?

Cat A fit-out is the landlord's base build: finished floors, suspended ceilings, basic lighting, air-conditioning distribution, fire protection and toilets, delivered as an open-plan shell. Cat B fit-out is everything the tenant adds: partitions, meeting rooms, reception, pantry, carpentry, branded finishes, extra power and data, and furniture. Grade A offices in Singapore are usually handed over at Cat A, and leases often require tenants to reinstate the unit to Cat A or bare condition when they move out.

### Do I need HDB or BCA approval for interior fit-out works?

Yes for certain works. HDB flats must be renovated by a contractor on HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors, and works such as hacking walls or altering windows need an HDB renovation permit, normally applied for by the contractor. Structural works need plans endorsed by a Professional Engineer and approval from the Building and Construction Authority (BCA). Commercial fit-outs also need building management approval and, where fire safety is affected, SCDF submissions through a Qualified Person. Condo owners need MCST approval and usually pay a refundable renovation deposit.

### How long does an interior fit-out take in Singapore?

A carpentry-focused fit-out of a new BTO flat usually takes 6 to 10 weeks from site measurement to handover, with fabrication alone taking 3 to 5 weeks. A resale flat with hacking and wet works typically needs 8 to 12 weeks. A Cat B office of 1,000 to 5,000 sq ft generally needs 6 to 12 weeks on site plus 2 to 6 weeks for approvals. Mall retail units often get a fixed 4 to 8 week fit-out period.

### Which carpentry material is better: plywood or MDF?

Plywood is the standard carcass material for Singapore built-ins because it holds screws well, resists sagging and tolerates humidity better than MDF. MDF has a smoother surface suited to spray-painted doors, but it swells if water penetrates its edges, so avoid it near sinks and in kitchen bottom cabinets. Most carpentry is finished in high-pressure laminate (HPL), which is durable and scratch-resistant. For wet areas, aluminium or PVC foam board cabinets are moisture-proof alternatives. Ask for board type, thickness and laminate brand in the quotation.

### Should I choose a CaseTrust-accredited or HDB-registered contractor?

For any HDB flat, an HDB-registered contractor is mandatory. CaseTrust accreditation, administered by the Consumers Association of Singapore (CASE), is voluntary but adds protection: accredited renovation businesses use a standard contract and must protect customer deposits. Empire Works states CaseTrust accreditation on its website. Verify both statuses online before paying a deposit.

### What is a fair payment schedule for a fit-out project?

A fair schedule ties payments to verifiable milestones and holds back a final portion until defects are fixed. A common residential structure is 10 to 20 percent on signing, 30 to 40 percent at the start of works, 30 to 40 percent on carpentry installation, and the final 5 to 10 percent after handover inspection. Commercial fit-outs usually use monthly progress claims with 5 percent retention through the defects liability period. Be cautious of any contractor asking for more than 50 percent before work begins.
