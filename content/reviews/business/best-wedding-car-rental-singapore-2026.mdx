## What to Look for When Renting a Wedding Car in Singapore

**Bridal car decoration service.** Confirm whether flower and ribbon decoration is included in the package or charged separately, and whether you provide the flowers or the operator does. Ask to see photographs of previous decorated vehicles. A provider who does not include this in their standard wedding package will require additional coordination on the wedding day when you have no capacity for additional logistics.

**Wedding day itinerary experience.** Ask the operator directly whether their driver has handled gate ceremony pickups before, and whether they understand the typical sequence of a Singapore wedding day. Drivers who have done this know that the gate ceremony may run beyond the scheduled time, that the solemnisation venue may have specific vehicle access restrictions, and that the banquet arrival needs to be timed to the minute. Operators who cannot discuss these specifics lack wedding-specific experience.

**Vehicle presentation standard.** For the bridal car specifically, the vehicle will appear in every photograph and video from the day. Request to inspect the actual vehicle — or a clear photograph of the exact vehicle being provided — before confirming the booking. Do not accept a booking where the specific vehicle is unconfirmed. Also confirm the cleaning and inspection protocol on the morning of the wedding day.

**Backup vehicle policy.** A vehicle breakdown on a wedding day is a catastrophe. Ask every provider directly: what is your backup vehicle policy if the booked vehicle is unavailable on the day? Professional operators have a backup protocol. Operators who cannot answer this question clearly should be excluded regardless of other factors.

**Driver punctuality record for timed events.** Read recent reviews specifically for wedding day timing. A provider with a 4.9 average but reviews mentioning late arrivals on wedding days should be excluded. For wedding car rental, driver punctuality is the single most critical operational factor — everything else can be adapted, but a late bridal car cannot be recovered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does wedding car rental cost in Singapore?
Wedding car rental in Singapore typically costs SGD 350–800 for a half-day bridal car depending on vehicle class. A Mercedes-Benz E-Class or S-Class bridal car is typically SGD 350–550 for a half-day, while a Bentley Flying Spur for premium engagements typically costs SGD 600–900 or more. Guest vehicles are typically SGD 150–350 per vehicle per session depending on vehicle class and distance. Full-day arrangements covering gate ceremony through to banquet departure cost more and should be quoted comprehensively. Always request a fully itemised quote before confirming, covering decoration, waiting time, and any surcharges for early morning or late-night runs.

### What is the difference between a bridal car and a guest car for a Singapore wedding?
The bridal car is the primary vehicle transporting the bride (and typically the groom during or after the gate ceremony) and appears in all formal wedding photography and videography. Guest cars are the supporting vehicles moving family members, the wedding party, and guests between venues. Guest cars are typically a tier below the bridal car in vehicle specification and may not include decoration. The bridal car is a photography and symbolic statement; the guest cars are a logistics requirement.
