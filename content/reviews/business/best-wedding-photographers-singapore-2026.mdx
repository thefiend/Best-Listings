## What to Look for When Hiring a Wedding Photographer

**Portfolio consistency over highlight reel quality.** It is easy for any photographer to curate a stunning Instagram feed from years of shooting. Ask to see complete galleries from three to five recent weddings, not just their best images. A consistent photographer produces gallery-worthy work throughout the full day, not just during golden hour or at iconic locations.

**Alignment with your shooting style preferences.** Wedding photography broadly divides into posed and editorial, candid and documentary, and cinematic and artistic styles. Review each studio's body of work carefully to determine whether their default output matches what you envision for your own wedding. Misalignment here is the most common cause of couple disappointment.

**Clear contract terms for deliverables and timelines.** Confirm in writing the exact number of edited photos included, the video reel duration if applicable, the expected turnaround time, album inclusion or pricing, and whether a second shooter is part of the package. Vague package descriptions often lead to post-event disputes.

**Communication responsiveness before booking.** How a photographer communicates during the inquiry and booking phase is a strong predictor of how they will communicate on your wedding day. Studios that take days to respond to emails or give unclear answers to questions about their packages often create unnecessary stress closer to the event.

**Backup equipment and contingency plans.** Professional wedding photographers should own multiple camera bodies and lens sets, carry backup batteries and memory cards, and have a clear plan for what happens if they fall ill on your wedding day. Ask directly about their contingency protocols before signing any contract.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does wedding photography cost in Singapore in 2026?

Wedding photography packages in Singapore typically range from $1,500 to $6,000 depending on coverage duration, number of photographers, video inclusion, and album production. ROM-only packages start around $800 to $1,500. Full actual day coverage with a second shooter and highlight video generally falls between $3,000 and $6,000 at premium studios.

### When should I book a wedding photographer in Singapore?

You should book your wedding photographer at least 9 to 12 months before your wedding date for popular weekend slots. Many top studios are fully booked for peak-season weekends well over a year in advance. For ROM ceremonies on weekdays, three to six months' notice is usually sufficient.

### What is the difference between actual day and pre-wedding photography?

Actual day photography covers your wedding ceremony and reception as events unfold. Pre-wedding photography is a separate, pre-planned shoot at locations of your choice, typically done months before the wedding, focused on couple portraits and creative compositions rather than event documentation.

### Do Singapore wedding photographers also offer videography?

Many studios offer combined photography and videography packages, and some specialise equally in both. Studios like Annabel Law Productions and Hey Stranger Photography are known for strong combined packages. If video is important to you, ensure you review the videography portfolio separately from photography work.

### What should I prepare for a pre-wedding shoot in Singapore?

Prepare two to three outfit changes, coordinate with your photographer on location choices and time of day for ideal lighting, bring any props that represent your relationship, and ensure any required location permits are secured. Discuss the editing style beforehand so post-processing aligns with your preferences.

### Can I request specific shots or a shot list?

Yes. Most professional wedding photographers welcome a shot list covering must-have family group combinations and key moments. However, for documentary-style photographers, avoid over-scripting the day as it can interfere with natural storytelling. Share your priorities and trust the photographer to execute them.

### What happens if my photographer is sick on my wedding day?

Professional studios should have a clear backup plan in writing. Ask explicitly before signing the contract. Most established studios maintain networks of trusted associate photographers who can step in at short notice with a full briefing on your wedding details.

### Should I choose a photographer based on Google reviews or Instagram following?

### Is it worth hiring a second photographer for my wedding?

For weddings with more than 80 guests or with parallel events happening simultaneously, a second photographer is highly recommended. It ensures comprehensive coverage and prevents key moments being missed.
