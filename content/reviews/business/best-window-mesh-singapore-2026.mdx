## What to Look for When Hiring a Window Mesh Installer in Singapore

**Match the mesh type to the window.** Magnetic screens suit casement windows with a flat frame surface, fixed-frame screens suit windows that are rarely opened, and zip track or retractable screens suit sliding doors and wide openings. A good installer recommends a type per window rather than quoting one product for the whole home.

**Pet-grade mesh for cats.** Cat owners should insist on pet-resistant mesh in a screwed or firmly anchored frame, not one held only by magnets or adhesive. A cat that can push a panel out on a high floor is a serious safety risk.

**On-site measurement and itemised quotes.** Reputable window mesh installers in Singapore measure every opening on site and itemise the quote per window or door. Be cautious of flat prices quoted over WhatsApp, as poor measurements are the leading cause of gaps and sagging mesh.

**Warranty on frame and mesh separately.** Ask how long the warranty covers the frame, the mesh fabric, and moving parts such as springs or zip track mechanisms. One to three years is common for standard products.

**Recent, specific reviews.** A rating of 4.5 stars or above with at least 50 Google reviews is a strong baseline. Look for recent reviews that mention HDB or condo installations and after-sales follow-up.

**Respect for the window structure.** Singapore homeowners are responsible for window safety, so make sure the installer does not drill into or modify window rivets, hinges, or locks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does window mesh cost in Singapore per window?

Window mesh in Singapore typically costs SGD 60 to SGD 150 per window for standard magnetic or fixed-frame mosquito screens, depending on window size and frame finish. Pet-resistant cat mesh usually costs SGD 120 to SGD 300 per window because of the thicker fabric and sturdier frame. Retractable, pleated, or zip track screens for sliding doors and balconies generally range from SGD 250 to SGD 700 per opening. A full HDB 4-room flat covered with standard mesh commonly totals SGD 800 to SGD 2,500, so always request an itemised quote per window.

### What is the difference between magnetic, retractable, and fixed window mesh?

Magnetic mesh attaches to the window frame with a magnetic strip and can be lifted off in seconds for cleaning, making it the most affordable and renter-friendly option. Fixed mesh is mounted in a permanent frame and is best for windows that are rarely opened or for cat-proofing, since it cannot be pushed out easily. Retractable and zip track mesh rolls or slides away when not in use, which suits sliding doors and balconies, but costs more because of the mechanism.

### Is cat mesh strong enough to stop my cat from escaping?

Pet-grade cat mesh is significantly stronger than standard insect mesh and resists clawing and pushing from most domestic cats when it is installed in a firmly anchored frame. The frame matters as much as the mesh: a strong fabric held only by magnets or adhesive can still be pushed out. Treat cat mesh as an escape barrier rather than a fall-protection device, and for high-floor flats with children, combine it with invisible grilles or window grilles.

### Do I need HDB approval to install mosquito mesh?

Installing mosquito mesh on the inside of HDB windows generally does not require HDB approval, as it does not alter the building facade or the window structure. Owners should still make sure the installation does not damage window rivets, hinges, or locks, since they are responsible for window safety. Condominium residents should check MCST by-laws, as some developments regulate anything visible from the exterior.

### How do I clean window mesh?

Brush or vacuum window mesh every few weeks to remove dust. For a deeper clean, rinse removable panels with water and mild soap and let them dry before refitting, or wipe fixed mesh in place with a damp microfibre cloth. Avoid bleach, solvents, and high-pressure water, which weaken the mesh coating.

### How long does window mesh last in Singapore?

Standard fibreglass window mesh typically lasts five to eight years in Singapore, depending on sun exposure, humidity, and how often the screens are handled. Pet-grade cat mesh usually lasts five to ten years, while stainless steel mesh can last more than ten years with minimal maintenance. West-facing windows exposed to strong afternoon sun tend to degrade fastest.

### Will window mesh block airflow and light?

Window mesh reduces airflow and light slightly, but the effect is usually small enough that most households do not notice it in daily use. Darker mesh colours such as charcoal or black tend to be less noticeable from inside than grey or white, and high-transparency mesh is available if ventilation or views are a priority.

### Can window mesh keep out geckos and other insects besides mosquitoes?

Yes, properly fitted window mesh keeps out geckos, flies, moths, and most flying insects as well as mosquitoes. The key is a gap-free fit around the full frame, since geckos will find any opening along the edges.

### How long does window mesh installation take?

Most window mesh installations in Singapore take one to three hours for a typical HDB flat once the screens are made. Expect a site measurement first, then one to two weeks of fabrication for custom mesh before a single installation visit.
