## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does roof leak repair cost in Singapore?

A targeted roof leak repair in Singapore typically costs SGD 300–800 for a residential property, depending on leak severity, access difficulty, and the materials required to seal the breach. Full roof waterproofing membrane application — where the entire roof surface is stripped and re-coated rather than patching a specific failure point — runs SGD 2,000–6,000 for a standard HDB penthouse or terrace house flat roof, scaling with roof area and membrane type. Complex leaks requiring scaffolding, parapet wall dismantlement, or simultaneous structural repair can exceed SGD 8,000. Always request an itemised written quote after a site inspection, never a phone estimate — the difference between a patch job and a full waterproofing application is significant enough that a remote quote has no diagnostic basis.

### Who is responsible for roof repairs in HDB flats versus private properties?

For HDB flats, the responsibility depends on location. Roof slabs and common roof areas above the top floor are managed by the HDB Town Council, which coordinates and funds maintenance through conservancy fees. If you are a top-floor flat owner experiencing a leak through the common roof, contact your Town Council directly — they are obligated to arrange repair. For internal roof structures within your unit boundary, the flat owner is responsible. In private landed properties, the entire roof is the owner's responsibility without exception. For condominiums, the strata management corporation (MCST) covers common roof areas above the building envelope, while individual penthouse or top-floor unit owners retain responsibility for internal roof elements within their unit boundaries.

### How do professionals find the source of a roof leak?

Experienced roof leak repair contractors use a combination of visual inspection, moisture mapping with a pin-type or capacitance moisture meter, and in some cases thermal imaging cameras to distinguish wet from dry substrate. The key diagnostic insight is that water rarely enters a roof and drops vertically — it tracks along structural members, insulation layers, and ceiling cavities before appearing as a stain at a location that may be metres from the actual breach. Surface inspection alone without measuring moisture content in the roof substrate typically results in misidentification. Contractors who apply waterproofing sealant over a visually suspected area without confirming moisture levels are patching the wrong location in a significant proportion of complex leak cases.

### How long does a roof leak repair take in Singapore?

A straightforward targeted repair — sealing a cracked tile, re-pointing a flashing joint, or patching a localised membrane failure — is typically completed within a single visit of two to four hours. More extensive repairs involving membrane strip-out and full reapplication require two to three working days depending on roof area and curing time requirements for the chosen waterproofing system. Full roof replacement for a standard terrace house takes three to seven working days. Weather is a constraint: waterproofing membrane application and sealant curing requires dry conditions, so rain delays are common and contractually unavoidable during Singapore's monsoon months. Build a one to two week buffer into any repair timeline scheduled between November and March to account for weather-related interruptions.
