## What to Look for When Hiring a Facebook Ads Agency

The most important factor when selecting a Facebook ads agency in Singapore is not their website or their pitch deck: it is their willingness to show you actual campaign data from comparable clients. Ask for anonymised account screenshots showing campaign structure, audience segmentation, creative test results, and cost-per-acquisition trends. Any agency unable to produce this before engagement is operating from templates rather than strategy.

Conversion tracking setup is non-negotiable. Meta Pixel alone is insufficient in a world where iOS privacy changes and browser restrictions limit cookie-based attribution. The agency you hire should be installing Meta's Conversions API and configuring server-side event matching as a standard part of campaign setup, not as an add-on. If they don't mention Conversions API during the initial conversation, that is a meaningful signal about their technical depth.

Check who owns the ad account. Your Facebook Ads account should be owned by your Business Manager from day one. Agencies that insist on running campaigns from their own account rather than granting access to yours are creating a situation where you have no data continuity and no ability to switch agencies without losing your entire historical audience and conversion data.

Creative testing is what separates performance agencies from maintenance agencies. A well-managed Facebook Ads account in Singapore should be testing new ad creative every two to four weeks: different hooks, formats, angles, and offers. If your agency is running the same creatives for months, the algorithm is running on stale signals and your results will plateau. Ask specifically about their creative testing cadence before signing.

Finally, verify the reporting structure. Monthly PDF reports with screenshots of Meta's own dashboard are not analysis: they are data delivery. The agency you hire should be explaining what the data means, what decisions it informs, and what changes they are making as a result. If the monthly report arrives without any strategic recommendation, you are paying for account management, not strategic expertise.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much do Facebook Ads agencies in Singapore charge?

Facebook Ads management fees in Singapore typically range from SGD 1,500 to SGD 3,500 per month for SME campaign programmes covering a single product or service line, SGD 3,500 to SGD 7,000 per month for multi-campaign structures with active creative testing and audience segmentation, and SGD 7,000 to SGD 15,000-plus per month for enterprise-level programmes with multiple campaign objectives, large creative libraries, and integration with broader marketing technology stacks. Ad spend is separate from management fees and is billed directly by Meta. Agencies quoting below SGD 800 per month for active Facebook Ads management are almost certainly running templated campaigns without meaningful strategic input or creative testing.

### What is the difference between Facebook Ads and Instagram Ads?

Facebook and Instagram Ads are managed through the same Meta Ads Manager platform and can share audiences, creative, and budgets across both platforms within a single campaign. Facebook tends to perform better for older demographics (35-plus), B2B lead generation, and detailed interest-based targeting. Instagram performs better for younger audiences (18-34), visually-led consumer brands, and story and reel formats where creative quality drives engagement. Most well-structured campaigns run across both platforms simultaneously, with the delivery algorithm optimising spend allocation based on where your specific audience converts more efficiently.

### How long does it take to see results from Facebook Ads in Singapore?

A well-structured Facebook Ads campaign in Singapore with a sufficient budget typically enters an initial learning phase during the first two to four weeks as Meta's algorithm optimises delivery to your converting audience segments. Meaningful performance data, enough to make informed optimisation decisions, is usually available by week four. Campaigns targeting cold audiences for lead generation typically stabilise on cost-per-lead within six to eight weeks. E-commerce campaigns optimising for purchase events may take eight to twelve weeks to fully exit the learning phase, particularly if conversion volume is below Meta's recommended 50 purchase events per week threshold. Results promised within seven days are a reliable indicator of an agency overstating what is achievable.

### What should Facebook Ads in Singapore cost per lead?

Cost-per-lead from Facebook Ads in Singapore varies substantially by industry and campaign quality. Professional services (legal, financial advisory, medical) with high-intent audiences and strong creative typically see SGD 30 to SGD 80 per lead. E-commerce businesses targeting product-aware audiences can achieve SGD 10 to SGD 30 per lead for lower-consideration purchases. B2B lead generation for enterprise software or services commonly runs SGD 80 to SGD 200 per qualified lead. These figures reflect well-structured campaigns with proper tracking: poorly structured campaigns with broad targeting and no conversion event optimisation can produce costs five to ten times higher while delivering lower-quality leads.

### What is the Meta Conversions API and why does it matter?

The Meta Conversions API (CAPI) is a server-side integration that sends conversion event data directly from your web server to Meta's platform, bypassing the browser-based limitations that have reduced the accuracy of Meta Pixel tracking since Apple's App Tracking Transparency changes in 2021. Without CAPI, a significant proportion of conversions, particularly from iOS users, are either not tracked at all or are tracked with reduced accuracy. The practical effect is that Meta's algorithm cannot optimise delivery toward your converting audience segments as effectively, which raises cost-per-conversion and reduces campaign efficiency. Any Facebook ads agency in Singapore that is not implementing CAPI as a standard part of campaign setup is running on degraded tracking data and delivering suboptimal results.

### Is Facebook Ads or Google Ads better for Singapore businesses?

Facebook Ads and Google Ads serve different stages of the acquisition funnel and are most effective when run as a coordinated programme rather than competing alternatives. Google Ads captures existing demand: users actively searching for your product or service. Facebook and Instagram Ads create and amplify demand: reaching users based on demographic, interest, and behavioural signals rather than search intent. For businesses in established categories with clear keyword demand (legal, medical, financial services, home renovation), Google Ads often delivers lower cost-per-lead because intent is high. For businesses building new categories, reaching younger demographics, or selling visually-led products and services, Facebook and Instagram Ads frequently outperform search. The best Facebook ads agency in Singapore will help you understand which allocation serves your specific funnel stage.

### What Facebook Ads formats work best in Singapore?

Video ads, including short-form vertical video optimised for Reels and Stories, consistently outperform static image ads in Singapore's current social media environment, particularly for cold audience prospecting where storytelling and demonstration are required to create purchase intent. Static single-image ads remain effective for retargeting campaigns targeting warm audiences who already know the brand and need a direct response offer. Carousel ads work well for e-commerce with multiple products or for service businesses showing multiple use cases. Lead generation ads (Meta's native lead forms) reduce friction for service businesses by capturing contact details without requiring users to leave Facebook, though lead quality is typically lower than website conversion campaigns because the intent threshold is lower. A well-managed account tests multiple formats continuously rather than committing to a single ad type.

### What questions should I ask a Facebook Ads agency before hiring them?

Ask the following before signing any engagement: Can you show me anonymised account screenshots from clients in a similar category? What is your creative testing cadence and process? Do you install Conversions API as part of your setup? Who owns the ad account, your Business Manager or the agency's? What specific conversion events will you optimise towards? How do you attribute results across Meta and other channels? What is included in monthly reporting and what strategic recommendations will you provide? What is the minimum contract length and the notice period for termination? An agency that answers all of these clearly and specifically before you commit is operating with the transparency that warrants a managed budget.

### How do I know if my current Facebook Ads agency is underperforming?

Key indicators that a Facebook Ads agency is underperforming include: ad creative that has not been refreshed in more than four weeks, cost-per-lead increasing month-on-month without a corresponding increase in lead quality, campaigns optimising for reach or engagement rather than conversion events, absence of Conversions API in the tracking setup, monthly reports showing Meta's own dashboard screenshots without strategic commentary, no active A/B tests running in the account, and the agency operating from their own Business Manager rather than yours. If your account shows three or more of these signals, the agency is providing account maintenance rather than active performance management.
