## What to Look for When Hiring a Painting Contractor in Singapore

**Surface preparation quality.** Professional painting starts with proper surface preparation, not the paint itself. This means filling all cracks and holes, sanding rough areas, applying primer on bare patches, and protecting floors and furniture with drop cloths. Contractors who skip preparation steps produce work that chips, peels, or shows uneven texture within 12 to 18 months. When requesting quotes, always ask how the contractor handles surface preparation and whether skim coat work is included for walls with significant imperfections.

**Paint brand and grade specification.** Not all paints are equal. Nippon, Dulux, and Jotun premium grades offer better coverage, washability, and durability than budget alternatives. A quote that states "quality paint" without specifying the brand and grade is a yellow flag. Request that the paint brand, grade name, and number of coats be written into the quotation before you confirm. Premium paints typically add SGD 100 to SGD 200 to a whole-apartment job: a small premium for significantly better durability.

**Review volume and recency.** In the painting contractor market, a 4.9-star rating with 100 recent reviews is far more meaningful than 5.0 stars from 15 reviews. Check that reviews span the past 12 months and cover a range of property types. A sudden cluster of 5-star reviews posted over a short period is a common indicator of artificial review inflation. Established contractors with 200 or more verified reviews have demonstrated consistency across hundreds of client projects.

**Written quotation with scope detail.** A professional painting contractor provides a written quotation that specifies the areas to be painted (room by room), the paint brand and grade, the number of coats, whether primer is included, and the total price. Verbal quotes or lump-sum figures without detail are difficult to dispute if quality issues arise after completion. Always request and retain the written quotation.

**Defects and touch-up policy.** Reputable painting contractors offer a touch-up visit within 4 to 6 weeks of project completion to address any minor issues: small holidays in coverage, minor masking inconsistencies, or surface reactions that become visible after drying. Ask whether this is included before confirming and get it written into the agreement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to paint an HDB flat in Singapore?

A standard 4-room HDB full interior repaint costs SGD 800 to SGD 1,500 for basic emulsion paint. With premium paint brands such as Nippon Medifresh or Dulux Ambiance, expect SGD 1,200 to SGD 1,800. Feature wall painting or special finishes such as textured or metallic adds SGD 200 to SGD 500. Prices vary by contractor, surface condition, and the scope of surface preparation required.

### How long does it take to paint a Singapore HDB flat?

A 4-room HDB interior repaint typically takes one to two full working days for a professional team. This includes surface preparation, primer application on bare patches, and two coats of emulsion. Larger flats (5-room and executive) may take two to three days. Homes requiring extensive crack filling or skim coat work may add an extra half day.

### What paint brand is best for HDB flats in Singapore?

Nippon Paint and Dulux are the most widely recommended brands by professional painting contractors in Singapore. For washability and durability in kitchen and bathroom areas, Nippon Odour-less All-in-1 or Dulux Ambiance are popular choices. For bedrooms and living areas, standard premium emulsion grades provide good coverage and longevity at reasonable cost. Avoid the cheapest economy grades as they require more coats for coverage and show wear faster.

### Do I need to move furniture before painters arrive?

Most painting contractors ask clients to move portable furniture away from walls or to the centre of the room to allow perimeter access. Bulky items such as sofas and wardrobes are typically protected with drop cloths on-site by the painting team. It is advisable to remove fragile items and store valuables before work commences.

### What is included in a professional painting job in Singapore?

A professional painting job includes: surface inspection and minor crack/hole filling, sanding rough areas, masking of floors, skirting, and fixtures, primer application on bare patches, and two coats of the specified emulsion paint. Skim coat application for walls with significant damage is typically quoted separately. Always clarify what is included versus what is quoted as an add-on before confirming the job.

### How do I check if a painting contractor is legitimate in Singapore?

Request the contractor's business name and UEN (Unique Entity Number) and verify it on ACRA's BizFile portal. Check that the Google Business Profile has a verifiable address. Review the recency and variety of Google reviews. Request a written quotation on company letterhead. Legitimate painting contractors are straightforward about providing this information: evasiveness on any of these points is a red flag.

### How often should I repaint my HDB flat?

Most Singapore homeowners repaint their HDB flat every 5 to 8 years under normal living conditions. High-traffic areas such as kitchens, hallways, and bathrooms may show wear faster and benefit from repainting every 3 to 5 years. Using a higher-grade washable emulsion extends this interval. A fresh coat of paint is also recommended when preparing a flat for sale or rent as it significantly improves first impressions.

### Can I paint my HDB flat myself?

Minor touch-up painting is allowed as DIY for HDB flat owners. However, full interior repaints are typically far more time-consuming than most homeowners estimate: a 4-room flat takes a professional team one to two days. Surface preparation quality is also significantly harder to achieve without professional tools and experience. Most homeowners who attempt DIY repaints report unsatisfactory results on walls with existing cracks or imperfections. For whole-apartment repaints, engaging a professional contractor typically delivers better results at a lower total cost when labour and materials are factored in.

### What should I do if the paint job quality is poor?

Raise the issue with the contractor in writing immediately after observing defects, with photographs documenting the specific areas. Reputable contractors have a touch-up policy and will return to rectify genuine workmanship defects within the agreed defects liability period. If the contractor refuses to return, refer the dispute to the Consumers Association of Singapore (CASE) for mediation. Always retain the written quotation and any work order documentation.

### Is there a best time of year to paint in Singapore?

There is no ideal season for interior painting in Singapore due to the year-round tropical climate. However, avoid scheduling painting during the Northeast Monsoon season (November to January) if exterior painting is required, as heavy rain can interrupt work and affect adhesion. Interior painting can be done any time of year. Good ventilation during and after painting accelerates drying regardless of season.
