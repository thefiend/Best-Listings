## How We Ranked These Travel Agencies

Our evaluation of Singapore's best travel agencies used five weighted criteria. Google rating and review volume formed the primary signal, with agencies needing a minimum 4.4-star rating and at least 50 reviews to qualify. We weighted agencies with over 500 reviews significantly more heavily to distinguish proven long-term performers from those with limited data. Destination breadth was assessed by examining each agency's publicly available tour catalogue, giving higher scores to agencies covering multiple continents and offering a range of travel styles from group tours to independent packages. Customer support quality was evaluated through client review analysis, specifically looking for themes around responsiveness, disruption handling, and post-trip assistance. Value assessment compared package inclusions against typical direct booking costs for the same itinerary components. Licensing status was verified, with only Singapore Tourism Board-licensed agencies included. Agencies with documented complaints about misleading package terms or unresolved client disputes were excluded regardless of their overall rating.

## What to Look for When Hiring a Travel Agency

**Destination expertise versus general coverage.** A travel agency that handles everything from Bhutan to Bali may have shallower destination knowledge than a specialist. If you are planning a complex itinerary to a less common destination, consider agencies that demonstrate real depth in that specific region rather than listing it as one of 50 available destinations.

**Licensing and accreditation.** All legitimate Singapore travel agencies must hold a valid Singapore Tourism Board travel agent licence. NATAS (National Association of Travel Agents Singapore) membership provides an additional layer of consumer protection. Verify both before making any deposits, as unlicensed operators are not covered by consumer protection schemes.

**Package inclusions and exclusions.** Travel packages vary enormously in what is included. Ask explicitly about airport transfers, tipping requirements, single-supplement surcharges, meal inclusions, and whether guided tours or attraction entry fees are covered. The lowest headline price often has the most hidden costs once all exclusions are added back.

**After-sales support and disruption handling.** The most important test of a travel agency is how they respond when things go wrong. Ask prospective agencies directly about their emergency contact procedures, what happens if a flight is cancelled mid-trip, and how they have handled major disruptions for past clients. Agencies with documented strong review patterns for handling disruptions provide the most reliable assurance.

**Payment security and refund policies.** Understand how deposits and final payments are held, whether they are placed in protected accounts, and what the cancellation and refund policy is before signing. Travel insurance is almost always worth adding, and many agencies offer this as an optional or bundled service.

### Which is the best travel agency in Singapore for group tours?

For group tours, Chan Brothers Travel and EU Holidays are the top choices, both holding thousands of verified reviews and offering comprehensive group tour programs across Asia, Europe, and worldwide destinations. ### Are travel agencies worth using in Singapore compared to booking directly?

### What should I do if my travel agency goes out of business before my trip?

Bookings made through Singapore Tourism Board-licensed agencies have some consumer protection, but it does not guarantee full refunds. Always pay by credit card where possible as it provides additional chargeback protection. Travel insurance covering agency insolvency is also available and recommended for high-value bookings.
