Singapore's home cleaning market has expanded rapidly in recent years, driven by dual-income households, an ageing population, and a growing expectation that professional-grade cleanliness should be accessible rather than a luxury. The demand for home cleaning singapore has produced a large and fragmented industry ranging from one-person independent cleaners to fully digitalised platforms managing thousands of regular bookings per week. Navigating this market: where service quality, reliability, and pricing vary enormously: requires understanding both what you need and which type of provider is best placed to deliver it.

The main categories of home cleaning service singapore reflect the different states a home can be in and the different outcomes an occupant might need. Regular cleaning: typically scheduled weekly, fortnightly, or monthly: covers vacuuming, mopping, bathroom sanitisation, kitchen surface cleaning, and general tidying. This is the most commonly booked service and the one most households use to maintain a consistently clean living environment without dedicating their weekends to domestic chores. Spring cleaning is a more intensive, periodic service covering areas that regular cleaning does not reach: ceiling fans, window tracks, grout lines, inside kitchen cabinets, and appliance interiors. Post-renovation cleaning is a specialised service addressing the construction dust, adhesive residue, and surface staining that renovation work leaves behind: a particular necessity in Singapore's highly active property improvement market. Move-in and move-out cleaning are intensive one-time services designed to restore a property to a pristine standard, either for a new tenant moving in or an outgoing occupant fulfilling lease obligations.

The distinction between HDB flat cleaning and condo or landed property cleaning matters in Singapore's context. HDB flats: which house approximately 80% of Singapore's resident population: tend to have more standardised layouts, which makes it easier for cleaning companies to price accurately and schedule efficiently. Condominiums and landed properties introduce more variables: larger floor areas, more complex layouts, additional amenities such as private pools and outdoor terraces, and higher-specification finishes that may require different cleaning products or techniques. Some cleaning companies explicitly price and staff for one segment or the other; others cover all property types with the same team.

When choosing a home cleaning service singapore, the most important factors are verification of the cleaner's background and identity, consistency of assignment (whether you get the same cleaner each visit), insurance coverage for accidental damage, and transparency around pricing and cleaning supplies. Platforms that manage background-checked independent cleaners offer flexibility and competitive pricing; dedicated cleaning companies offer more control over standards and training. Both models have strong operators in this market, and the right choice depends on whether you prioritise price flexibility or operational consistency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does home cleaning cost in Singapore in 2026?**

Home cleaning costs in Singapore vary by service type, property size, and provider model. For regular part-time cleaning, the typical rate is SGD 20–30 per hour, meaning a 3-hour session for a standard 3-room HDB flat costs SGD 60–90, and a 4-hour session for a 4-room flat or small condo runs SGD 80–120. Platform-based services like Helpling price similarly, with rates influenced by the individual cleaner's rating and experience level. Spring cleaning: a more intensive service covering areas not addressed in regular cleaning: typically costs SGD 150–350 for an HDB flat depending on the number of rooms and the scope of the brief. Post-renovation cleaning is priced on property size and renovation scope, commonly ranging from SGD 300–600 for a standard HDB flat and SGD 500–1,200 for a larger condo or landed property. Move-in/move-out cleaning is similarly priced to post-renovation cleaning. Always confirm whether the quoted price includes cleaning supplies, as some services price on a bring-your-own-supplies basis.

**How often should I get my home cleaned?**

The appropriate frequency for home cleaning in Singapore depends on household size, lifestyle, and cleanliness standards. For a couple or single occupant in a 2-room or 3-room flat with no children or pets, a fortnightly cleaning session is typically sufficient to maintain a clean environment. For families with children, particularly households with young children who generate more mess, a weekly cleaning session is more practical. Households with pets: especially dogs that shed: generally benefit from weekly cleaning to manage hair and dander accumulation. Larger properties above 1,500 square feet typically require at least weekly cleaning to prevent dust and grime from accumulating in less-trafficked areas. Singapore's humid climate accelerates mould growth in bathrooms and kitchens, making regular cleaning especially important for bathroom grout, shower screens, and kitchen surfaces. A practical rule: if you notice the home feeling noticeably dirty within a week of a cleaning session, increase the frequency.

**What is the difference between regular cleaning and spring cleaning?**

Regular cleaning in Singapore covers the maintenance tasks required to keep a home hygienic and tidy between sessions: vacuuming and mopping floors, wiping kitchen surfaces and appliances externally, cleaning bathrooms including toilets, basins, and showers, emptying bins, and general tidying. It is designed to be completed in 3–4 hours for a standard HDB flat and is priced accordingly. Spring cleaning is a far more intensive, periodic service that addresses the areas regular cleaning does not reach: inside kitchen cabinets and the refrigerator, the interior of the oven and microwave, ceiling fan blades and housing, air-conditioning filter covers (external), window tracks and frames, door frames and skirting boards, grout scrubbing, mattress vacuuming, and the comprehensive cleaning of storage areas. A full spring clean typically takes 6–10 hours for a standard HDB flat and is usually booked once or twice a year: commonly before Chinese New Year, Hari Raya, Deepavali, or before a major family gathering. The cost difference reflects the time and labour involved: spring cleaning typically costs 3–5 times more than a standard session.

**Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?**

This depends on the cleaning company or platform you use. Some home cleaning services in Singapore operate on a bring-your-own-supplies basis, where the cleaner brings all equipment and products: vacuum cleaner, mops, cloths, cleaning agents, and disinfectants: and the cost of these supplies is factored into the session price. Others operate on a client-supplies basis, where the homeowner provides all cleaning products and the cleaner provides only labour; this model is common with part-time cleaning platforms and typically results in a lower hourly rate. A third model, used by some dedicated cleaning companies, involves the cleaner bringing specialist equipment such as steam cleaners or commercial-grade scrubbers while the homeowner provides basic household products. When booking a cleaning service singapore, always confirm the supplies arrangement in advance to avoid the cleaner arriving without the tools needed or the homeowner being charged separately for products they expected to be included. For post-renovation and spring cleaning services, cleaners should always bring their own specialist products, as these jobs require chemicals and equipment not typically found in a Singapore household.
