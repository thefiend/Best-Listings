## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes Bukit Timah a good area for preschools?

Bukit Timah is one of Singapore's most educationally dense residential areas, sitting within the catchment zones of several top-ranked primary schools and hosting a high concentration of purpose-built preschools with strong programme quality. The area's green corridor — encompassing Bukit Timah Nature Reserve, Dairy Farm, and the Central Catchment — makes it particularly attractive for nature-based and outdoor-learning preschools such as Adventure Tree, which uses the area's natural environment as an active pedagogical resource. The density of high-quality options in the childcare bukit timah market means families can compare curricula, visit multiple campuses, and make genuinely informed enrolment decisions.

### What age do children typically start preschool in Singapore?

Children in Singapore typically begin preschool at Nursery 1 (N1) at age 3, progressing through Nursery 2 (N2), Kindergarten 1 (K1), and Kindergarten 2 (K2) before entering Primary 1 at age 7. Infant care is available from 2 months and toddler programmes from 18 months at full-day childcare centres. ECDA recommends that quality early childhood education begins no later than age 3, with earlier high-quality care shown to benefit language development and school readiness in children from all family backgrounds.

### What are the main curriculum types available at Bukit Timah preschools?

The Bukit Timah area offers a broader range of preschool curriculum philosophies than most Singapore districts. Options include nature-based and forest school programmes (Adventure Tree), authentic Montessori (The Little House), global and inquiry-led international curricula (Odyssey), structured Canadian bilingual approaches (MapleBear), faith-integrated community programmes (Green Pastures), and Singapore NEL-aligned structured bilingual programmes (Superland, Learning Vision Hwa Chong). This concentration of pedagogically diverse operators means families can find genuine alignment with their values rather than defaulting to the nearest available option.

### How much does preschool in Bukit Timah cost?

Preschool fees in the Bukit Timah area vary considerably by curriculum type and operator. Community and NEL-aligned operators typically charge SGD 700–1,200 per month for full-day childcare before subsidies. Mid-premium operators with specialist programmes range from SGD 1,200–1,800 per month. Premium operators with distinctive curriculum approaches, specialist staffing, and purpose-built environments — including nature-based and international programmes — typically start at SGD 1,800 per month and can exceed SGD 2,500. Government childcare subsidies are available to Singapore citizens and permanent residents based on household income; families should check eligibility through the LifeSG portal before comparing net fees across operators.
