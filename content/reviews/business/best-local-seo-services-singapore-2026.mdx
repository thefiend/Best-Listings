## What to Look for When Hiring a Local SEO Service

Choosing a local SEO provider in Singapore requires evaluating five specific capabilities beyond general digital marketing credentials. First, GBP expertise: ask the agency to walk through exactly how they will optimise your Google Business Profile, including category selection, attribute completion, post cadence, Q&A management, and photo strategy. Agencies that cannot explain these in detail are likely to do surface-level work. Second, citation audit capability: the agency should be able to identify every existing citation for your business, flag NAP inconsistencies, and correct them systematically across Singapore directories including Yellow Pages, Straits Times directories, and local niche platforms. Third, review generation strategy: review velocity is one of the strongest local ranking signals in Singapore; the agency should have a documented process for increasing review rate without violating Google's guidelines. Fourth, local schema implementation: your business website should have correct LocalBusiness schema markup that matches your GBP exactly; agencies that do not handle website on-page work as part of local SEO are missing a critical signal layer. Fifth, measurable reporting tied to business outcomes: local pack position tracking, GBP insight reports covering call clicks, direction requests, and website visits, and a clear attribution model that connects local SEO activity to revenue.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is local SEO and why does it matter for Singapore businesses?

Local SEO is the process of optimising a business's online presence to appear in Google's local search results, specifically the local pack (the three businesses shown on the map at the top of local search results) and the organic listings below it. For Singapore businesses that rely on local customers, whether through walk-in traffic, service-area enquiries, or phone calls, appearing in the local pack for relevant search terms is one of the highest-ROI digital investments available. Studies consistently show that local pack listings receive 42% of all clicks on local search result pages, with a strong bias towards the first and second positions. In Singapore's competitive market, where consumers routinely search for services near their location before purchasing, local SEO directly influences phone call volume, foot traffic, and service enquiries.

### How long does it take to see results from local SEO in Singapore?

Most Singapore businesses see meaningful local pack movement within 60 to 90 days of a properly executed local SEO campaign, though the timeline depends on how competitive the category is and how much optimisation work needs to be done from the starting state. Businesses with no existing GBP, inconsistent citations, and zero reviews will take longer to build authority than businesses with an established GBP that needs targeted improvements. In highly competitive categories such as dentistry, legal services, or financial advisory in the central districts, achieving and maintaining top 3 local pack positions may take four to six months of sustained work. Be sceptical of agencies that promise first-page local pack rankings within two to four weeks: sustainable local rankings are built over months, not days.

### What is Google Business Profile and how does it affect local rankings?

Google Business Profile (GBP), formerly Google My Business, is the free business listing that controls how your business appears on Google Maps and in local search results. It is the single most influential factor in local SEO for Singapore businesses. A fully optimised GBP includes correct business category and subcategories, complete service descriptions with local keywords, a comprehensive attribute set, a high volume of recent photos, an active post cadence, and a strong review count with a high average rating. Google uses GBP completeness signals alongside proximity, relevance, and prominence to determine local pack rankings. Businesses with incomplete or inconsistently maintained GBPs rank below competitors with identical services and better-optimised profiles: a gap that a competent local SEO agency can close systematically.

### What is NAP consistency and why does it affect local SEO?

NAP stands for Name, Address, and Phone number: the three pieces of business information that must be identical across every online mention of your business, including your website, GBP, social media profiles, and business directories. Google uses NAP consistency as a trust signal: when a business's name, address, and phone number are consistent across dozens of citation sources, Google gains confidence in the accuracy of the information and ranks the business higher in local results. Conversely, inconsistent NAP data, such as different phone numbers on different directories, variations in business name formatting, or outdated addresses, reduces Google's confidence and suppresses local rankings. A citation audit and NAP correction exercise is one of the first steps any competent local SEO agency should perform before any other optimisation work begins.

### Should I prioritise local SEO or Google Ads for local visibility in Singapore?

For most Singapore businesses, local SEO should be prioritised over Google Ads for local visibility, because the economics favour it strongly over a 12-month horizon. Google Ads for local terms in Singapore typically costs SGD 2 to SGD 8 per click in moderate competition categories, with costs as high as SGD 15 to SGD 30 per click in high-competition verticals such as legal, financial, and medical services. A local pack ranking achieved through local SEO delivers clicks at zero marginal cost indefinitely, and the phone calls and direction requests from GBP cost nothing per engagement. The investment in local SEO typically pays back within six to nine months for businesses in moderate competition categories. Ads remain valuable for immediate visibility during the period before local SEO rankings are established, and for targeting terms too competitive for organic local inclusion, but as a long-term channel, local SEO delivers materially better ROI for most Singapore SMEs.

### What are the most important local ranking factors for Singapore businesses in 2026?

Google's local ranking algorithm considers three primary categories: relevance (how well your GBP and website match the searcher's query), proximity (how close your business is to the searcher's location), and prominence (how well-known and trusted your business is online). The most actionable factors within these categories for Singapore businesses in 2026 are: GBP completeness and accuracy (primary business category, complete services section, attributes); review quantity and recent velocity (a steady flow of new reviews outperforms a large static count); citation consistency (NAP matching across Singapore directories); website on-page local signals (LocalBusiness schema, localised keyword mentions, service area pages); and behavioural signals such as click-through rate from search results and call click rate from the GBP. Proximity is a factor agencies cannot control, but all other categories are directly optimisable with a structured local SEO programme.
