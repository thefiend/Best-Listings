## What to Look for When Hiring a Frosted Glass Contractor in Singapore

**Google reviews and recency.** A 4.5-star rating with 30 or more reviews is the baseline for a credible frosted glass contractor in Singapore. More important than the aggregate score is recency: a contractor with 100 five-star reviews but nothing posted in the past 3 months may have changed quality or staffing. Check that recent reviews mention the same types of work you need, shower screens, partitions, or decorative panels, not just generic praise.

**Scope match for your project.** Frosted glass contractors range from residential-only specialists handling shower screens and privacy panels, to full engineering firms capable of structural glass walls and commercial partitions. Match the contractor's documented capability to your specific requirement before enquiring. A contractor who primarily does residential shower screens may not have the tools or experience for a large commercial office partition system.

**Glass type and specification clarity.** Reputable contractors specify the glass type in their quotations: tempered or toughened glass, laminated glass, or decorative frosted panels. Tempered glass is the safety standard for shower screens and any glass that could be impacted, and a contractor who does not specify this unprompted may be cutting costs on safety-critical material. Ask directly what glass type and thickness is included in the price.

**Pricing transparency.** Legitimate frosted glass contractors provide written quotations with itemised costs covering glass, frame or hardware, and labour separately. Verbal quotes or single-line lump-sum pricing make it impossible to compare contractors accurately and often lead to scope disputes at the point of invoicing. Request an itemised written quote before agreeing to any work.

**Installation method and hardware quality.** For frameless glass systems, the quality of the clamps, hinges, and sealing is as important as the glass itself. Ask what hardware brand is used and whether it carries a warranty. For framed systems, ask about the aluminium grade and finish. Contractors who cannot answer these questions are unlikely to be sourcing quality materials.

**Licensing and BCA registration.** For glass works that form part of a structural renovation, the contractor must have the appropriate BCA licensing. For standard residential frosted glass installation, formal licensing is not required, but checking that the business has a registered address and a verifiable company registration number (UEN) provides basic accountability. Avoid contractors who operate only via messaging apps with no physical address or business registration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does frosted glass cost in Singapore?

Frosted glass panels in Singapore typically cost SGD 80 to SGD 200 per square metre for standard residential applications including shower screens and privacy panels. Tempered frosted glass, the safety standard for wet areas and impact-risk zones, costs SGD 120 to SGD 250 per square metre installed. Commercial glass partitions with aluminium framing start at SGD 150 to SGD 300 per square metre depending on partition height, frame type, and hardware specification. Custom or decorative frosted designs with etching or patterning cost more.

### What is the difference between frosted glass and sandblasted glass?

Frosted glass is produced by acid-etching or applying a frosted film to clear glass, creating a translucent surface that diffuses light while maintaining privacy. Sandblasted glass achieves a similar visual effect through physical abrasion of the glass surface, and is generally more durable and scratch-resistant than acid-etched frosting. Both are used for shower screens, partitions, and decorative applications. Frosted film is a third option, applied to existing glass, less permanent but more cost-effective for temporary or renter-friendly applications.

### What questions should I ask before hiring a frosted glass contractor?

Ask: What type of glass do you use and what is the thickness? Is it tempered or toughened for safety-critical applications? Can you provide an itemised written quotation? What hardware brand are you using for hinges and clamps? Do you handle measurement, fabrication, and installation yourselves or subcontract? What is the installation timeline from confirmed order to completion? Is there a defects liability period on the workmanship?

### Is frosted glass safe for shower screens in Singapore?

Yes, but only if tempered (toughened) glass is used. Under Singapore's building codes, glass used in wet areas, including shower screens and glass doors, must be tempered to reduce the risk of shattering into dangerous shards on impact. Any contractor quoting a shower screen who does not specify tempered glass should be asked directly, and any contractor who confirms they use non-tempered glass should be avoided. Tempered glass, when it fails, breaks into small blunt fragments rather than sharp shards.

### Can frosted glass be retrofitted to existing windows in Singapore?

Yes. Frosted glass film can be applied to existing clear glass windows without replacing the glass, at a lower cost than full glass replacement. The film is applied directly to the glass surface and achieves a similar privacy and light-diffusion effect. It is less durable than etched or tempered frosted glass and can be peeled off, making it popular for renters. For permanent applications, full glass replacement with etched or tempered frosted glass is recommended.

### Does frosted glass reduce visibility completely?

No. Frosted glass is translucent, not opaque. It diffuses light and obscures details, providing privacy while still allowing light to pass through. For complete visual separation, frosted glass laminated with an opaque interlayer, or a solid partition with a frosted glass highlight panel, is the more appropriate specification.
