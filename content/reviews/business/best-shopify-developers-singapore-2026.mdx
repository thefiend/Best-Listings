## What to Look for When Hiring a Shopify Developer

**Platform expertise, not just web development.** Many general web agencies list Shopify as a service without genuinely understanding the platform. Ask specifically: how many Shopify stores have they launched in Singapore? Can they show live store URLs? Do they understand Shopify's Liquid templating language, or do they install purchased themes without customisation? An agency that truly knows Shopify will be able to answer questions about metafields, app integration strategy, checkout extension limitations, and the difference between Shopify and Shopify Plus features without hesitation.

**SEO built in from the start.** Shopify has known structural limitations for SEO: duplicate URL patterns, collection-product canonical challenges, and default URL structures that require correct configuration. A Shopify developer who does not address these at the build stage will hand over a store that ranks poorly and requires expensive remediation. Ask your developer specifically how they configure canonical URLs, handle product-in-collection URL structures, and set up schema markup for product and review data before you agree to the build.

**Mobile checkout performance.** More than 70% of Singapore e-commerce traffic originates from mobile devices. A Shopify store that loads in four seconds on a mobile connection loses a significant proportion of its visitors before the product page fully renders. Ask any developer you are considering for Core Web Vitals scores from stores they have built. If they cannot produce this information, treat it as a signal that performance is not part of their build process.

**Local payment integration.** Singapore consumers expect PayNow and local bank transfer options alongside international cards. A Shopify developer who has not integrated Singapore-specific payment methods before will require additional scoping time and may miss the correct implementation. Confirm that local payment setup is included in the quoted scope before signing any agreement.

**Post-launch support terms.** Shopify stores always require adjustments after launch: product images that need optimisation, payment flows that need testing across different mobile browsers, or shipping zone configurations that require refinement as order geography expands. Confirm in writing what post-launch support is included in the build engagement, how long it lasts, and what the hourly or retainer rate is for ongoing modifications after the support window closes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a Shopify store cost in Singapore in 2026?

Shopify store development costs in Singapore in 2026 vary by complexity. A basic store using a customised premium theme with up to 50 products, payment integration, and standard shipping configuration typically costs between SGD 3,500 and SGD 6,000. A mid-tier store with a partially custom theme, product filtering, loyalty app integration, and multi-currency support ranges from SGD 6,000 to SGD 12,000. A fully custom Shopify theme with bespoke Liquid coding, complex variant logic, ERP or inventory system integration, and multi-storefront configuration can reach SGD 15,000 to SGD 25,000. Shopify Plus projects with custom checkout scripting and B2B wholesale features typically start from SGD 20,000. Shopify subscription costs are separate: SGD 39 to SGD 399 per month depending on plan, excluding apps and transaction fees.

### What is the difference between a Shopify developer and a web designer?

A Shopify developer has specific knowledge of Shopify's Liquid templating system, the Storefront API, Shopify app ecosystem, checkout customisation rules, and the platform's structural constraints and SEO implications. A general web designer may be able to install and configure a Shopify theme, but cannot write custom Liquid code, build bespoke app integrations, or properly address Shopify's canonical URL and duplicate content challenges. For stores beyond the most basic template configuration, the difference in technical depth is commercially significant: a developer-led build will perform better, scale more reliably, and require fewer emergency fixes after launch.

### Should I use Shopify or WooCommerce for my Singapore business?

Shopify is the better choice for most Singapore merchants who want a managed platform with predictable hosting, security, and uptime. It is faster to launch, requires no server management, and has a large ecosystem of apps covering local payment methods, loyalty programmes, and inventory management. WooCommerce is worth considering if you need deep WordPress integration, own a large existing WordPress content site, or require a customisation level that Shopify's architecture cannot support cost-effectively. WooCommerce requires more ongoing technical maintenance and hosting management, which adds operational overhead. For most Singapore SMEs and DTC brands launching or migrating their first serious e-commerce store, Shopify is the lower-risk and lower total-cost-of-ownership platform.

### How long does it take to build a Shopify store in Singapore?

A standard Shopify store build with a customised theme, up to 100 products, payment integration, and basic SEO setup takes four to eight weeks from briefing to launch. Timeline depends heavily on how quickly the merchant provides product photography, copy, and brand assets: content delays are the most common cause of Shopify projects running over schedule. A fully custom Shopify theme built from scratch requires eight to twelve weeks. Shopify Plus builds with custom checkout and third-party ERP integration typically require ten to sixteen weeks. Agencies quoting complete builds in under two weeks for anything beyond a single product landing page should be asked specifically what is being abbreviated to meet that timeline.

### What Shopify apps do Singapore stores typically need?

The most commonly integrated Shopify apps for Singapore stores include: a local payment gateway app for PayNow and PayLah integration, a product reviews app for social proof, a loyalty and rewards programme app, an email marketing app integrated with Shopify customer data, a shipping and fulfilment app supporting Singapore courier providers (J&T, NinjaVan, Lalamove), and a Google Shopping feed app for product listing ads. The correct app selection depends on the store's product type, average order value, and marketing strategy. An experienced Shopify developer will recommend a minimal app stack that covers core requirements without creating performance drag from excessive app scripts on every page load.

### How do I improve my Shopify store's Google rankings?

Shopify stores rank well organically when four elements are configured correctly: product and collection page meta titles and descriptions written with keyword intent in mind; canonical URLs set to avoid product-in-collection URL duplication; schema markup for Product, Offer, and Review data to qualify for Google rich results; and Core Web Vitals scores (particularly Largest Contentful Paint and Cumulative Layout Shift) within Google's recommended thresholds. Additionally, Shopify stores benefit from a content marketing layer: blog posts and buying guides targeting informational keywords that bring in shoppers at an earlier stage of the purchase journey. Merchants building a new Shopify store should confirm that all four technical elements are part of the build scope before committing to any developer.

### Can a Shopify developer help with Google Shopping and paid ads?

Yes, and the best ones integrate paid media strategy into the Shopify build rather than treating it as a separate service. Google Shopping campaigns require a correctly configured product feed with accurate titles, descriptions, pricing, and inventory status: all sourced from the Shopify product data. A Shopify developer who also manages paid media can ensure the feed is optimised at the data source rather than corrected downstream in Google Merchant Center. For Singapore merchants planning to invest SGD 2,000-plus per month in paid shopping campaigns, having the developer and the campaign manager in the same agency removes a coordination risk that commonly causes feed errors and campaign underperformance.

### What is Shopify Plus and when does a Singapore business need it?

Shopify Plus is Shopify's enterprise tier, typically appropriate for Singapore merchants generating SGD 1 million or more in annual store revenue, or those needing features unavailable on standard Shopify plans: custom checkout scripting, checkout extensibility, B2B wholesale storefronts, multi-storefront management (separate stores for different markets from one admin), advanced automation via Shopify Flow, and a dedicated merchant success manager. Shopify Plus pricing starts at approximately USD 2,300 per month. For most Singapore merchants below the SGD 1 million revenue threshold, the advanced Shopify plans (Basic, Shopify, Advanced) at SGD 39 to SGD 399 per month provide sufficient functionality for growth. A Shopify developer can advise on the correct plan based on current and projected order volume and the specific features required.
