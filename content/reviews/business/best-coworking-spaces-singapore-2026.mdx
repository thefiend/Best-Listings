## How We Ranked These Coworking Spaces

We ranked each coworking space using Google Maps rating and review volume as primary indicators of member experience, then assessed location accessibility by MRT proximity, breadth of workspace product (hot desk, dedicated desk, private office, meeting room), internet infrastructure quality, amenity standards, pricing transparency, and the quality of the community environment. ## What to Look for When Hiring a Coworking Space in Singapore

**Location and commute practicality.** The best coworking space is the one you will actually use consistently. A premium CBD space that requires 45 minutes of commuting each way delivers less value than a 4.8-rated space within walking distance of your home. Before committing to a membership, calculate the realistic commute time and cost against the frequency of your intended use.

**Internet reliability.** Internet quality is the single most important infrastructure element for knowledge workers. Ask operators for their internet specification (dedicated fibre vs shared broadband), whether download and upload speeds are symmetric, and whether backup connectivity exists for outages. The best way to validate this is through a day pass trial during a busy period: infrastructure failures that reviewers have mentioned will typically appear in the Google review summary.

**Meeting room availability and booking.** Hot desk memberships that include meeting room credits are common, but credit allocations vary widely: from 2 hours per month to 10 hours per month at the same headline price. Understand exactly what meeting room time is included in your membership, what the cost is for overages, and how far in advance meeting rooms typically need to be booked at peak times.

**Community quality and composition.** For solo workers and founders, the community dimension of coworking is often the primary value driver beyond basic infrastructure. Spaces that invest in community management, events, and member introductions deliver meaningfully better outcomes than those that operate as transactional hot desk providers. Ask operators about their member events programme and request an introduction to two or three existing members before committing.

**Flexibility and exit terms.** Singapore's coworking market is highly competitive and most operators offer month-to-month flexibility at a small premium over longer commitments. Avoid spaces that require 3-month or 6-month minimum commitments without a genuine discount justification, and always review the exit terms in your membership agreement before signing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between a hot desk, dedicated desk, and private office?

A hot desk is an unassigned workspace where you choose any available desk on arrival: suitable for members who visit infrequently or prefer variety in their seating. A dedicated desk is a permanently assigned desk where you can leave personal belongings and set up your monitor: suitable for daily users who need consistency. A private office is a fully enclosed space for 1–20 people, providing visual and acoustic privacy for teams requiring confidential conversations or focused deep work.

### What are typical coworking prices in Singapore?

Hot desk day passes typically cost SGD 20–50 depending on space quality and location. Monthly hot desk memberships range from SGD 200–700. Dedicated desk monthly memberships range from SGD 400–900. Private offices for 2–4 people range from SGD 1,200–3,500 per month. CBD locations command a 30–50% premium over comparable suburban spaces. Most operators offer discounts of 10–20% for 3-month or longer commitments.

### Can I use my CPF to pay for coworking in Singapore?

No. CPF funds cannot be used to pay for coworking memberships. Coworking fees are operational business expenses payable from corporate or personal cash flow. Businesses registered in Singapore may deduct coworking fees as a business expense for corporate income tax purposes, subject to the applicable conditions under IRAS guidelines.

### Are coworking spaces good for freelancers in Singapore?

Coworking spaces are well-suited to freelancers for several reasons: they provide a professional environment separate from home distractions, enable use of a business address on invoices and correspondence, offer meeting rooms for client meetings, and provide the social infrastructure that reduces the isolation experienced by solo remote workers. Freelancers should prioritise spaces with month-to-month memberships, reliable internet, and community programming that generates referral business opportunities.

### What amenities should I expect at a coworking space in Singapore?

Standard amenities include high-speed internet, printing facilities, air conditioning, pantry with coffee and tea, lockable storage, and phone booths or quiet zones. Premium spaces additionally offer dedicated meeting rooms with AV equipment, standing desks, shower facilities, mail handling, a dedicated business address, and reception services. Always verify the specific amenity package against your requirements before committing: amenity descriptions vary significantly between operators.

### What are the best areas for coworking in Singapore?

The CBD (Raffles Place, Tanjong Pagar, Marina Bay) offers prestigious addresses and proximity to financial and professional services clients but commands premium pricing. Orchard and Somerset are highly accessible, centrally located, and suit client-facing professionals. Paya Lebar and the East corridor are increasingly popular for East Singapore residents with modern developments and good MRT access. Ang Mo Kio, Hougang, and north Singapore neighbourhood spaces offer quality coworking without the CBD commute for residents of these areas.

### How do I book a meeting room at a coworking space?

Most coworking spaces offer meeting room booking through their member portal or mobile app, typically showing real-time availability across all rooms. Meeting room credits are usually included in monthly membership packages: unused credits typically expire at month end. Day pass visitors can usually book meeting rooms on arrival at an hourly rate. For important client meetings, book meeting rooms at least 2–3 days in advance during business hours.

### Is coworking safe for confidential work?

Coworking spaces involve shared environments where conversations may be overheard. For highly confidential work: legal matters, financial negotiations, M&A discussions: use private offices or fully enclosed meeting rooms rather than open hot desks. Most quality coworking spaces have phone booths and soundproofed meeting rooms suitable for sensitive calls. For document security, use a dedicated locker and avoid printing confidential documents to shared printers without supervising the output tray.

### Do coworking spaces in Singapore offer day passes?

Yes. Most coworking spaces in Singapore offer day passes for visitors who want to trial the space or work from a particular location without a membership commitment. Day passes typically cost SGD 20–50 and include a hot desk, WiFi access, and use of common amenities. Some spaces offer half-day passes at lower rates. Day pass availability makes trialling multiple spaces before committing to a monthly membership straightforward and cost-effective.
