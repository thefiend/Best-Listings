## What to Look for When Choosing a Skin Clinic in Singapore

**Doctor credentials and MOH registration.** In Singapore, only doctors registered with the Singapore Medical Council (SMC) may perform aesthetic procedures involving lasers, injectables, and prescription medications. Before booking, verify that the clinic's doctors are SMC-registered. Dermatologists hold an additional specialist credential from the Specialist Accreditation Board. For complex conditions like psoriasis, eczema, or skin cancer, a board-certified dermatologist is the appropriate level of care.

**Google reviews, volume, and recency.** A 4.8+ rating with at least 100 reviews is a reliable baseline for a Singapore skin clinic. Volume matters as much as the average: a clinic with 900 reviews at 4.9 is more credible than one with 20 reviews at 5.0. Also check recency, a clinic with no new reviews in the past three months may have changed staff or reduced operating standards.

**Treatment breadth versus specialisation.** Some patients need a full-service aesthetic clinic covering laser, injectables, body, and face. Others need a specialist for a specific condition. Match the clinic's focus to your actual need. A clinic with exceptional acne scar results may not be the best choice for chronic eczema management, and vice versa.

**Pricing transparency and consultation ethics.** Reputable Singapore skin clinics provide a consultation before recommending treatments and give an itemised quote without obligation to commit. Clinics that push you to sign a package during the first visit, before a proper assessment, are a yellow flag. Expect to pay SGD 80–150 for a consultation at a medical aesthetic clinic and SGD 150–500+ for individual treatment sessions.

**Location and accessibility.** Singapore's MRT network makes cross-island travel manageable, but for a multi-session treatment course spanning 3–6 months, proximity matters. A clinic 30 minutes from your home or office is more likely to see you completing your full course than one requiring a commute.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a skin clinic cost in Singapore?

A medical aesthetic consultation in Singapore typically costs SGD 80–150. Individual laser treatments such as Pico laser or fractional CO2 range from SGD 200–600 per session. Acne scar treatment packages, which usually involve 4–6 sessions, run SGD 1,200–3,500 depending on severity and modality. Botox starts around SGD 300–600 per area and fillers from SGD 700–1,200 per syringe.

### What is the difference between an aesthetic clinic and a dermatology clinic in Singapore?

An aesthetic clinic is staffed by general practitioners who have undergone training in cosmetic procedures, including lasers, injectables, and skin-boosting treatments. A dermatology clinic is led by a specialist dermatologist with additional postgraduate training and specialist accreditation from Singapore's Specialist Accreditation Board. Dermatologists can diagnose and treat medical skin conditions including eczema, psoriasis, skin cancers, and rosacea. For purely cosmetic concerns, a reputable aesthetic clinic is sufficient. For medical or chronic skin conditions, a dermatologist is the appropriate choice.

### How do I choose a good skin clinic in Singapore?

Check Google reviews (minimum 4.7 stars, minimum 100 reviews). Verify the doctor is SMC-registered. Confirm the clinic performs a proper skin assessment before recommending treatments. Ask for an itemised quote before committing to any package. A good clinic will give you realistic expectations and a clear timeline, not promise instant results or pressure you into multi-session packages at the first appointment.

### Are skin clinics in Singapore regulated?

Yes. The Ministry of Health (MOH) regulates medical aesthetic clinics in Singapore. Only SMC-registered doctors may administer prescription treatments, lasers, and injectables. Since 2008, MOH has enforced guidelines restricting certain aesthetic procedures to doctors with specific training. Clinics operating outside these guidelines risk licence suspension. Always verify a clinic's registration before booking.

### What are the most common treatments at skin clinics in Singapore?

The most common treatments at Singapore skin clinics include: Pico laser for pigmentation and brightening, fractional CO2 laser for acne scar resurfacing, Botox for fine lines and jaw slimming, hyaluronic acid fillers for volume restoration, HIFU and Ultherapy for skin tightening, chemical peels for exfoliation and tone correction, and skin boosters such as Juvederm Volite for hydration. Medical dermatology services include acne management, eczema and psoriasis treatment, mole and skin tag removal, and skin cancer diagnosis.

### How many sessions are needed for acne scar treatment?

Most acne scar treatment protocols in Singapore require 4–6 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart. Mild scarring may show visible improvement after 3 sessions. Severe ice-pick or boxcar scarring typically requires a combination of fractional laser, subcision, and filler, and may take 6–10 sessions over 9–12 months.
