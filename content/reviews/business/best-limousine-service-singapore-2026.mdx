## What to Look for When Hiring a Limousine Service in Singapore

**Fleet documentation with specific vehicle makes and models.** A legitimate limousine service can provide its fleet list with specific vehicle makes, models, and registration details on request. Operators who describe vehicles vaguely as "luxury cars" or "executive vehicles" without naming makes and models are typically using third-party hires with no direct quality control. Always ask for the specific vehicle before confirming the booking.

**LTA vocational licence confirmation.** All professional drivers providing hire-car services in Singapore must hold a valid Vocational Licence (VC) issued by the Land Transport Authority. Operators who cannot confirm their drivers' VC status are operating outside LTA regulation. This is particularly important for corporate clients with duty-of-care obligations to travelling employees and visiting executives.

**Fixed-rate pricing before booking confirmation.** Reputable limousine services provide a fixed rate per route or confirmed hourly rate before dispatching the vehicle. Variable pricing, meter-based billing, or "call for a quote" responses with no published rates should prompt you to request written confirmation before the driver is sent. This protects against on-arrival price changes that are a documented problem with lower-tier operators.

**Punctuality record from verified reviews.** Read recent reviews specifically for comments on punctuality rather than overall rating alone. A service with a 4.9 average but three recent reviews mentioning late arrivals for airport runs is a more significant concern than the headline score suggests. Limousine service punctuality is non-negotiable for airport transfers and timed formal events.

**Airport transfer protocol.** For Changi Airport pickups, confirm whether the driver tracks flight arrivals in real time, where the designated waiting location is, how waiting time is charged if there are delays, and whether the service includes a meet-and-greet with a name card in the arrivals hall. The answers to these questions separate a professional limousine service from a rebranded cab booking.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a limousine service cost in Singapore?
A standard airport transfer from Changi to a Singapore city hotel costs SGD 45–90 depending on vehicle class. Hourly limousine hire rates range from SGD 80–200 per hour depending on vehicle type and minimum hire duration. Full-day disposals (8–10 hours) typically cost SGD 500–1,200. Wedding limousine packages for a single vehicle typically range from SGD 350–800 for a half-day engagement. Premium vehicles such as Bentley or Rolls-Royce command significantly higher rates and should be quoted directly.

### What is the difference between a limousine service and a taxi in Singapore?
A limousine service provides a fixed-rate, pre-booked vehicle with a professionally presented driver assigned specifically to your booking. Taxis use metered pricing and are available on demand without pre-booking. Limousine services include additional features such as flight tracking for airport transfers, meet-and-greet service, longer complimentary wait times, and consistent vehicle quality. For corporate engagements, events, or VIP arrivals, the gap in presentation and reliability between a dedicated limousine service and a taxi is significant.

### Are limousine services in Singapore licensed and regulated?
