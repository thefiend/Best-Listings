## What to Look for When Hiring a Yacht Charter

**Vessel size and capacity matching your group.** Yacht charters specify a maximum safe capacity, which is a hard limit, not a guideline. Overcrowded vessels are both illegal and unsafe. Provide your exact headcount to operators and confirm the vessel's certified passenger capacity is appropriate. For comfort, budget for a vessel with 20 to 30 percent headroom above your group size.

**MPA certification and crew licensing.** All vessels operating commercially in Singapore waters must hold valid Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) certificates of fitness and safety inspection. Ask operators directly for their vessel certification status and confirm that the captain holds an appropriate MPA licence for the vessel class and charter waters.

**Charter duration and minimum booking requirements.** Most Singapore yacht charter operators set minimum booking durations of two to four hours. Clarify whether your intended charter duration meets the minimum and understand how overtime charges apply if your event runs longer than booked. Budget carefully for fuel surcharges, which may be additional to the base rate.

**Catering and beverage arrangements.** Some operators provide all-inclusive catering packages while others require you to supply provisions or work with approved caterers. Confirm whether the vessel has cooking facilities, ice storage, and suitable serving infrastructure for your food plan. For evening events, check whether the vessel has adequate indoor and deck lighting.

**Weather contingency and cancellation policy.** Singapore's tropical weather can produce sudden afternoon storms. Understand the operator's cancellation and rescheduling policy for weather-related issues, and confirm whether they offer a full refund or reschedule option if conditions make sailing unsafe. Purchase comprehensive travel insurance for any high-value charter booking.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a yacht charter cost in Singapore?

Yacht charter prices in Singapore typically range from $300 to $600 per hour for smaller vessels (20 to 30 pax) and $800 to $2,000 per hour for larger yachts (50 to 100 pax).
