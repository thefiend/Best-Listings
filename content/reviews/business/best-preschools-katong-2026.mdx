### How do I choose between the many preschools in the Katong area?

Begin by clarifying your priorities: academic preparation, bilingual development, outdoor and creative learning, faith integration, or working-parent convenience. Then verify ECDA licence and SPARK certification status for each shortlisted centre on the ECDA public register, and visit campuses while they are operating to observe whether the environment matches what the promotional materials describe. Ask specifically about teacher qualifications, average staff tenure at that campus, daily Mandarin instruction time, and how the centre communicates with parents about developmental progress. Review counts and ratings on Google are useful starting signals, but a campus visit and direct conversation with the centre principal will tell you more than any online indicator.

### What is the typical fee range for preschools in Katong?

Fees at Katong preschool singapore operators range from approximately SGD 800 per month at community-oriented centres through to SGD 2,000 or more per month at premium specialist operators. Families eligible for government childcare subsidies can reduce net fees materially: the ECDA subsidy calculator on the LifeSG portal provides household-specific estimates.

### Is Adventure Tree Preschool Katong suitable for children who are not yet school age?

Adventure Tree's Katong campus accepts children from 18 months, with age-appropriate programmes designed for toddlers through to Kindergarten 2 (age 6).
