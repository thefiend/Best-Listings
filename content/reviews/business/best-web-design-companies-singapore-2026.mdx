### How much does web design cost in Singapore in 2026?

Web design costs in Singapore in 2026 vary considerably by project scope. Template-based SME websites using WordPress or similar CMS platforms typically range from SGD 2,000 to SGD 5,000. Custom-designed corporate sites with bespoke UI/UX, CMS integration, and SEO foundations range from SGD 5,000 to SGD 15,000. Complex e-commerce platforms with payment gateway integration, inventory management, and custom product filtering start from SGD 10,000 and can reach SGD 30,000-plus for full custom builds. Agencies quoting below SGD 1,500 for a complete website are typically using pre-built themes with minimal customisation and no SEO structure. For businesses investing in a site as a lead-generation asset, the total cost of ownership: including post-launch SEO and maintenance: is the more meaningful number than the initial build cost alone.

### How long does it take to build a website in Singapore?

A standard SME corporate website takes four to eight weeks from briefing to launch with a competent web design company. This assumes the client provides content (text and images) on schedule: content delays are the most common reason projects run over timeline. E-commerce builds with multiple product categories, payment integration, and shipping configuration typically require eight to twelve weeks. Enterprise projects with custom CMS development, third-party API integrations, or multilingual requirements can extend to three to six months. Agencies promising complete websites in under two weeks for anything beyond a single landing page should be asked specifically what is being cut to meet that timeline.

### What should I look for when hiring a web design company in Singapore?

Five factors matter most: First, a portfolio with real client work across your industry or adjacent sectors: a company that has built sites for professional services or e-commerce businesses similar to yours understands the specific requirements. Second, evidence of SEO integration: ask whether on-page SEO, meta structure, and Core Web Vitals are part of the build or a separate engagement. Third, a defined post-launch support period: sites always require adjustments after launch and the support terms should be contractually specified. Fourth, verified Google reviews with genuine client names and project details rather than generic five-star feedback. Fifth, a clear project management process with milestone sign-offs and content deadlines communicated before work begins.

### WordPress, custom build, or SaaS platform: which is right for my Singapore business?

WordPress remains the correct choice for most Singapore SMEs and corporate sites: it is well-supported locally, SEO-friendly with correct configuration, and the largest ecosystem of developers means future modifications are never constrained by a proprietary platform. Custom builds make sense when business requirements exceed what CMS platforms can handle: complex web applications, marketplace features, or deeply customised user experiences. SaaS platforms like Shopify are strong for straightforward e-commerce where speed to market and built-in payment infrastructure outweigh the cost of customisation limitations. Avoid proprietary CMS platforms built and owned by the agency: if you ever switch providers, you may lose access to or control of your own site. Always confirm that the platform your agency proposes allows you to take the site with you if the relationship ends.
