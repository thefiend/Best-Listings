## What to Look for When Hiring an MCST Maintenance Contractor in Singapore

**BCA registration and relevant licences.** Any contractor performing structural, M&E, or civil works for an MCST must hold the appropriate BCA registration under the relevant workhead. Electrical works require a licensed electrical worker under the Energy Market Authority. Contractors who cannot produce a valid registration number or current licence should be disqualified regardless of price or review count.

**Service scope match.** MCST maintenance covers a wide range of works from routine repainting and plumbing to structural repairs and waterproofing. Identify whether your property's primary maintenance needs are routine multi-trade, M&E-focused, waterproofing-heavy, or structural before selecting a contractor. A contractor who excels at routine maintenance may not have the structural engineering capability needed for R.C. repairs on your carpark deck.

**Google reviews and recency.** A 4.5-star Google rating with 50 or more reviews is the credibility baseline for MCST maintenance contractors. More important than the aggregate is recency: check that reviews include projects from the past 6 months and reflect the type of maintenance work your property requires. A general contractor with strong residential reviews but no evidence of MCST or commercial project experience may lack the compliance rigour that strata management requires.

**Structured quoting and contract documentation.** Reputable MCST contractors provide itemised written quotations covering scope, materials, timeline, payment schedule, and variation order process before works commence. Contractors who resist written documentation create accountability gaps that expose the Management Corporation to cost overruns and defect disputes. Always require a written contract that specifies defect liability period and the process for remediation.

**Defect liability period and workmanship warranty.** MCST contractors should provide a clear defect liability period, typically 12 to 24 months for maintenance works, during which any defects arising from their workmanship are rectified at no additional cost. Confirm this in writing before signing. Contractors who cannot commit to a defect liability period are signalling low confidence in their own workmanship.

**Emergency response capability.** MCST maintenance obligations under the BMSMA do not pause outside business hours. Contractors who offer extended availability, or a dedicated emergency response line, are materially more valuable to Management Corporations than those who operate standard office hours only. Confirm the contractor's availability and response commitment for urgent maintenance calls before awarding a contract.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does MCST maintenance cost in Singapore?

MCST maintenance contract costs in Singapore range widely based on property size, scope, and services included. Routine maintenance contracts for a standard condominium typically range from SGD 5,000 to SGD 20,000 per year for multi-trade handyman and routine works. Specialist contracts covering M&E management, waterproofing, or structural maintenance are priced separately and can range from SGD 10,000 to SGD 80,000 or more per contract cycle depending on scope and property size.

### What is an MCST contractor and what do they do?

An MCST (Management Corporation Strata Title) contractor is engaged by a strata property's Management Corporation to maintain and repair common areas and building systems. Their scope can include painting, plumbing, electrical maintenance, waterproofing, structural repairs, carpark maintenance, M&E servicing, and landscaping. MCST maintenance obligations are governed by the Building Maintenance and Strata Management Act (BMSMA), which requires the MC to keep common property in good repair and condition.

### Do MCST contractors in Singapore need to be licensed?

MCST contractors must hold appropriate licences depending on the works they perform. Electrical works require EMA-licensed electrical workers. Structural works require BCA-registered contractors under the relevant workhead. Gas works require Singapore-licensed gas service workers. General building contractors working on private strata properties without structural or licensed-trade elements do not require a specific contractor licence, but should hold BCA registration and relevant bizSAFE certification as a minimum standard.

### How do I choose an MCST contractor for my condominium?

Start by identifying your property's primary maintenance needs: routine multi-trade, M&E systems, waterproofing, or structural. Request itemised quotations from at least three contractors whose scope matches those needs. Verify BCA registration and licence status for the relevant workheads. Check Google review count and recency. Confirm defect liability period and emergency response availability in writing. Response speed to your initial enquiry is a reliable signal of how the contractor will communicate during a live contract.

### How far in advance should an MC appoint a maintenance contractor?

Management Corporations should begin the contractor appointment process at least 3 to 6 months before the current contract expires or new maintenance needs arise. For large-scope contracts covering M&E, waterproofing, and structural works, allow 6 months to issue a proper request for quotation, evaluate submissions, conduct site visits, and complete contract negotiation. Rushing contractor appointment typically results in poor scope definition and reduced ability to negotiate on price and terms.

### What are the red flags when hiring an MCST maintenance contractor?

Red flags include: inability to produce a BCA registration number or relevant licence, no verifiable physical business address, vague or lump-sum quotations with no itemised scope, requests for more than 30% upfront payment before works commence, absence of a defect liability clause in the contract, no track record of MCST or commercial strata project experience, and Google reviews that are suspiciously clustered or generic.

### Can an MCST contractor subcontract works to third parties?

Many MCST contractors subcontract specialist trade works. Subcontracting is acceptable provided the primary contractor remains fully accountable for the quality, compliance, and timeline of all works under their contract. Require the contractor to disclose any subcontractors and confirm that subcontracted works are covered by the same defect liability period as directly executed works. Contractors who refuse to disclose subcontracting arrangements create a compliance risk for the Management Corporation.

### What is the difference between an MCST contractor and a facilities management company?

An MCST contractor is typically engaged for specific maintenance works or ad-hoc projects. A facilities management company provides a comprehensive, contract-based service that covers building maintenance, M&E, cleaning, security, and other operational services under a single agreement with regular reporting. Facilities management is better suited to larger strata properties where a systematic, proactive approach to property care is preferred over reactive maintenance call-outs.

### What happens if an MCST contractor's work causes damage to a unit owner?

If a contractor engaged by the Management Corporation causes damage to a subsidiary proprietor's property, the MC is generally liable for the cost of remediation. This underlines the importance of engaging contractors who carry adequate public liability insurance and professional indemnity coverage. Always verify that the contractor's insurance is current and sufficient for the scope of works before commencement.

### How often should MCST maintenance be carried out in Singapore?

Frequency depends on the maintenance element. General inspections and routine works such as painting, plumbing checks, and M&E servicing are typically on annual or biannual cycles. Waterproofing inspections should be conducted annually, with remediation as required. Structural inspections are mandated under BCA's mandatory building inspection requirements at 5-year intervals for buildings over a certain age. Emergency reactive maintenance should be responded to within hours, not days.
