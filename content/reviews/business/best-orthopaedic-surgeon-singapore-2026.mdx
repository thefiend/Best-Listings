## What to Look for When Hiring an Orthopaedic Surgeon in Singapore

**Sub-specialty focus.** Orthopaedics covers the entire musculoskeletal system. A surgeon who specialises in spine surgery is not the right choice for an ACL reconstruction, and vice versa. Identify your condition first, then look for a surgeon whose practice is primarily built around that area. Clinics that name their sub-specialty in the practice name (OrthofootMD, Hip and Knee Orthopaedics, Centre for Spine and Orthopaedic Surgery) signal genuine focus rather than general coverage.

**Google review volume and recency.** A 5.0 rating with 50 reviews is less reliable than a 4.9 rating with 500 reviews. Volume matters because it reduces the statistical effect of outlier reviews. Recency matters because a clinic's quality can change if a surgeon changes partner practices, retires, or relocates. Check that reviews have been posted in the last 3 months before booking.

**Consultation approach.** Singapore's top orthopaedic surgeons are distinguished by their willingness to explain diagnosis and treatment options in plain language, to consider non-surgical management first, and to give patients time to ask questions. Reviews consistently reward surgeons who do not rush consultations. This is both a quality signal and a practical indicator of how the post-operative relationship will function.

**Medisave and insurance compatibility.** Most orthopaedic procedures in Singapore are Medisave-claimable under MediShield Life or Integrated Shield Plans, but the claimable amount varies by procedure and insurer. Confirm with the clinic's billing coordinator before your first consultation whether your specific condition and proposed treatment pathway is covered, and what out-of-pocket exposure to expect.

**Hospital and facility access.** Singapore's top orthopaedic surgeons typically practise at Mount Elizabeth (Orchard or Novena), Gleneagles, or Parkway East for private surgery. The hospital where your surgeon has operating privileges matters for admitted procedures. If your surgeon is at Novena but your insurer panel covers only Mount Elizabeth Orchard, there may be billing implications worth clarifying upfront.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does an orthopaedic surgeon cost in Singapore?

Initial consultation fees at private orthopaedic clinics in Singapore typically range from SGD 150 to SGD 350. Diagnostic imaging (X-ray, MRI) adds SGD 200 to SGD 1,500 depending on the scan. Surgical procedures range widely: arthroscopic keyhole surgery starts around SGD 8,000 to SGD 12,000, while total knee or hip replacement can cost SGD 25,000 to SGD 45,000 in a private hospital. Medisave and Integrated Shield Plans can offset a significant portion of surgical costs, but the out-of-pocket component depends on your specific plan and the procedure involved.

### What is the difference between an orthopaedic surgeon and a physiotherapist?

An orthopaedic surgeon is a medical doctor who specialises in diagnosing and treating musculoskeletal conditions, including performing surgery. A physiotherapist is an allied health professional who provides rehabilitation, exercise therapy, and manual treatment but cannot diagnose conditions, order imaging, or perform surgery. For a new injury or undiagnosed pain, see an orthopaedic surgeon first. Physiotherapy is typically the next step after diagnosis, either as a primary treatment or as part of surgical recovery.

### How do I know if I need orthopaedic surgery in Singapore?

Not all orthopaedic conditions require surgery. Most orthopaedic surgeons in Singapore follow a conservative-first approach, trialling physiotherapy, medications, and injections before recommending surgical intervention. Surgery is typically recommended when conservative treatment has failed over a defined period, when structural damage is severe (such as complete ligament rupture or advanced joint degeneration), or when delay would worsen the outcome. An orthopaedic consultation will clarify whether surgery is necessary for your specific condition.

### Are orthopaedic consultations covered by Medisave in Singapore?

Outpatient specialist consultations at private clinics are generally not Medisave-claimable unless accompanied by a day surgery or admitted procedure. Surgical procedures performed in a licensed private or restructured hospital are typically Medisave-claimable under approved procedure codes. MediShield Life and Integrated Shield Plans cover hospitalisation and surgical costs subject to deductibles and co-insurance. Confirm your specific coverage with your insurer and the clinic's billing team before proceeding.

### How long is the waiting time to see an orthopaedic surgeon in Singapore?

At top-rated private clinics, initial consultation waiting times typically range from 1 to 4 weeks. Urgent referrals via a GP with a referral letter can sometimes accelerate appointments to within the same week. Public restructured hospitals (SGH, Tan Tock Seng, NUH) offer subsidised orthopaedic care but waiting times can extend to 1 to 3 months for non-urgent cases. For acute injuries, the A&E pathway at a restructured hospital provides faster initial assessment.

### What should I bring to my first orthopaedic consultation in Singapore?

Bring any previous imaging (X-ray films, MRI reports, or CD/USB with DICOM files), a summary of your medical history and current medications, your referral letter if applicable, and your NRIC or passport for registration. If you have seen other specialists for the same condition, bring their reports.
