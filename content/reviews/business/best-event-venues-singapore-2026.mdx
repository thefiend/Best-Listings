## What to Look for When Hiring an Event Venue

**Capacity and layout flexibility.** The stated maximum capacity of a venue can be misleading if it refers to a standing cocktail arrangement when you need a seated dinner configuration. Ask for the maximum seated capacity, the maximum standing capacity, and whether the venue can accommodate your specific layout including a stage, activity zone, or food station arrangement.

**Catering policy.** Some Singapore event venues are tied to specific caterers or require in-house F&B purchasing. Others allow external catering or a fully bring-your-own arrangement. Clarify this early as it significantly affects both your food choices and your total event cost.

**Technical facilities.** For corporate events, workshops, and presentations, confirm what audio-visual equipment is included: projector or screen, microphone system, Bluetooth speaker, and WiFi quality. Many boutique venues have limited AV infrastructure and rental costs for additional equipment can be significant.

**Noise restrictions and curfew.** Many Singapore event spaces in shophouse and residential-adjacent locations have strict noise and music curfews, often ending at 10pm or 10:30pm. If your event includes a DJ or live music, confirm these restrictions explicitly before booking.

**Parking and accessibility.** Not all guests will travel by MRT. Check the parking availability at or near the venue for your expected number of drivers. For family events with elderly guests or young children, step-free accessibility and proximity to drop-off points matters significantly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to rent an event space in Singapore?

Event space rental in Singapore typically costs $80 to $250 per hour for small venues accommodating up to 50 guests. Full-day rental for medium-sized venues (50 to 100 guests) ranges from $800 to $2,500. Premium or uniquely designed spaces in central locations can cost $2,000 to $5,000 for a full day. Prices vary significantly based on location, facilities, and minimum booking hours.

### What is the best event venue for a birthday party in Singapore?

The Jolly Space and The Happi Loft are the top choices for birthday parties in Singapore, both offering party-friendly setups, flexible catering, and teams experienced in supporting celebration events. Our Little Space @ WIS is the best option for families in the east Changi area.

### Can I bring my own food and drinks to a rented event space in Singapore?

This varies by venue. Many boutique event spaces in Singapore allow external catering or bring-your-own arrangements, while some require in-house catering packages or approved vendor lists. Always confirm the catering policy in writing before booking, as this is one of the most common sources of event day disputes.

### How early should I book an event space in Singapore?

For popular weekend timeslots at well-reviewed venues, book at least 4 to 8 weeks in advance. For peak event dates such as public holiday weekends and auspicious dates in the Chinese calendar, 2 to 3 months' advance booking is recommended. Many venues offer weekday slots at shorter notice.

### What is the difference between an event venue and a function room?

A function room typically refers to a dedicated event space within a hotel, community club, or commercial building with standard furnishing and facilities. An event venue tends to imply a standalone or purpose-designed space with a distinct aesthetic identity. The venues on this list are primarily boutique event spaces with defined visual character rather than generic function rooms.

### Do Singapore event venues provide tables and chairs?

Most event venues include basic furniture such as tables, chairs, and sometimes cocktail tables in the rental fee. More specialised furniture, staging, and decorative elements are typically available as optional add-ons or can be sourced from event rental companies. Confirm exactly what is included in the base rate before signing.

### What venues are suitable for corporate team events in Singapore?

HIGHfive Event Space and Enclosed. Event Space are the strongest choices for corporate events, combining professional management, central locations, and versatile indoor configurations. Level Two Space also works well for smaller corporate workshops and away days.

### Are these event venues suitable for solemnisation ceremonies?

Yes. Level Two Space, Norris House, and Enclosed. Event Space are all popular for solemnisation ceremonies. Their intimate scale, aesthetic quality, and flexible setup options make them well-suited to civil solemnisations and small wedding receptions.
