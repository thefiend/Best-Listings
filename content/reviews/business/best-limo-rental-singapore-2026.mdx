## What to Look for When Renting a Limo in Singapore

**Rental duration options.** Confirm whether the operator offers hourly, half-day, and full-day rental structures. Some operators only offer point-to-point transfers and don't genuinely support disposal or timed hire. If you need to hold a vehicle and driver for several hours, confirm the duration structure and what is included in each block before booking.

**Vehicle documentation.** A legitimate limo rental operator can provide the specific make, model, and in many cases the registration number of the vehicle before the rental date. Operators who describe vehicles only as "luxury car" or "executive sedan" without specifics may be using third-party hires with no direct quality control over what arrives.

**LTA registration.** All vehicles used for hire in Singapore must be registered and approved by the Land Transport Authority. Drivers must hold a valid Vocational Licence (VC). Confirm both before booking, particularly for corporate engagements where duty-of-care obligations apply.

**Cancellation policy.** For event-day limo rental: weddings, galas, corporate functions: understand the cancellation and amendment policy clearly before booking. Confirm the notice period required to cancel without full-charge liability and whether date changes are possible without rebooking fees.

**Driver inclusion.** All Singapore limo rentals include a licensed chauffeur: there is no self-drive luxury hire option under LTA regulations. The driver is always part of the rental. Confirm whether the operator allows you to request a specific driver for repeat bookings, which matters for clients who value consistency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I rent a limo in Singapore?

Contact the operator directly via their website or WhatsApp with your rental date, duration, pickup location, and destination or itinerary. Reputable operators will respond with a fixed-rate quote for the vehicle class and duration you require. Confirm the vehicle make and model, driver details, and the full pricing before paying any deposit. All limo rentals in Singapore include a chauffeur: there is no self-drive luxury vehicle hire available.

### How much does limo rental cost per hour in Singapore?

Hourly limo rental in Singapore typically ranges from SGD 80–200 per hour depending on vehicle class and operator. An executive saloon (Mercedes-Benz E-Class) typically starts around SGD 80–120 per hour. Premium S-Class or equivalent vehicles range from SGD 130–200 per hour. Rates usually include fuel and the chauffeur's fee. Confirm whether there is a minimum hire period, which most operators set at two or three hours for hourly bookings.

### Can I rent a limo without a driver in Singapore?

No. Under Singapore Land Transport Authority (LTA) regulations, all vehicles offered for commercial hire must be operated by a licensed chauffeur holding a valid Vocational Licence. There is no self-drive luxury car hire option available from the operators on this list or from any LTA-compliant limo rental provider in Singapore. The chauffeur is always included as part of the rental cost.

### What vehicles are available for limo rental in Singapore?

Common vehicles available for limo rental in Singapore include the Mercedes-Benz E-Class and S-Class for executive bookings, the Toyota Alphard and Hyundai Staria for group rentals, the Mercedes-Benz V-Class for large groups, and the Bentley Flying Spur for premium wedding and VIP occasions.
