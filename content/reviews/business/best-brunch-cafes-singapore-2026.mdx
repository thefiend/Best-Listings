Finding a great brunch cafe in Singapore is harder than it looks. The island has hundreds of options, but most disappoint on at least one dimension — the food is good but the wait is punishing, the ambience is Instagram-worthy but the coffee is forgettable, or the menu looks promising and the execution does not follow through. We evaluated over 60 brunch cafes across Singapore, ranking each on Google Maps rating, review volume, food consistency, coffee quality, value for money, halal accessibility, and the all-important factor that separates a great brunch from a stressful one: whether you can actually get a table. This list covers the best options for the classic lazy weekend brunch, the smart weekday late breakfast, and every occasion in between.

**Key Takeaways**

- **Best overall:** My Awesome Cafe at Telok Ayer, 7,890 Google reviews at 4.7 stars, the highest-reviewed brunch cafe in Singapore by a significant margin
- **Best for international flavours:** Brunches Cafe on Rangoon Road with 3,099 Google reviews at 4.7 stars, serving global breakfasts and snacks in a playful retro-decorated space
- **Best French brunch experience:** Merci Marcel Orchard with 4,092 Google reviews at 4.7 stars, the benchmark for all-day European brunch dining in Singapore
- **Best heritage setting:** Wildseed Cafe at The Alkaff Mansion with 3,170 reviews at 4.5 stars, set inside a restored colonial mansion with sweeping views
- **Best halal option:** Brunch Club by The Halal Corner, fully halal-certified with 738 reviews at 4.7 stars — one of the best halal brunch experiences in the city
- A typical brunch in Singapore costs SGD 15–40 per person for food, with coffee adding SGD 6–9 per cup; pricing varies significantly between neighbourhood cafes and hotel-adjacent or mall-based spots
- Weekend brunch queues at popular CBD and Orchard cafes can run 30–60 minutes — arrive before 10am or after 1:30pm for walk-in seating, or book in advance where reservations are accepted

## How We Ranked These Brunch Cafes

We ranked each cafe on: Google Maps rating (minimum 4.4 required), review count (minimum 300 verified reviews), food quality signals across multiple review batches, coffee quality, ambience and setting, value per dollar spent, wait times and reservation availability, and halal or dietary accessibility. Cafes with persistent patterns of long unexplained waits, inconsistent kitchen execution, or service complaints were excluded or ranked lower regardless of star rating. We also assessed area coverage to ensure this list is useful regardless of which part of Singapore you are in.

### 1. My Awesome Cafe, Best Overall

<img src="/images/places/best-brunch-cafes-singapore/my-awesome-cafe.jpg" alt="My Awesome Cafe" className="w-full rounded-lg my-4 object-cover" style={{height: "300px"}} />

My Awesome Cafe, set inside a beautifully repurposed heritage shophouse on Telok Ayer Street, is Singapore's highest-reviewed brunch cafe by a significant margin — an extraordinary 7,890 Google reviews at 4.7 stars, a volume that no marketing campaign can fabricate and only years of consistently good food and an engaging atmosphere can produce. The menu centres on handcrafted salads and sandwiches built from quality ingredients, with brunch dishes that reward attention to detail rather than relying on dramatic presentation or novelty for its own sake. The repurposed decor — salvaged materials, warm lighting, and thoughtful touches throughout the space — creates an environment that feels like a discovery rather than a corporate rollout, which is increasingly rare in Singapore's saturated cafe landscape.

The cafe's wine and spirits list is an underappreciated differentiator. Most brunch cafes in Singapore pivot awkwardly between breakfast-only and an evening bar concept, but My Awesome Cafe manages the transition smoothly — the same space and the same quality of execution carries through from the lunch hour to the evening without the atmosphere thinning out. For CBD workers looking for a midweek brunch that does not feel like a canteen, or for weekend visitors wanting a proper sit-down meal in a heritage neighbourhood, this is the most reliable choice on the island.

## Brunch Cafes by Area

Singapore's best brunch cafes are distributed across the island rather than concentrated in a single district. Here is a quick reference by neighbourhood to help you choose based on where you are.

**Orchard Road and River Valley:** Merci Marcel Orchard and Avenue On 3 Cafe are both strong Orchard options — Merci Marcel for a full French brunch experience with pastries and wine, Avenue On 3 for a quieter, higher-rated alternative inside The Paragon.

**CBD and Tanjong Pagar:** My Awesome Cafe at Telok Ayer is the top CBD pick by a considerable margin — the most-reviewed brunch cafe in Singapore, well-positioned for both weekday late breakfasts and weekend visits.

**Bugis, Kampong Glam, and Little India:** Brunch Club by The Halal Corner on Jalan Pisang is the best halal brunch option in this corridor, while Brunches Cafe on Rangoon Road and Hey Slow Joe on Tessensohn Road cover the Farrer Park area for non-halal alternatives.

**Botanic Gardens and Holland Village:** Wildseed at The Garage inside the Botanic Gardens is the area's standout — open from 7:30am daily with the Gardens walk as a natural accompaniment.

**Southern Ridges and HarbourFront:** Wildseed at The Alkaff Mansion offers the best heritage brunch setting in Singapore, accessible by bus from HarbourFront and worth the slight detour from the MRT network for the atmosphere alone.

**East Singapore — Joo Chiat, Katong, and Siglap:** The Brewing Ground at The Yards on Joo Chiat Place is the best family-friendly brunch option in the East, with a playground and patio that set it apart from the standard East Coast cafe.

**North-East Singapore:** Lola's Cafe at Simon Road is the reliable all-day brunch option for residents of Potong Pasir, Upper Serangoon, and Toa Payoh, with a wide menu and a 9am–10pm format that covers more of the day than any other cafe on this list.

## What to Look for When Choosing a Brunch Cafe in Singapore

**Operating hours and service window.** Many Singapore cafes use the word "brunch" loosely — some open at 11am, which is lunch by any practical definition. If you want a genuine morning brunch, look for opening times of 8–9am. The cafes on this list that open at 7:30am (Wildseed at The Garage) or 8am (Merci Marcel, Wildseed at Alkaff Mansion, Hey Slow Joe, The Brewing Ground) are the ones genuinely committed to the morning window.

**Halal certification for mixed groups.** Singapore's diverse population means that brunch groups frequently include diners with halal requirements. Confirming whether a cafe is halal-certified before arriving saves significant awkwardness. Brunch Club by The Halal Corner is the strongest halal brunch option on this list, but always verify certification status independently as these can change.

**Price range and value signals.** The typical Singapore brunch costs SGD 15–40 per person for food, with specialty coffee adding SGD 6–9 per cup. Heritage settings and Orchard Road locations command a premium — budget SGD 30–50 per person at Merci Marcel or Wildseed at Alkaff Mansion, versus SGD 15–25 at neighbourhood options like Brunches Cafe, Hey Slow Joe, or Lola's Cafe. The price gap does not always correspond to a food quality gap — some of the lower-priced cafes on this list are executing at a higher level than more expensive alternatives.

**Weekend wait times.** The most popular cafes on this list — My Awesome Cafe, Merci Marcel, and Wildseed at Alkaff Mansion — experience peak queues of 20–45 minutes on Saturday and Sunday mornings between 10:30am and 1pm. The practical solutions are to arrive before 10am, to visit after 1:30pm when first-sitting diners are turning over, or to choose cafes that take reservations. Calling ahead on weekday brunches is rarely necessary at most of these venues.

**Family-friendliness.** The Brewing Ground at Joo Chiat with its outdoor patio and playground, and Lola's Cafe at Simon Road with its laid-back atmosphere and wide menu, are the strongest family brunch options on this list. Wildseed at The Garage in the Botanic Gardens also works well for families given the outdoor space and the garden walking that naturally extends the visit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best brunch cafe in Singapore overall?

My Awesome Cafe at Telok Ayer is the top-ranked overall brunch cafe in Singapore, with 7,890 Google reviews at 4.7 stars — the highest review volume of any brunch cafe on the island. Merci Marcel Orchard is the top pick specifically for a French brunch experience, while Avenue On 3 at The Paragon holds the highest average rating at 4.8 stars with strong consistent reviews.

### Which brunch cafes in Singapore are halal-certified?

Brunch Club by The Halal Corner on Jalan Pisang near Bugis is the most prominent halal-certified brunch cafe in Singapore, with 738 Google reviews at 4.7 stars. Always verify current halal certification status directly with the cafe before visiting, as certification status can change.

### How much does brunch typically cost in Singapore?

A typical brunch at a good Singapore cafe costs SGD 15–40 per person for food, plus SGD 6–9 per coffee. Total spend per person including drinks is generally SGD 25–50 at neighbourhood cafes and SGD 35–65 at premium locations like Merci Marcel or Wildseed at The Alkaff Mansion. Prices listed are indicative as at August 2026.

### Which brunch cafes open earliest in Singapore?

Wildseed at The Garage inside the Botanic Gardens opens earliest at 7:30am daily. Hey Slow Joe, Wildseed at The Alkaff Mansion, Merci Marcel Orchard, and The Brewing Ground all open at 8am or 8:30am, making them the best options for early morning brunch. Most other cafes on this list open at 9am or later.

### Are there good brunch cafes outside the CBD and Orchard areas?

Yes — Brunches Cafe at Rangoon Road covers the Farrer Park and Little India area, The Brewing Ground at Joo Chiat covers East Singapore, Lola's Cafe at Simon Road serves the Upper Serangoon corridor, and Wildseed at The Alkaff Mansion and Wildseed at The Garage serve the Southern Ridges and Botanic Gardens precincts respectively. The Singapore brunch scene has meaningful quality options across most parts of the island.

### What is the difference between a brunch cafe and a regular cafe in Singapore?

A brunch cafe typically opens earlier (8–10am rather than 10–11am), runs a morning-focused menu with eggs dishes, pastries, and breakfast mains alongside the standard coffee and cake options, and designs its space for a longer seated dining experience rather than takeaway or quick coffee. The best brunch cafes on this list all share these characteristics — they are genuinely designed around the brunch occasion rather than simply adding breakfast items to an afternoon cafe menu.

## Verdict

My Awesome Cafe at Telok Ayer is the top pick for most Singapore brunch occasions — the review volume alone (7,890 at 4.7 stars) represents a level of consistent quality verification that cannot be replicated by any other cafe on this list. Merci Marcel Orchard is the pick for a proper French brunch experience with pastries, wine, and a European ambience that the CBD options do not offer. Avenue On 3 at The Paragon holds the highest rating on this list at 4.8 stars and remains underappreciated relative to its quality. For halal brunch, Brunch Club by The Halal Corner is the clear recommendation. For families in the East, The Brewing Ground with its playground and patio is the practical choice. And for a brunch that doubles as an occasion — a heritage mansion setting that justifies the effort of getting there — Wildseed at The Alkaff Mansion has no equal in Singapore.
