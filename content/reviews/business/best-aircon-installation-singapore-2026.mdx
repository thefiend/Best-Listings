## How We Ranked These Aircon Installation Companies

Our ranking methodology for the best aircon installation companies in Singapore covers six criteria. **Regulatory compliance**: all technicians performing refrigerant-handling and electrical work must hold BCA-registered worker credentials and NEA licensing; any company unable to confirm this was excluded regardless of review score. **Brand certification**: installers trained and certified directly by Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Panasonic, LG, and Samsung scored higher, as manufacturer certification ensures correct installation procedures and preserves warranty validity. **Scope of installation services**: providers covering the full range from standard residential split units through to commercial multi-split and cassette systems ranked above those handling residential-only work. **Pricing transparency**: companies that provide itemised written quotes covering unit cost, piping, electrical, and installation labour before any technician visits scored higher than those quoting only on-site. **Verified Google reviews**: minimum 4.5 stars across a meaningful review count; review recency, response to complaints, and consistency over time were all assessed. **Response time**: we sent test enquiries to each company and measured time to first substantive reply during business hours. ## What to Look for When Hiring an Aircon Installation Company

**BCA registration and NEA licensing.** Singapore's Building and Construction Authority requires workers performing refrigerant-handling and electrical work to hold valid registrations. Any installation company that cannot confirm their technicians' credentials before arriving on-site should be avoided. Ask directly: "Are your technicians BCA-registered for refrigerant work?" A reputable installer will answer this immediately.

**Brand certification.** Aircon manufacturers including Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Panasonic, and LG run formal installer certification programs. A certified installer follows the manufacturer's installation procedures exactly, which preserves warranty validity and ensures the system performs to its rated specifications. Ask whether the company's technicians are certified by the brand you are purchasing. An uncertified installation can void a manufacturer warranty that should cover parts and labour for 5 years.

**Itemised written quotation before work begins.** A trustworthy aircon installation quote covers four line items: unit supply cost, piping and trunking materials, electrical work, and installation labour. Any installer who provides only a single total figure without breaking it down, or who will only confirm pricing on-site, should be treated with caution. The quote should be fixed before any technician visits your property.

**Site survey quality.** A proper installation starts with a site survey: the technician assesses the wall, ceiling, and floor routing options, measures the piping runs, checks the electrical load capacity, and confirms drainage gradients before finalising the installation plan. An installer who quotes without a site survey is guessing on piping lengths and routing complexity, which frequently leads to scope changes and price additions on the day.

**Post-installation warranty registration.** Every major brand's Singapore warranty requires the installer to register the unit with the manufacturer within a specified window after installation. A professional installer handles this registration as part of the job handover. Confirm in advance that warranty registration is included, and ask for written confirmation of the registration at handover.

**Review recency and response pattern.** A 4.8-star rating across 50 reviews posted three years ago tells you less than 4.8 stars across 500 reviews with activity in the past 30 days. Check that recent reviews reflect the same quality as the aggregate score, and check whether the company responds to any negative reviews: the response tells you more about their service culture than the review itself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does aircon installation cost in Singapore in 2026?

Standard pricing for aircon installation in Singapore ranges from SGD 400–700 per indoor unit for a basic residential split-unit supply-and-fit package, inclusive of piping up to 4 metres. Multi-split systems start from SGD 1,200–1,800 for a 2-room configuration and scale with the number of indoor units. Longer piping runs, complex routing through walls or ceilings, additional electrical work, and premium brands add to the base cost. A full 3-room HDB installation covering three indoor units and one outdoor unit typically costs SGD 1,500–2,500 supply-and-fit. Always request an itemised quote covering unit cost, piping materials, electrical connections, and labour separately before comparing providers.

### How long does aircon installation take in Singapore?

A standard split-unit installation for one indoor unit takes 2 to 3 hours from technician arrival to testing. A full multi-split system covering 3 to 4 indoor units in a standard HDB or condominium typically takes 4 to 6 hours with a two-person team. More complex installations involving extended piping runs, ceiling cassettes, or multi-storey landed properties can take a full day. Most reputable installers will give you a realistic time estimate after the site survey, not before.

### What is the difference between a split unit and a multi-split aircon system?

A split-unit system has one outdoor unit connected to one indoor unit, giving independent temperature control per room but requiring a separate outdoor unit for each room. A multi-split system connects one outdoor unit to multiple indoor units (typically 2 to 5), which reduces the number of outdoor units and the piping complexity, but means that if the shared outdoor unit fails, all connected rooms lose cooling simultaneously. Multi-split systems are generally preferred in Singapore HDB flats and condominiums due to space and aesthetic constraints on outdoor unit placement.

### Does aircon installation in Singapore require a permit?

Installation of a standard residential split or multi-split system in Singapore does not require a building permit. However, the technicians performing refrigerant handling must be registered with the BCA under the Refrigerant Handling (Vocational Licence) framework, and any electrical work must be performed by a licensed electrical worker under the Energy Market Authority's regime. Commercial installations involving ducted systems, high-capacity equipment, or structural modifications may require additional approvals. Always confirm your installer holds the relevant credentials before work begins.

### Which aircon brands are most commonly installed in Singapore in 2026?

Daikin holds the largest market share for residential aircon installation in Singapore, followed by Mitsubishi Electric, Panasonic, LG, and Samsung. Daikin and Mitsubishi Electric are generally regarded as the premium-tier options with stronger after-sales parts availability and longer-established Singapore service networks. Panasonic and LG offer strong mid-tier value. The choice of brand affects which installers are most appropriately certified for your system, as manufacturer certification programs vary between brands.

### How do I know if an aircon installation was done correctly?

A correctly installed aircon unit should cool the room to the set temperature within 15 to 20 minutes, produce no unusual noise from either the indoor or outdoor unit, show no condensation dripping from the indoor unit body (only from the drain pipe), and have all piping and trunking neatly aligned and secured. After installation, ask the technician to demonstrate the drainage test: pouring water into the drain tray to confirm it flows freely to the drain pipe outlet. Any vibration, rattling, or water pooling inside the indoor unit indicates an installation issue and should be reported immediately for rectification under the installer's workmanship warranty.

### How far in advance should I book an aircon installation in Singapore?

For standard residential installations with no urgency, booking 5 to 7 days in advance is sufficient for most providers to schedule a convenient slot. During peak periods coinciding with BTO key collection cycles, year-end renovation seasons, and the months of March to May when Singapore temperatures peak, popular installers can be booked out 2 to 3 weeks ahead. If you are fitting out a new flat and have a firm move-in date, book your installation company as soon as keys are collected rather than waiting until the week before.

### What should be included in an aircon installation warranty in Singapore?

A standard residential aircon installation in Singapore should include: a manufacturer warranty on the compressor (typically 5 years), a parts warranty (typically 2 to 3 years), and an installer workmanship warranty covering the installation itself (typically 1 year). Confirm all three are in place before signing off on the job. The manufacturer warranty is void if the unit is not registered with the manufacturer by the installer, so confirm at handover that registration has been completed and request the registration confirmation in writing.

### What are red flags when hiring an aircon installation company in Singapore?

Price quotes provided without a site survey. Refusal to confirm BCA technician registration credentials. No written itemised quotation before work begins, pricing only confirmed on-site. No manufacturer warranty registration included in the service. Significantly below-market pricing with no transparent explanation. Pressure to proceed immediately without adequate time to compare quotes. Any installer exhibiting more than one of these behaviours should be disqualified regardless of their review score.

### Can I buy my own aircon unit and hire a company only for installation?

Yes, supply-only installation is offered by most reputable aircon companies in Singapore. The installation cost when supplying your own unit is typically SGD 200–400 for a standard split unit, covering piping up to 4 metres, trunking, electrical connections, and commissioning. Note that some installers may decline to register the manufacturer warranty if the unit was not purchased through them: confirm this before proceeding. Also confirm that the unit you purchase is the correct capacity (BTU or horsepower) for the room dimensions, as an undersized unit will run continuously without reaching the set temperature and will fail prematurely.
