Singapore runs on air conditioning. With ambient temperatures averaging 31–33°C year-round and relative humidity rarely dropping below 80%, aircon is not a seasonal luxury but a permanent infrastructure requirement for homes, offices, and commercial premises alike. Unlike temperate climates where HVAC systems rest through winter, Singapore units run continuously, accumulating dust, mould, and scale that reduce cooling efficiency, raise electricity bills, and degrade indoor air quality over time. Regular aircon servicing is the single most effective way to extend unit lifespan, maintain energy efficiency, and ensure the air your household breathes is clean.

Aircon servicing in Singapore covers five core service types. **General servicing** (also called routine servicing) involves cleaning the air filter, fan coil, and drip tray without wall dismounting: the standard quarterly maintenance for a well-kept unit. **Chemical wash** involves partial disassembly and flushing the coils and fan with chemical solution; recommended when a unit smells musty or cools poorly despite recent general servicing. **Chemical overhaul** is a full strip-down: the blower wheel, drain pan, and all internal components are removed, individually cleaned, and reassembled: necessary for units neglected over a year or exhibiting persistent performance issues. **Repair** covers fault diagnosis and replacement of components including capacitors, PCB boards, gas refills, and compressor repairs. **Installation** covers supply, delivery, and fitting of new split, multi-split, or cassette systems across residential and commercial premises.

For a unit in regular use, the NEA-recommended servicing interval is once every three months. Units in dusty environments, homes with pets, or commercial premises with high footfall may need servicing every six to eight weeks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I service my aircon in Singapore?

Every three months is the standard recommendation for residential split-unit systems in Singapore, consistent with NEA guidance. Singapore's tropical climate means aircon units run year-round without any seasonal rest period, and the combination of continuous use and high ambient humidity accelerates dust and mould accumulation inside the unit. Units in high-dust environments, homes with pets, or rooms with heavy daily use may benefit from servicing every six to eight weeks. Commercial units operating continuously across a business day should be serviced monthly. Quarterly servicing maintains cooling efficiency, prevents mould growth on the fan coil, and extends the working lifespan of the unit: deferring service beyond six months typically increases the risk of needing a chemical wash rather than a standard general service.

### What is the difference between general servicing, chemical wash, and chemical overhaul?

**General servicing** involves cleaning the air filter, wiping the fan coil surface, and clearing the drip tray, without removing the unit from the wall. It is the standard quarterly maintenance for a well-kept unit and takes 20–30 minutes per unit. Cost: SGD 25–70 per unit.

**Chemical wash** involves partial disassembly of the unit and flushing the coils, drain pan, and fan blower with a chemical solution that breaks down accumulated grime, mould, and scale. It is needed when a unit smells musty, cools poorly despite recent general servicing, or has not been serviced in over six months. Cost: SGD 80–150 per unit.

**Chemical overhaul** is a full strip-down: the blower wheel, drain pan, and all internal components are individually removed, cleaned with chemical solution, and reassembled. It is the most thorough option and is required for units neglected for over a year, units with visible mould growth, or units that continue to underperform after repeated chemical washes. Cost: SGD 200–350 per unit.

### My aircon is not cold: what could be causing it, and what is the fix?

The most common causes of poor cooling in Singapore aircon units are: a clogged air filter restricting airflow (fix: general service or self-clean the filter); dirty fan coils reducing heat exchange efficiency (fix: chemical wash); low refrigerant gas from a slow leak (fix: gas top-up plus leak investigation); a faulty compressor or capacitor (fix: repair or component replacement); or incorrect temperature setting for the humidity level: Singapore's humidity means setting the unit to 24–25°C is more effective than attempting 18°C. If a unit is blowing air but not cooling to the set temperature after recent servicing, a gas top-up and coil inspection should be the first diagnostic steps before committing to more expensive repair work.

### How much does aircon servicing cost in Singapore in 2026?

Standard pricing ranges in Singapore for 2026: general servicing SGD 25–70 per unit (market average SGD 35–45 for a standard residential split unit); chemical wash SGD 80–150 per unit; chemical overhaul SGD 200–350 per unit; gas top-up SGD 80–150 depending on refrigerant type; installation from SGD 400–600 per indoor unit supply-and-fit for a standard residential split system. Most three-bedroom HDB flats with three to four units spend SGD 105–180 for a full general servicing visit. Price variation reflects the company's scale, technician qualifications, and whether the quote includes GST. Always request an itemised written quote before any technician arrives, and confirm whether GST is included in the figure provided.
