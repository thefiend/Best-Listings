## What to Look for When Hiring a Tempered Glass Contractor in Singapore

**Google reviews and volume.** A 4.5-star rating with 50 or more verified reviews is the minimum credibility threshold for glass contractors in Singapore. Glass installation is a precision trade: panels that are cut or fitted incorrectly can shatter under thermal stress or structural load. A contractor with hundreds of consistent five-star reviews has demonstrated the ability to fabricate and install correctly across a high volume of projects, not just on a handful of well-managed jobs.

**Glass specification and safety compliance.** Tempered glass in Singapore should meet SS 341 (Singapore Standard for tempered glass) or an equivalent international standard. Ask whether the glass carries a certification mark. For shower screens and balustrades, safety film lamination is a secondary protection measure that prevents panel collapse into shards if the glass breaks. A reputable contractor should be able to confirm the specification and provide documentation on request.

**In-house fabrication vs. reselling.** Some contractors source and fit glass that another supplier fabricates, others fabricate in-house. In-house fabrication gives the contractor direct accountability for panel dimensions, polishing quality, and edge finishing. When panels are cut off-site by a third party, measurement errors may not be caught until installation day. Ask whether fabrication is done in-house or outsourced.

**Measurement process.** Precision measurement is the most critical step in glass installation. A contractor who takes a single quick measurement and quotes immediately is cutting a corner that will show in the final installation. A professional contractor will measure multiple reference points, account for wall tolerance, and confirm dimensions before placing a fabrication order. Ask how measurements are taken and how dimensional errors are handled.

**Written quotation covering scope and warranty.** Legitimate glass contractors provide a written quotation that specifies glass type, thickness, frame system, number of panels, and installation scope. Ask what warranty is provided on workmanship and glass. A standard workmanship warranty for glass installation in Singapore is 12 months; frame and hardware warranties vary by product. Verbal-only agreements with no written scope are a disqualifying signal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does tempered glass installation cost in Singapore?

Tempered glass in Singapore typically costs SGD 80 to SGD 300 per square metre installed, depending on glass thickness, panel size, frame type, and complexity of the installation. Shower screens start around SGD 400 to SGD 800 for a standard framed unit. Frameless glass partitions and structural glazing cost more, generally starting from SGD 150 per square metre. Request itemised quotations from two or three contractors to compare material and labour costs separately.

### What is the difference between tempered glass and laminated glass?

Tempered glass is processed by heating to around 600 degrees Celsius and then rapidly cooled, creating surface compression that makes it four to five times stronger than standard float glass. When it breaks, it shatters into small blunt fragments rather than sharp shards. Laminated glass bonds two or more glass layers with a PVB interlayer; when broken, the fragments stay adhered to the interlayer rather than dispersing. Laminated glass is used where glass retention after breakage is critical, such as balcony glass floors and overhead glazing. For most residential applications in Singapore, tempered glass is the standard specification.

### What thickness of tempered glass is used for shower screens in Singapore?

Standard shower screen panels are 8mm or 10mm tempered glass. 8mm is adequate for standard swing and sliding shower doors; 10mm is recommended for frameless shower enclosures and larger panel sizes. 12mm is used for heavy glass doors and panels in commercial or high-specification residential applications. Ask your contractor to specify the thickness in writing in their quotation.

### How long does tempered glass installation take?

A standard residential installation such as a shower screen or glass partition typically takes 2 to 4 hours on-site, following a 3 to 7 day fabrication lead time after final measurements are taken. Commercial glazing projects with multiple panels or structural elements may take 1 to 3 days of installation. The full timeline from initial measurement to completed installation is typically 1 to 2 weeks for residential work.

### Do I need approval from HDB for tempered glass works?

Replacing like-for-like glass panels in existing frames generally does not require HDB approval. Installing new glass partitions, removing walls to create glass-enclosed spaces, or structural glazing in HDB flats may require HDB consent and potentially BCA approval. Your contractor should advise on permit requirements based on your specific scope before works commence. Always confirm in writing that required permits have been applied for before hacking or structural works begin.

### What questions should I ask before hiring a tempered glass contractor?

Ask: What glass specification and thickness will be used? Is the glass SS 341 certified or equivalent? Is fabrication done in-house? What warranty do you provide on workmanship and glass? What is the lead time from measurement to installation? Can you provide a written quotation with scope, materials, and payment schedule? ### Can tempered glass be drilled or cut after tempering?

No. Tempered glass cannot be cut, drilled, or modified after the tempering process. Any drilling or cutting causes the panel to shatter due to the internal stress created during tempering. All holes, notches, and cutouts must be machined into the glass before tempering. This is why accurate measurement and pre-planning is critical: a panel fabricated with incorrect hole positions cannot be reworked on-site.

### Is tempered glass safe for shower screens and balustrades in Singapore?

### How do I maintain tempered glass panels and shower screens?

Clean tempered glass with a soft cloth and a mild glass cleaner. Avoid abrasive cleaning pads, which scratch the surface and make the glass harder to clean over time.
