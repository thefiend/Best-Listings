## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does website development cost in Singapore?

Website development costs in Singapore vary widely by scope and technical complexity. A basic brochure site on WordPress typically runs SGD 2,000 to 5,000. A custom-designed corporate site with CMS, contact forms, and basic SEO architecture is commonly SGD 5,000 to 15,000. An e-commerce platform with payment gateway integration, product management, and mobile checkout optimisation ranges from SGD 8,000 to 30,000. Enterprise web applications with custom back-end logic, API integrations, and role-based access control can exceed SGD 50,000. Beware of quotes significantly below market rate — underpriced web development in Singapore typically involves offshore development with local account management, template deployments sold as custom builds, or scope exclusions that generate expensive change orders after project kick-off.

### Should I choose custom development or a WordPress build?

The right choice depends on your content management requirements, expected traffic, and integration needs. WordPress is a mature, well-supported platform suited to most corporate websites, blogs, and small-to-medium e-commerce deployments. It is cost-effective, widely understood by Singapore developers, and easy for non-technical staff to update. Custom development is justified when your site requires logic that no off-the-shelf CMS can handle — complex membership systems, multi-step booking workflows, real-time data feeds, or deep integration with internal business systems. Headless CMS architectures sit between the two: a structured content layer decoupled from the front-end, suited to performance-critical sites or businesses publishing across multiple channels. If you are unsure, start by mapping your content management and integration requirements before selecting a platform.

### How long does website development take in Singapore?

A standard brochure website takes four to eight weeks from brief to launch with a responsive agency and a client who provides content on schedule. An e-commerce platform with custom product cataloguing and payment integration typically takes eight to sixteen weeks. Enterprise web applications with multi-system integrations and phased testing requirements can run four to nine months. The most common cause of delays in Singapore web development projects is late content delivery from the client — copy, images, and brand assets handed over after the development phase has started. Agree a content deadline at project kick-off and treat it as a milestone that affects the launch date.

### What should I check before hiring a website development company in Singapore?

Review their live portfolio, not just screenshots — visit the URLs and test load time, mobile layout, and navigation on your phone. Ask specifically who will write the code: the Singapore team or an offshore development partner managed locally. Confirm ownership of all deliverables: source code, CMS credentials, domain, and hosting account should transfer to you on completion. Understand the post-launch support terms — what is covered in the first 30 and 90 days, what triggers a change order, and what the ongoing maintenance retainer covers. Check that Core Web Vitals and basic on-page SEO structure are included in the scope, not quoted as add-ons. Finally, ask for a client reference you can call directly rather than a written testimonial on their site.
