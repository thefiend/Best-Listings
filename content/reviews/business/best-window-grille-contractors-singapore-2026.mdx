## What to Look for When Hiring a Window Grille Contractor in Singapore

**HDB and BCA approval status.** For HDB flats, window replacement and structural grille installation must be carried out by HDB-registered contractors. Verify that any contractor you engage is on HDB's Approved Contractors list for window works. Installing unapproved windows or grilles in an HDB flat can result in fines and mandatory reinstatement at the homeowner's expense.

**Invisible grille certification.** Not all invisible grille products are certified to the same safety standard. Look for products with Singapore-relevant safety certifications and BCA approval. The cable tension system, anchor fixing design, and locking mechanism all affect the child safety performance of invisible grilles. Contractors offering significantly lower prices for invisible grilles may be using uncertified products with inferior cable systems.

**Stainless steel versus mild steel grilles.** Standard powder-coated mild steel grilles are the most common and least expensive option but are susceptible to rust in Singapore's humid and coastal environment. Stainless steel grilles cost 20 to 40% more but resist corrosion significantly better. For high-floor units, sea-facing properties, or homes near the coast, stainless steel is the more durable long-term investment.

**MCST requirements for condominiums.** Many condominium management councils require specific grille and window designs, materials, and colours to maintain the development's external facade consistency. Before engaging a contractor, confirm the approved specifications with your MCST and ensure the contractor is familiar with the submission process. Experienced condo specialists save significant time by handling MCST submissions as part of the installation process.

**Warranty on installation and product.** Window and grille installations carry workmanship warranties from reputable contractors, typically 12 months. Hardware components such as hinges, locks, and cable tensioners often carry separate manufacturer warranties. Confirm both in writing before installation. Post-installation adjustments to window friction stays or grille alignment should be included in the workmanship warranty period.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much do window grilles cost in Singapore?

Standard steel grilles cost SGD 50 to SGD 120 per linear metre installed. Stainless steel grilles cost SGD 80 to SGD 180 per linear metre. Invisible grilles (cable systems) cost SGD 100 to SGD 200 per linear metre depending on product brand and certification level. A standard 3-room HDB full grille installation typically costs SGD 800 to SGD 2,000 depending on grille type and number of windows.

### Do I need HDB approval to install grilles in Singapore?

For HDB flats, window grille installation does not require separate HDB approval as long as the grilles conform to HDB's approved dimensions and do not obstruct the window's emergency egress requirements. However, window replacement (changing the window frame itself) requires an HDB-registered contractor. Always confirm with your contractor whether your scope requires HDB registration or submission.

### What is the difference between invisible grilles and standard grilles?

Invisible grilles use stainless steel cables tensioned between anchor points to create a child safety barrier without obstructing the view. Standard grilles use steel or stainless steel bars in a grid or decorative pattern. Invisible grilles cost more but provide an unobstructed view and are popular for high-floor units or homes with garden views. Standard grilles are more cost-effective and also deter break-ins more visibly.

### How long does grille installation take for an HDB flat?

Full-home grille installation for a 4-room HDB flat (covering all bedroom and living room windows) typically takes 3 to 6 hours, often completed in a single visit. Invisible grille installation takes slightly longer due to cable threading and tension adjustment. The installation team typically requires clear access to all windows from inside the unit.

### Are invisible grilles safe for children in Singapore?

BCA-certified invisible grille systems are designed to meet child safety requirements when installed correctly. The key factors are cable spacing (should not allow a child's head to pass through: typically less than 100mm between cables), anchor fixing integrity, and cable tension. Always confirm that the invisible grille product holds BCA approval and has been independently certified for child safety before installation.

### How do I maintain window grilles in Singapore?

For standard grilles: wipe down monthly with a damp cloth to remove dust and salt deposits. Apply a light coat of WD-40 or silicone spray to hinged sections and locks annually. For stainless steel grilles: wipe with a damp cloth and dry immediately to prevent water spotting. For invisible grilles: check cable tension twice a year and retighten if any slack is detected. Report any bent or damaged cables to the installer immediately.

### What should I look for when choosing an invisible grille brand?

Key factors: BCA approval or equivalent certification for child safety, cable diameter (at least 1.5mm stainless steel), anchor fixing design (concealed anchors are more secure), locking mechanism quality, and track record of the installer. Avoid brands with unclear certifications or cables thinner than 1.5mm.

### Can I install grilles on HDB windows if I rent out my flat?

Yes. Window grilles are permitted in HDB flats regardless of whether the flat is owner-occupied or rented. If you are making changes to windows (replacing window frames rather than adding grilles to existing windows), an HDB-registered contractor must be engaged. Tenants are not permitted to make structural window modifications without the owner's consent and HDB compliance.

### How do I get an HDB-approved grille contractor in Singapore?

Visit the HDB website and search the Approved Contractors list under the window servicing and installation category. Alternatively, contact the contractors directly and ask them to confirm their HDB registration number before confirming any window work. Registered contractors can provide their HDB contractor registration documentation on request.

### What is the lifespan of window grilles in Singapore?

Standard powder-coated mild steel grilles last 10 to 15 years in Singapore's climate before significant rusting occurs, particularly in coastal or high-humidity locations. Stainless steel grilles last 20 to 30 years with minimal maintenance. Invisible cable grille systems last 15 to 20 years before cables require replacement, depending on exposure to salt air and UV.
