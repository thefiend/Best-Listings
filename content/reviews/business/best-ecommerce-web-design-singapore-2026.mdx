## What to Look for When Hiring an Ecommerce Web Design Company

Choosing the wrong ecommerce agency costs more than the build price. A store that launches with structural SEO problems, poor mobile performance, or a broken checkout will generate recurring losses in missed conversions and paid traffic that fails to convert. Before signing with any agency, apply these five criteria consistently:

**Platform expertise, not platform agnosticism.** Ask whether the agency has genuine depth in the specific platform you need: WooCommerce, Shopify, Magento, or custom. A generalist agency that builds whatever you ask for is less valuable than a specialist that knows the platform's limitations, optimal configurations, and common failure points. Platform expertise is demonstrated through a portfolio of real ecommerce work on that platform, not a sales deck claiming proficiency in everything.

**SEO integration from the wireframe stage.** On-page SEO, URL structure, product schema, canonical tags, and Core Web Vitals are not features you can retrofit onto a completed site without significant rework. Ask the agency at briefing stage exactly how SEO is integrated into their build process and request to see a site they have launched with strong organic performance. Agencies that treat SEO as a post-launch add-on are building stores that start with a structural visibility disadvantage.

**Mobile-first build process.** Over 60% of ecommerce traffic in Singapore arrives on mobile devices. A build process that designs for desktop and adapts for mobile produces different results than one that designs for mobile and scales up. Ask to see mobile product pages and checkout flows on real devices: not just browser previews: before committing to an agency's design process.

**Post-launch support terms in writing.** Every ecommerce store requires adjustments after launch: broken product variants, gateway configuration, shipping rule errors, SEO indexation issues. The standard of an agency's post-launch support is as important as the quality of the build itself. Get the support terms, response times, and included hours documented in the contract before work begins.

**Verified reviews with ecommerce-specific client feedback.** Generic five-star reviews with no project detail are a weak signal. Look for reviews that reference specific ecommerce outcomes: checkout conversion rates, mobile performance, SEO results, or integration quality. Real ecommerce clients describe real ecommerce outcomes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does ecommerce web design cost in Singapore in 2026?

Ecommerce web design costs in Singapore in 2026 vary considerably by scope and platform. A basic Shopify setup with a pre-built theme, up to 50 products, and standard payment gateway configuration typically ranges from SGD 3,000 to SGD 6,000. A custom Shopify or WooCommerce build with bespoke design, complex product variants, multi-currency support, and SEO foundations ranges from SGD 8,000 to SGD 20,000. Custom-built ecommerce platforms with ERP integration, advanced inventory management, or marketplace connectivity start from SGD 25,000 and can reach SGD 80,000-plus for large-scale builds. Agencies quoting below SGD 2,000 for a complete ecommerce store are typically delivering template builds with minimal customisation, no SEO structure, and limited payment options: not a foundation for revenue growth.

### How long does it take to build an ecommerce website in Singapore?

A standard Shopify ecommerce store with up to 100 products, custom design, and payment integration takes six to ten weeks from briefing to launch with a competent agency. WooCommerce builds with custom features or extensive product catalogues typically require eight to fourteen weeks. Custom-built ecommerce platforms with third-party integrations, ERP connections, or complex shipping logic can take three to six months depending on the complexity of the integration requirements. The most common cause of timeline delays is content: product photography, copy, and specification data that clients are responsible for providing. A realistic content timeline agreed at briefing prevents the majority of schedule overruns.

### What is the best ecommerce platform for Singapore businesses in 2026?

Shopify is the strongest choice for most Singapore consumer brands launching direct-to-consumer ecommerce stores: it is fast, mobile-optimised, well-supported locally, and the payment infrastructure works correctly out of the box with Singapore payment methods including PayNow and local card issuers. WooCommerce is the better choice for businesses that need deeper integration with existing WordPress content sites, more flexible product structures, or a platform that does not charge transaction fees. Custom builds are justified only when business requirements genuinely exceed what both Shopify and WooCommerce can support: complex B2B portals, marketplace features, or heavily customised checkout experiences. Avoid proprietary platforms built and owned by the agency: if you ever change providers, you may lose access to or control of your own store.

### Do I need separate SEO services after my ecommerce site is built?

This depends entirely on whether your agency integrated SEO into the build or treated it as a separate engagement. Agencies that build first and optimise later deliver stores that launch with structural SEO problems: poor URL architecture, missing product schema, unoptimised page titles, and Core Web Vitals scores that suppress search rankings. If your ecommerce site was built with SEO integrated from the wireframe stage, the organic foundation is already in place and ongoing SEO work focuses on content, link building, and ranking maintenance. If the build did not include SEO integration, the correct first step is a technical SEO audit before investing in any ongoing SEO retainer: retrofitting correct structure is cheaper than building content on a broken foundation.

### What is the difference between ecommerce web design and ecommerce web development?

Design covers the visual and UX layer: how the store looks, how products are presented, how the navigation and checkout flow guide shoppers toward purchase. Development covers the technical implementation: how the design is built in code, how the platform is configured, how payment gateways are integrated, and how the site performs under real traffic conditions. The best ecommerce agencies manage both as a unified discipline: a beautifully designed store with poor technical execution produces slow load times and checkout errors; a technically sound store with poor UX design produces low conversion rates. For ecommerce specifically, performance and conversion design are inseparable from the visual design process.

### How do I evaluate an ecommerce web design agency's portfolio in Singapore?

Request live URLs of ecommerce stores they have built: not screenshots or mockups. Test those sites on a mobile device using your own mobile connection. Check load times using Google PageSpeed Insights. Add a product to cart and step through the checkout to see how the purchase flow performs. Look at the product page structure: are product names, descriptions, and images optimised for search? Is the breadcrumb navigation correct? Does the site have structured data visible in a schema validator? Ask the agency what Core Web Vitals scores those live stores currently achieve. A strong ecommerce agency will give you specific performance benchmarks for their completed work because those benchmarks are a key part of what they are selling.

### Can I run Google Shopping ads with any ecommerce website?

Google Shopping requires a correctly structured product feed submitted to Google Merchant Center. WooCommerce and Shopify both support this with the right plugins or native integration, but the feed quality depends on how products are structured in your store: missing GTINs, incorrect product categories, or incomplete product data will cause feed errors that prevent ads from serving. The agencies on this list that offer integrated digital marketing: OOm, MediaPlus Digital, and Best Web Design Singapore: can configure your product feed, connect Merchant Center, and launch Google Shopping campaigns as part of the same engagement that built your store. This integrated approach avoids the feed errors that commonly occur when a separate ads agency works with a store they did not build.

### What ongoing maintenance does an ecommerce store require after launch?

An ecommerce store requires regular maintenance across several areas: platform and plugin updates to maintain security and compatibility, product catalogue updates as inventory changes, SEO monitoring to track ranking changes and address indexation issues, performance monitoring to catch load time regressions, and payment gateway maintenance as providers update their APIs. Most Singapore ecommerce agencies offer monthly maintenance retainers covering these essentials, typically ranging from SGD 200 to SGD 800 per month depending on catalogue size and the level of active SEO management included. Businesses that skip maintenance retainers typically encounter a deferred cost: security vulnerabilities, broken checkout flows, or ranking drops that require emergency remediation at higher per-incident cost than a regular retainer would have prevented.
