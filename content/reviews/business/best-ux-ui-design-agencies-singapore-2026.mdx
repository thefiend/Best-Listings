## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a UX/UI design agency actually do?

A UX/UI design agency handles two complementary disciplines. UX (user experience) design focuses on how a digital product works: the structure of information, the flow between screens, the logic of navigation, and the ease with which users complete their goals. UI (user interface) design focuses on how the product looks and feels: typography, colour, spacing, iconography, and visual hierarchy. A strong UX/UI agency conducts user research to understand audience needs, creates wireframes and prototypes to validate design decisions before development, designs high-fidelity interfaces that balance aesthetics with usability, and works with developers to ensure the live product matches the approved design. In Singapore, most UX/UI agencies also handle front-end development, providing an end-to-end service from research through to launch.

### How much does UX/UI design cost in Singapore?

UX/UI design project costs in Singapore vary significantly by scope. A UX audit of an existing website typically costs SGD 3,000 to SGD 8,000. A UX/UI redesign for a standard business website with 10 to 20 pages ranges from SGD 8,000 to SGD 25,000. E-commerce UX/UI projects with custom checkout flows range from SGD 15,000 to SGD 40,000. Complex web applications or multi-platform projects can exceed SGD 50,000. Monthly UX retainers for ongoing iteration and testing typically range from SGD 2,000 to SGD 8,000 per month. Pricing reflects the depth of user research, the number of unique page templates, the complexity of interactive elements, and whether the agency handles development alongside design. Always request an itemised quote that separates research, design, and development costs.

### How long does a UX/UI design project take in Singapore?

A typical UX/UI project for a business website in Singapore takes 8 to 16 weeks from kickoff to launch. The timeline breaks down approximately as follows: discovery and research (2 to 3 weeks), wireframing and information architecture (2 to 3 weeks), high-fidelity UI design (3 to 4 weeks), development and implementation (3 to 5 weeks), and testing and launch (1 to 2 weeks). Projects with extensive user research, multiple stakeholder review cycles, or complex interactive features will extend beyond this range. E-commerce platforms with custom checkout flows typically require 12 to 20 weeks. The single most common cause of timeline overruns in Singapore UX projects is delayed client feedback during review stages: agreeing on review turnaround times at project kickoff helps keep the schedule on track.

### What is the difference between UX design and web design?

Web design traditionally refers to the visual design and development of websites, often focused on layout, graphics, and content presentation. UX design is a broader discipline that starts with understanding user needs through research, defines how information should be structured and how users should navigate between tasks, and then applies visual design to support those functional objectives. A web designer may create a visually appealing page; a UX designer ensures that the page helps users accomplish what they came to do. In practice, the best outcomes come from agencies that integrate both: visual craft informed by user research. When evaluating agencies in Singapore, look for those that can articulate their research process, not just show portfolio screenshots.

### Should I hire a local Singapore UX/UI agency or an overseas team?

For businesses primarily serving Singapore customers, a local UX/UI agency offers significant advantages. They understand local user behaviour patterns, including mobile-first browsing habits, bilingual content expectations, and Singapore-specific trust signals such as ACRA registration numbers and local phone formats. Local agencies can conduct in-person user testing with actual Singapore users, participate in face-to-face design workshops, and respond within Singapore business hours when urgent revisions are needed. Overseas agencies may offer lower hourly rates, but the total project cost often equalises once communication overhead, timezone coordination, and revision cycles are factored in. For projects requiring deep understanding of Singapore market conventions, a local agency consistently delivers faster and more contextually appropriate results.

### How do I evaluate a UX/UI agency's portfolio?

Look beyond visual aesthetics when reviewing a UX/UI agency's portfolio. The strongest signal of UX capability is whether the agency can explain the problem each project solved, not just show the final screens. Ask these questions: What user research was conducted before design started? What was the measurable outcome after launch (conversion rate improvement, task completion rate, reduced bounce rate)? How does the mobile experience compare to desktop? Is the live site consistent with the portfolio screenshots, or has implementation degraded the design? Request case studies that include before-and-after metrics rather than static mockups. An agency that can demonstrate measurable impact from their UX work is fundamentally more credible than one that relies solely on visual portfolio presentation.
