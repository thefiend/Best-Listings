Singapore's investment landscape is among the most tightly regulated in Asia. Every retail investment platform operating here must hold a Capital Markets Services (CMS) licence issued by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), which also mandates strict client asset segregation, annual audits, and compliance with the Securities and Futures Act 2001. That regulatory baseline means you can invest with confidence — but it doesn't tell you which platform is right for your goals, fee tolerance, or portfolio strategy.

In 2026, the choice has never been wider. Robo-advisors like Endowus, StashAway, and Syfe have democratised low-cost passive investing, while self-directed brokerages such as moomoo, Tiger Brokers, and Interactive Brokers give active traders direct market access to SGX, Nasdaq, NYSE, HKEX, and beyond. The challenge is knowing where each platform excels — and where it falls short. This review ranks all ten on fees, fund selection, regulatory standing, platform usability, and suitability for different investor profiles.

- **Best overall:** Endowus — the only MAS-licensed platform that accepts CPF OA/SA, SRS, and cash in a single interface

- **Best for low-cost passive investing:** Syfe — Core portfolios from 0.35% p.a. with full SGX REIT exposure

- **Regulatory note:** All ten platforms hold valid MAS CMS licences; Endowus, StashAway, and Syfe are additionally licensed as Registered Fund Management Companies (RFMCs) or hold full LFMC status

## How We Ranked These Investment Platforms

Our rankings are based on six criteria weighted to reflect what actually matters to Singapore investors in 2026.

**Fee transparency and total cost of ownership.** We calculated the all-in annual cost for a SGD 50,000 portfolio on each platform, including management fees, fund expense ratios, FX conversion spreads, and any custody or withdrawal fees. Platforms that rebate trailer fees scored higher.

**Regulatory standing.** We verified each platform's MAS licence status, licence type (CMS, RFMC, LFMC), and whether client assets are held in segregated trust accounts. Platforms with no regulatory infractions on MAS's public register scored maximum marks.

**Investment universe.** We assessed the breadth of accessible instruments — unit trusts, ETFs, individual stocks, bonds, REITs, CPF-eligible funds, and alternative assets — and weighted this against the likely needs of the target investor profile.

**CPF and SRS compatibility.** Singapore's CPF and SRS schemes offer meaningful tax advantages. Platforms that integrate these accounts natively score significantly higher, as CPF-investing is a high-value differentiator that most international platforms cannot replicate.

**Platform usability.** We evaluated onboarding friction, mobile app quality, charting and research tools, customer support responsiveness, and the availability of paper trading or simulation modes.

**Returns and portfolio methodology.** For robo-advisors, we examined the underlying portfolio construction methodology, historical performance relative to benchmark, and rebalancing approach. For brokerages, we assessed execution quality, order types available, and margin lending rates.

### 1. Endowus — Best Overall

<img src="/images/places/best-investment-platforms-singapore/endowus.jpg" alt="Endowus" className="w-full rounded-lg my-4 object-cover" style={{height: "300px"}} />

Endowus is the standout investment platform in Singapore for one reason that no competitor can match: it is the only MAS-licensed platform authorised to accept investments from all three pools of Singapore money — CPF Ordinary Account (OA), CPF Special Account (SA), SRS, and personal cash — through a single, unified interface. For Singaporeans looking to maximise tax-advantaged investing while also growing their cash portfolio under consistent fund management, this is transformative. Founded in 2017 and holding a full MAS Capital Markets Services licence (Licence No. CMS101261), Endowus operates as a Licensed Fund Management Company (LFMC) and is one of the few robo-advisors regulated to manage CPF monies.

The fund selection is genuinely institutional. Rather than retail share classes of popular index funds, Endowus negotiates access to lower-cost institutional tranches from Dimensional Fund Advisors, PIMCO, BlackRock, Amundi, and Schroders — funds typically restricted to family offices and institutional investors with minimums in the millions. Critically, Endowus rebates 100% of trailer fees (also called fund retrocessions) back to investors. In practice, this means the effective fund expense ratio you pay through Endowus is often lower than what you would pay buying the same fund directly through a retail platform or bank. The access fee of 0.25–0.60% p.a. (sliding scale by AUM) is charged on top of the fund's expense ratio, but once trailer rebates are factored in, total cost of ownership is highly competitive.

Endowus is best suited to long-term, passive investors — particularly those with significant CPF balances who want to move OA funds out of the 2.5% guaranteed rate and into a globally diversified equity portfolio. The Cash Smart account, which allocates to money market and short-duration bond funds, currently yields 3.5–4.3% p.a. with T+1 liquidity, making it a compelling alternative to bank savings accounts. The Fund Smart feature allows self-directed investors to construct custom portfolios from over 300 vetted funds. The platform does not offer direct stock or ETF trading, so active traders will need a separate brokerage account.

### 2. StashAway — Best for Beginners

<img src="/images/places/best-investment-platforms-singapore/stashaway.jpg" alt="StashAway" className="w-full rounded-lg my-4 object-cover" style={{height: "300px"}} />

StashAway launched in Singapore in 2017 and quickly became the default recommendation for investors starting out, for good reason. There is no minimum investment amount — you can start with SGD 1 — and the onboarding process, which takes under ten minutes, is genuinely one of the smoothest in the market. StashAway is licensed by MAS as a Registered Fund Management Company (RFMC) and holds a CMS licence for fund management (Licence No. CMS101657).

The core investment methodology is the Economic Regime-based Asset Allocation (ERAA) framework, a proprietary macroeconomic model that dynamically adjusts portfolio allocations based on prevailing economic conditions — specifically the combination of GDP growth and inflation signals. In practice, this means the portfolio composition shifts between risk-on and risk-off configurations as the economic regime changes, rather than simply rebalancing to a fixed target. StashAway's portfolios are built entirely from low-cost ETFs (iShares, Vanguard, SPDR) listed on US exchanges, which provides broad global diversification. Fees are tiered: 0.80% p.a. on the first SGD 25,000, declining to 0.50% for SGD 25,001–50,000, 0.40% for SGD 50,001–100,000, and 0.20% for AUM above SGD 1,000,000.

For beginners, StashAway's biggest advantage is simplicity combined with genuine sophistication under the hood. You set a risk level, deposit money, and the platform handles everything else. The Simple Guaranteed account (currently ~3.3–3.8% p.a. on cash, capital guaranteed) provides a safe place to park emergency funds while still earning more than most bank savings accounts. StashAway does not support CPF or SRS investing, which is a meaningful gap for Singaporean investors with large tax-advantaged balances. SRS investing was added in late 2024 and rolled out gradually — verify current availability in-app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which investment platform is best for CPF investing in Singapore?**

Endowus is the only platform that accepts CPF Ordinary Account (OA) and Special Account (SA) funds alongside SRS and personal cash. For CPF equity investing in individual stocks, Phillip Securities (POEMS) and several traditional brokers participate in the CPF Investment Scheme (CPFIS). Note that CPF SA monies can only be invested in CPFIS-eligible products, which excludes most overseas ETFs and equities. Always verify CPFIS eligibility on the CPF Board website before transferring funds.

**Are robo-advisors safe in Singapore?**

All MAS-licensed robo-advisors in Singapore are required to hold client assets in segregated trust accounts with licensed custodian banks — meaning your money is legally separate from the platform operator's own assets and is protected in the event of the operator's insolvency. Endowus, StashAway, and Syfe all use DBS Bank or State Street Bank as their Singapore custodian. They are also subject to MAS annual audits and capital adequacy requirements. The risk is investment risk (market volatility), not counterparty risk to the platform itself.

**What fees should I expect to pay on an investment platform in Singapore?**

Costs fall into two categories. For robo-advisors, you pay a platform/management fee (0.20–0.80% p.a. depending on AUM tier) plus the underlying fund's expense ratio (typically 0.10–0.50% p.a. for ETF-based portfolios). For brokerages, you pay per-trade commissions (0.03–0.12% for SGX stocks, with minimums of SGD 0.99–10.00) and potentially custody fees for foreign-listed securities. Platform fees, inactivity fees, and FX conversion charges vary by provider — always read the fee schedule before funding an account.

**Do I need a CDP account to invest in Singapore stocks?**

You do not strictly need a CDP account — nominee custody brokers like Webull and some overseas-connected accounts hold shares on your behalf. However, CDP ownership gives you direct legal title to your shares, which means you receive corporate actions (dividends, rights issues, AGM notices) directly from the company's registrar. CDP-connected brokers in this review include moomoo, Tiger Brokers, Interactive Brokers, DBS Vickers, and POEMS. Opening a CDP account is free and takes five minutes at any SGX counter or online at cdp.sgx.com.

**What is the minimum amount needed to start investing in Singapore?**

Minimum investment requirements vary significantly. StashAway and Syfe have no minimum investment requirement. Endowus requires SGD 1,000 for cash portfolios and SGD 10,000 for CPF investing. Most stock brokerages have no minimum account balance but require at least enough to purchase a board lot (100 shares) of your chosen stock, which varies by share price. FSMOne's RSP allows SGD 50/month into unit trusts. The most accessible entry points for beginners are StashAway or Syfe for passive investing, or Webull for US fractional shares from USD 5.
