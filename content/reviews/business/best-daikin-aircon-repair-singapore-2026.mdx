## What to Look for When Hiring a Daikin Aircon Repair Service

**Daikin brand training or certification.** Daikin's inverter technology differs materially from generic aircon systems. Technicians trained on Daikin units understand how to interpret Daikin-specific fault codes (A1, E1, E7, L5, U4, and others), measure refrigerant pressure correctly for Daikin R-32 systems, and diagnose PCB board and inverter drive issues without unnecessary component replacement. Ask the company directly whether their technicians have Daikin training before booking.

**Fault diagnosis before repair quotation.** A reputable Daikin repair company will diagnose the fault before quoting repair costs. Be cautious of companies that quote a flat "repair fee" without first identifying the specific component at fault. Proper Daikin repair diagnosis covers: fault code history, refrigerant pressure measurement, compressor current draw, and PCB board output testing.

**Refrigerant handling compliance.** Singapore's National Environment Agency (NEA) requires that technicians handling refrigerants including Daikin's R-32 and R-410A hold the relevant certification. R-32 is mildly flammable and requires specific handling precautions. Always confirm that the technician attending holds the appropriate NEA certification before refrigerant work begins.

**Itemised written quotation.** For any Daikin repair involving component replacement (compressor, PCB board, fan motor, capacitor), insist on an itemised written quote that separates parts cost from labour cost before work begins. This protects you from post-service surprises and gives you a basis to compare quotes across providers.

**Parts sourcing and warranty.** Ask whether the company uses Daikin OEM parts or aftermarket alternatives, and what warranty applies to replaced components. OEM Daikin compressors and PCB boards carry manufacturer warranty; aftermarket parts may carry shorter or no warranty. A good Daikin repair company will confirm parts provenance before beginning the job.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does Daikin aircon repair cost in Singapore in 2026?

Daikin aircon repair costs in Singapore in 2026 depend on the fault type. Fault diagnosis and call-out fee: SGD 50–80. Refrigerant top-up (R-32 or R-410A): SGD 80–180 per unit depending on gas type and recharge volume required. PCB board replacement: SGD 250–600 depending on the board type and Daikin model. Compressor replacement: SGD 600–1,800 depending on unit capacity and whether OEM or compatible parts are used. Fan motor replacement: SGD 180–350. Capacitor replacement: SGD 80–150. Always request an itemised written quote before any work begins to avoid post-service cost disputes.

### How do I know if my Daikin aircon needs repair or just servicing?

Your Daikin unit needs repair rather than servicing if it is displaying an error code on the indoor unit display, if cooling performance has not improved after a recent chemical wash, if the outdoor unit is making unusual noise (grinding, clanking, or high-pitched squealing), if the unit cycles on and off repeatedly without reaching the set temperature, or if there is visible ice on the indoor coil or outdoor unit pipes. These symptoms indicate a mechanical or electrical fault rather than a cleaning issue. Standard general servicing or chemical wash will not resolve these problems.

### What are the most common Daikin fault codes and what do they mean?

Common Daikin fault codes and their typical causes: **A1** indicates PCB board fault on the indoor unit. **C4/C9** indicates thermistor sensor error. **E1** indicates PCB board fault on the outdoor unit. **E7** indicates outdoor unit fan motor fault. **L5** indicates inverter compressor overcurrent, often associated with a failing compressor or incorrect refrigerant charge. **U4** indicates communication error between indoor and outdoor units, often a wiring or PCB issue. These codes should be read and interpreted by a trained Daikin technician: do not attempt to clear fault codes without first identifying and resolving the underlying cause.

### How long does a Daikin aircon repair take in Singapore?

Most standard Daikin repairs take one to three hours on-site. Refrigerant top-up with a pre-identified leak source: 1–2 hours. PCB board replacement: 1–2 hours if the replacement board is available. Capacitor or fan motor replacement: 1 hour. Compressor replacement: 3–6 hours and may require a second visit if the compressor unit must be ordered. Complex multi-split system fault diagnosis may require an initial diagnostic visit followed by a repair visit once parts are sourced.

### Should I repair or replace my Daikin aircon unit?

As a general rule, if repair costs exceed 50% of the cost of a new equivalent unit, replacement is more economical. For a Daikin unit under five years old, most repairs (including PCB board replacement and refrigerant recharge) are economically worthwhile. For a unit over ten years old with a failing compressor, the cost of a new Daikin split unit (SGD 800–1,500 supply and install) is often comparable to compressor replacement costs, and the new unit comes with a fresh manufacturer warranty. Ask your repair technician for an honest assessment before committing to major component replacement on an aged unit.

### Is Daikin aircon covered under warranty for repairs in Singapore?

Daikin Singapore offers a standard 1-year parts and labour warranty on new residential units, extendable to 5 years on the compressor when registered with Daikin. Warranty repairs must be performed by Daikin-authorised service centres. If your unit is within the warranty period and the fault is not caused by misuse or improper installation, contact Daikin Singapore directly at 1800 363 0606 before booking an independent repair company. Using a non-authorised company for warranty-period repairs may void the remaining warranty.

### Why is my Daikin aircon not cold even after a gas top-up?

If your Daikin unit is still not cooling after a refrigerant top-up, the most likely causes are: a refrigerant leak that was not properly identified and sealed before recharging (gas will escape again within days to weeks); a dirty or blocked evaporator coil that is restricting heat exchange; a failing compressor that cannot build adequate refrigerant pressure even with a correct charge; or an incorrect refrigerant charge volume. A proper Daikin diagnostic should measure both high-side and low-side refrigerant pressure, not just perform a top-up by weight. If the system loses pressure within weeks of a top-up, insist on a leak detection check before authorising a further recharge.

### How often should I service my Daikin aircon to prevent major repairs?

Every three months is the standard recommendation for residential Daikin split units in Singapore. Regular quarterly servicing prevents the build-up of dust and mould on the evaporator coil, keeps the condensate drain clear, and allows a technician to identify developing issues (low refrigerant pressure, worn contactor, degraded fan capacitor) before they escalate into expensive repairs. Daikin units running continuously in Singapore's tropical climate with no maintenance accumulate faults faster than units in temperate climates: deferred servicing is the single most common cause of avoidable Daikin repair calls.
