## What to Look for When Hiring a Flooring Contractor in Singapore

**Subfloor preparation.** Professional flooring installation starts with the subfloor, not the flooring material. Inadequate levelling causes hollow spots under vinyl, creaking in laminate, and cracked grout in tiles. Always ask contractors how they assess and prepare the subfloor before installation: reputable contractors include a levelling compound application for uneven surfaces as standard rather than installing over an imperfect base.

**Material specification in quotation.** A professional flooring quotation specifies the flooring brand, product name, thickness, wear layer (for vinyl), and underlay type. Vague quotations that state only "vinyl flooring" or "laminate" without brand or specification make it impossible to compare proposals accurately. Request that all material details be listed in writing before confirming the job.

**Acoustic underlay compliance.** For condominium and high-rise HDB installations, many MCST management councils require a minimum acoustic underlay specification. Confirm with your MCST what impact sound level (IIC or ICIASF) is required, and ask your contractor to confirm the underlay specification meets this standard before installation. Failing this requirement can result in complaints from lower-floor neighbours and demands to reinstall.

**Warranty on material and installation.** Reputable flooring contractors provide a separate warranty on materials (typically covered by the manufacturer) and installation workmanship (typically 1 to 2 years from the contractor). Confirm both are in writing. Material defects such as delamination or surface wear are covered by the manufacturer; installation defects such as hollow spots, lifting, or edge separation are the contractor's responsibility.

**Review recency and project diversity.** In the flooring market, contractors who install one type of flooring well do not always deliver equivalent results across all types. Check that reviews cover your specific flooring type and are recent. A contractor with 300 reviews covering vinyl plank may lack competency in parquet or marble: verify the match between your project and the contractor's demonstrated track record.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does flooring installation cost in Singapore?

Vinyl plank flooring costs SGD 3 to SGD 8 per square foot installed, depending on thickness and brand. Laminate flooring is similar at SGD 3 to SGD 7 per square foot installed. Engineered timber flooring costs SGD 6 to SGD 15 per square foot installed. Marble and homogeneous tiles range from SGD 5 to SGD 20 per square foot depending on tile size and material grade.

### What is the best flooring type for HDB flats in Singapore?

Vinyl plank (LVP/SPC) is the most popular choice for Singapore HDB flats due to its durability, water resistance, ease of maintenance, and acoustic performance with appropriate underlay. Laminate is a cost-effective alternative for drier areas. Parquet and engineered timber are preferred for their aesthetic warmth but require more maintenance in Singapore's humid climate.

### Do I need to remove old flooring before installing new flooring?

In most cases, yes. Installing new vinyl or laminate over existing flooring raises the floor level, creates acoustic issues, and can cause height discrepancies at doors and transitions. Professional contractors typically remove existing flooring as part of the installation scope. Confirm whether removal and disposal is included in the quotation.

### What is the difference between SPC and LVP vinyl flooring?

SPC (Stone Plastic Composite) is a rigid core vinyl plank that is highly stable in temperature and humidity changes, making it ideal for Singapore. LVP (Luxury Vinyl Plank) refers broadly to vinyl plank flooring, which may include both flexible and rigid core variants. SPC is generally more stable and durable than standard flexible LVP in tropical conditions. Ask your contractor to confirm the core type when specifying vinyl flooring.

### How long does flooring installation take for an HDB flat?

A 4-room HDB flat vinyl plank installation typically takes one to two days including old floor removal and subfloor preparation. Tile installations take longer: typically 3 to 5 days due to adhesive cure time between layers. Parquet or timber floor installation takes 2 to 3 days. Factor in additional time for levelling compound if the subfloor has significant height variation.

### Is underlay required for vinyl flooring in Singapore condos?

Most condominium MCST management offices require an acoustic underlay with a minimum specification for vinyl and laminate floor installations. Failing to meet this requirement can result in neighbour complaints and demands to reinstall. Always confirm the MCST requirement before purchasing flooring and ensure your contractor confirms their underlay specification meets the standard.

### What should I avoid when choosing a flooring contractor in Singapore?

Avoid contractors who provide quotations without specifying flooring brand and product name, those who propose to install over existing flooring to avoid removal costs, contractors whose reviews are all from a short period (potential review inflation), and firms who cannot provide a written quotation with material specification. Very low quotations often omit subfloor preparation or use inferior underlay.

### How do I maintain vinyl plank flooring in Singapore?

Vacuum or dry-mop daily to remove grit that scratches the surface. Clean with a damp mop and pH-neutral floor cleaner weekly. Avoid excess water at seams and edges. Use furniture pads on chair and table legs. Keep direct sunlight exposure minimal as UV causes colour fade over time. With proper care, quality SPC vinyl plank lasts 15 to 25 years in Singapore conditions.

### Can flooring be installed during a BTO handover period?

Yes. Most homeowners schedule flooring installation as the first trade after HDB BTO handover, before any furniture or carpentry work. The ideal sequence is: demolition if required, then flooring, then electrical first fix, then carpentry, then painting. Confirm with your renovation contractor the correct sequence to avoid damage to newly installed flooring.

### What is a safe lead time for booking a flooring contractor in Singapore?

For standard HDB flooring projects, book 2 to 4 weeks in advance. During peak BTO collection periods (typically February to April and August to October) and Chinese New Year, popular contractors fill their schedules 4 to 6 weeks out. If your project has a fixed timeline, confirm availability before agreeing to any renovation sequence.
