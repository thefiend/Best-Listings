## What to Look for When Hiring an A&A Works Contractor in Singapore

**BCA registration and licensing.** Any contractor performing structural works in Singapore must hold an active BCA (Building and Construction Authority) General Builder licence or Specialist Builder licence for the relevant scope. A&A works involving reinforced concrete, structural modifications, or extension of gross floor area require BCA submission and approval before works commence. Verify your contractor's licence status on the BCA directory before signing any contract.

**Scope clarity: structural vs. non-structural A&A.** Not all A&A works are equal in regulatory complexity. Non-structural additions, internal partition changes, ceiling modifications, and cosmetic alterations are handled under the HDB renovation permit framework or a qualified person submission for private properties. Structural A&A, room additions, extensions, R.C. works, and changes to load-bearing elements require a full BCA submission with an appointed Qualified Person (QP). Confirm which category your project falls under before selecting a contractor, as not all firms on the market are equipped for structural submissions.

**Single contractor vs. separate structural and renovation engagement.** For A&A projects that combine structural works with interior renovation, the most efficient arrangement is a single contractor managing both scopes. Separate structural and renovation contractors introduce coordination risks: scheduling conflicts, accountability gaps when defects arise at the interface of structural and interior works, and no single point of contact during BCA inspection. Contractors like SinHao E&C PTE. LTD. that cover the full A&A-to-interior scope eliminate this coordination problem entirely.

**Pricing transparency and formal scope of works.** A&A projects are prone to variation because structural conditions uncovered during demolition frequently change scope. Reputable contractors provide a formal scope of works document alongside their quotation, with a clear variation order process for scope changes. Contractors who quote verbally or resist a written scope of works document are a significant risk in any A&A project.

**Response time and communication during BCA approval.** A&A projects in Singapore involve coordination with BCA, the QP, and sometimes URA or HDB during the approval stage. A contractor who communicates proactively during this stage, flagging submission status, inspection dates, and any approval queries, is significantly more valuable than one who leaves clients to chase updates. Test their communication quality before committing by sending a detailed initial enquiry and evaluating both the speed and specificity of the response.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are A&A works in Singapore?

A&A works refers to additions and alterations, which is the technical term used by BCA for any construction works that add to or alter an existing building. This includes room additions, extensions, structural modifications, roofing changes, and alterations to the building envelope. A&A works are distinct from new build construction and from purely cosmetic renovation, which does not require BCA submission.

### Do A&A works require BCA approval in Singapore?

Yes, for any structural A&A works. Structural additions and alterations, including extensions, new rooms, changes to load-bearing elements, and reinforced concrete works, require a BCA building plan submission approved by BCA before works commence. A Qualified Person (QP), typically a Professional Engineer or Registered Architect, must be appointed to prepare and submit the plans. Minor non-structural alterations may not require BCA approval but still require HDB or MCST clearance for managed properties.

### How much do A&A works cost in Singapore?

Minor non-structural A&A works, such as internal partition changes or minor room alterations, typically range from SGD 5,000 to SGD 30,000. Structural room additions or extensions for landed properties start at around SGD 50,000 to SGD 100,000 and can exceed SGD 300,000 for major multi-storey additions involving R.C. structures, roofing, and full interior fit-out. BCA submission fees and QP professional fees are additional costs, typically SGD 3,000 to SGD 15,000 depending on scope.

### How long do A&A works take in Singapore?

Minor non-structural A&A works typically take 2 to 6 weeks from commencement to completion. Structural A&A projects requiring BCA submission take longer overall because BCA plan approval typically requires 4 to 8 weeks before physical works can commence. Physical construction for a structural addition, depending on scope, typically takes 2 to 6 months. Factor in the full timeline from QP appointment through BCA approval to physical completion when planning your project.

### What is the difference between A&A works and renovation in Singapore?

Renovation covers interior works including carpentry, tiling, painting, plumbing, electrical, and fitting-out without changing the building's structural envelope. A&A works involve changes to the building itself: adding rooms, extending floor area, altering rooflines, or modifying structural elements. A&A works require BCA regulatory involvement; renovation generally does not, except for HDB permit requirements on certain trade works. Many projects involve both scopes running concurrently.

### Do HDB flats qualify for A&A works?

HDB flats have very limited scope for A&A works compared to landed properties. BCA's definition of A&A in an HDB context is tightly constrained: HDB does not permit extension of gross floor area in standard flat units. Most works in HDB flats that homeowners describe as A&A are technically renovation works requiring an HDB renovation permit. If you believe your HDB project genuinely requires structural modification, consult a QP first to clarify what is permissible under HDB guidelines before engaging a contractor.

### What is a QP (Qualified Person) and do I need one for A&A works?

A Qualified Person is a Professional Engineer (PE) or Registered Architect (RA) registered with BCA, appointed to take professional responsibility for a building project submission. For structural A&A works, a QP is mandatory to prepare and submit building plans to BCA. The QP also supervises the works and certifies completion. Your contractor may have an established relationship with a QP or you may need to separately appoint one. Confirm this arrangement before signing the construction contract.

### Are A&A works contractors in Singapore licensed?

BCA licensing requirements apply to contractors performing structural works: General Builder Class 1 or Class 2 licences, or Specialist Builder licences for specific trade categories. Contractors performing purely non-structural A&A or renovation works in private properties are not subject to mandatory licensing, though HDB renovation contractors must be registered with HDB. Always verify BCA licence status for any contractor proposing structural A&A works at bizsafe.mom.gov.sg or the BCA directory.

### What are red flags when hiring an A&A works contractor in Singapore?

Red flags include: no verifiable BCA licence for structural scope, verbal-only quotation with no formal scope of works document, inability to name their appointed QP for structural submissions, no prior A&A project references upon request, requests for more than 20% upfront before BCA approval is obtained, and resistance to a written variation order process. ### How do I verify an A&A contractor's BCA licence?
