## What to Look for When Hiring a Hair Loss Treatment Clinic in Singapore

**Credentials and qualifications.** Not every business calling itself a hair loss clinic employs qualified trichologists or licensed medical professionals. A certified trichologist (certified by the International Association of Trichologists or equivalent body) is the minimum for non-medical scalp treatment. If a clinic offers PRP injections, hair transplants, or prescription medications, a licensed doctor must be on-site. Always ask before booking.

**Diagnosis before treatment.** A reputable hair loss treatment clinic in Singapore will assess your scalp before recommending anything. This means a scalp analysis under magnification, questions about your medical history, diet, stress levels, and any medications you are taking. Any clinic that skips diagnosis and immediately recommends their most expensive package is a yellow flag regardless of their Google rating.

**Google reviews and recency.** A 4.7+ rating with 80+ reviews is the minimum credibility threshold. More important than the average is the recency: look for reviews posted in the last 3 months. A clinic with 500 reviews but nothing recent may have changed management or declined in quality. Consistent monthly reviews indicate ongoing client flow and sustained standards.

**Treatment method transparency.** Ask the clinic to explain what ingredients or technologies they use and why those are appropriate for your specific hair loss type. Androgenetic alopecia, postpartum shedding, traction alopecia, and stress-induced telogen effluvium all require different approaches. A clinic that offers the same treatment to every client is not treating your condition; it is selling you a product.

**Price clarity and no hard-sell tactics.** Singapore's hair loss industry has a well-documented history of high-pressure sales tactics where clients are pushed into expensive packages during their first consultation. Legitimate clinics provide itemised pricing for individual sessions and packages, allow you to start with a single session to assess results, and do not use time-limited offers or emotional pressure to close a sale.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does hair loss treatment cost in Singapore?
Basic scalp treatment sessions at trichology salons range from SGD 80 to SGD 200 per visit. Herbal treatment programmes typically cost SGD 60–120 per session. Medical procedures like PRP (platelet-rich plasma) therapy range from SGD 300 to SGD 800 per session, usually requiring a course of 3–6 sessions. FUE hair transplant surgery in Singapore starts at approximately SGD 8,000 and can exceed SGD 25,000 for larger graft counts.

### What is the best hair loss treatment in Singapore for men?
For androgenetic alopecia (male pattern baldness), the most effective non-surgical treatments are minoxidil (topical application), finasteride (oral medication, prescription required), and PRP therapy. Scalp treatment clinics can support these with targeted scalp care to maintain a healthy follicle environment. For advanced hair loss, FUE hair transplant surgery at a licensed clinic is the most permanent solution. V Aesthetics Hair Grow and Sage Medical Clinic both offer medically-supervised hair loss treatment for men in Singapore.

### What is the best hair loss treatment in Singapore for women?
Female pattern hair loss and postpartum shedding respond well to a combination of scalp treatment, nutritional support, and targeted medical therapies such as topical minoxidil or low-level laser therapy. Herbal treatments from Bee Choo Origin and trichology-based care from TrichoLab and Anagen Scalp are popular options for women who prefer non-medical approaches. Freia Medical is specifically recommended for women seeking a clinical approach in a comfortable, private setting.

### How do I know if my hair loss is serious enough to see a specialist?
If you are losing more than 100 hairs per day consistently for more than 3 months, noticing a widening parting, visible scalp through thinning hair, or patches of hair loss, you should consult a hair loss specialist. A qualified trichologist or dermatologist can identify whether the cause is androgenetic, hormonal, nutritional, or stress-related and recommend appropriate treatment. Early intervention produces significantly better outcomes than waiting.

### Are hair loss treatment clinics in Singapore regulated?
Medical hair loss procedures (PRP, hair transplants, prescription medications) must be performed by or under the supervision of a licensed doctor registered with the Singapore Medical Council. Non-medical scalp treatment salons are not subject to the same regulatory oversight, which is why it is important to verify staff qualifications independently. The Aesthetics Medical Practice guidelines from the Ministry of Health govern medical aesthetic clinics in Singapore.

### How many sessions does hair loss treatment take before seeing results?
Most trichology and scalp treatment programmes recommend an initial course of 8–12 sessions, typically weekly or fortnightly, before a maintenance schedule begins. Visible reduction in shedding is commonly reported after 4–6 sessions. Medical treatments like PRP therapy require 3–6 sessions before results become measurable, with improvements continuing for 6–12 months after the course. Hair transplant results are visible after 6 months and fully mature at 12–18 months.

### What are red flags when choosing a hair loss clinic in Singapore?
