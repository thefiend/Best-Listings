## What to Look for When Booking an Airport Transfer in Singapore

**Real-time flight tracking capability.** A professional airport transfer service in Singapore tracks your flight in real time and adjusts driver arrival time accordingly, whether your flight lands 30 minutes early or 90 minutes late. Operators who require you to call and confirm your own landing time are not operating a professional airport transfer: they are operating a pre-booked taxi. Ask directly whether flight tracking is automatic and included, not optional.

**Meet-and-greet with name card at arrivals.** The standard for a professional Changi Airport transfer includes the driver waiting in the arrivals hall with a card displaying your name or your guest's name. This eliminates ambiguity for first-time visitors, executives from overseas, and any traveller navigating a crowded arrivals hall after a long flight. Confirm whether name card meet-and-greet is included or a paid add-on before booking.

**Fixed pricing confirmed before dispatch.** Reputable airport transfer operators in Singapore provide a fixed price per route before the vehicle is sent. Variable pricing, meter-based billing, or rates that are "confirmed on arrival" are red flags. Always request written confirmation of the total price: including waiting time, expressway tolls, and any terminal surcharges: before confirming the booking.

**Changi terminal knowledge across T1 to T4.** Changi Airport Terminal 4 is a separate facility connected to the main terminal cluster by a dedicated shuttle bus, which creates confusion for drivers unfamiliar with its layout and pickup zone. Confirm that your operator has specific experience with your arrival terminal. This question alone will identify whether you are dealing with a Changi-experienced operator or one who works primarily from Grab bookings.

**Wait time policy for delayed flights.** Your flight being delayed is not your fault, and a professional airport transfer service will not charge you for reasonable delays that fall within a complimentary waiting period. Confirm the length of the complimentary wait (Elite Lux Limousine offers 60 minutes), what happens if the delay exceeds that period, and whether the driver will wait without additional cost until the flight arrives. The policy tells you how the operator views the service relationship.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does an airport transfer from Changi cost?
A standard Changi Airport transfer to a Singapore city-centre hotel or the CBD costs SGD 45–90 depending on vehicle class and distance. Economy saloon bookings for solo or two-passenger arrivals typically run SGD 45–65. Premium vehicles (Mercedes-Benz E-Class or S-Class) run SGD 75–120 for the same route. 7-seater maxi cabs for groups with luggage typically cost SGD 60–90. VIP packages with Bentley or additional protocol services are quoted directly and run SGD 150 and above.

### What is meet-and-greet at Changi Airport?
Meet-and-greet is a service where the driver enters the Changi Airport arrivals hall and waits for your arrival with a name card or tablet displaying your name. This means the passenger does not need to locate the vehicle or navigate to a pickup zone after clearing customs: the driver comes to them inside the terminal. This is standard for professional airport transfers and is particularly valuable for first-time visitors to Singapore, VIP guests, and international arrivals unfamiliar with Changi's layout.

### Do drivers track flights for arrival pickups?
Professional airport transfer operators in Singapore track flight arrivals in real time using flight number and arrival terminal data.
