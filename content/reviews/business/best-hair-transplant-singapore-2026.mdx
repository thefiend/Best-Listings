## What to Look for When Hiring a Hair Transplant Clinic

**Doctor credentials and SMC registration.** Hair transplant surgery in Singapore must be performed by a Singapore Medical Council-registered doctor. Before booking, verify the performing doctor's SMC registration number on the SMC website. Clinics that are vague about which doctor performs the procedure, or that use non-medical staff for surgical steps, should be avoided entirely.

**Treatment scope and diagnosis first.** The best clinics assess your hair loss cause before recommending a treatment. Hair loss can result from androgenetic alopecia, alopecia areata, nutritional deficiencies, hormonal imbalances, or scarring, each requiring a different intervention. A clinic that recommends a transplant or expensive program without first identifying the cause is prioritising revenue over results.

**Review specificity and volume.** A 4.8-star rating with 300+ specific reviews describing graft survival rates, recovery times, and 12-month outcomes is far more reliable than a 5.0 with 15 generic reviews. Look for reviews that name the procedure, the doctor, and a measurable outcome.

**Technique and technology.** For surgical transplant, FUE (Follicular Unit Excision) is the current standard, offering less scarring and faster recovery than FUT (strip harvesting). Robotic-assisted FUE (ARTAS) improves graft selection consistency. For non-surgical options, PRP with growth factor concentration, exosome therapy, and Regenera Activa represent the current evidence-based tier, though results vary by individual.

**Pricing transparency and graft-based quotes.** Reputable surgical clinics quote based on graft count assessed during consultation, not a fixed package price. If a clinic advertises a flat SGD 6,000 transplant without examining your donor density, the graft count and result will be unpredictable. Get an itemised quote after an in-person or video consultation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a hair transplant cost in Singapore?

Surgical FUE hair transplants in Singapore cost SGD 8,000–30,000 depending on the number of grafts required, typically 1,500–4,000 grafts for most male pattern baldness cases. Non-surgical options such as PRP therapy cost SGD 600–1,500 per session, with most programs requiring 3–6 sessions. Scalp micropigmentation ranges from SGD 1,500–5,000 for a full treatment.

### Is hair transplant surgery safe in Singapore?

Yes, when performed by an SMC-registered doctor in a licensed medical clinic, hair transplant surgery in Singapore is safe and well-regulated. FUE is a minimally invasive outpatient procedure with a recovery period of 7–14 days. Risks include temporary swelling, infection, and graft shock loss, all of which are manageable with proper aftercare from an experienced clinic.

### How do I choose the best hair transplant clinic in Singapore?

Verify the doctor's SMC registration, request before-and-after photos from actual patients treated at the clinic (not stock images), ask for a graft-count-based quote after in-person assessment, and check Google reviews for specific outcome descriptions. Avoid clinics that pressure you into packages during the first consultation.

### What is the difference between FUE and FUT hair transplant?

FUE (Follicular Unit Excision) extracts individual follicular units from the donor area using a micro-punch tool, leaving tiny circular scars. FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation) removes a strip of scalp from the donor area, leaving a linear scar. FUE is the preferred method in Singapore today due to faster recovery and less visible scarring, though FUT can be more efficient for patients needing very high graft counts.

### What is scalp micropigmentation and is it a hair transplant?

Scalp micropigmentation (SMP) is not a hair transplant. It is a cosmetic tattooing procedure that deposits pigment dots on the scalp to mimic the appearance of hair follicles or a shaved head, creating the illusion of density or a defined hairline. SMP is permanent but does not grow hair. It is best suited for patients with complete hair loss, post-transplant density gaps, or those not eligible for surgery.

### How many sessions does PRP hair treatment take in Singapore?

Most PRP hair treatment programs in Singapore involve 3–6 initial sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart, followed by maintenance sessions every 3–6 months. Results are typically visible after the third session. PRP is most effective for early to mid-stage androgenetic alopecia and less effective for scarring alopecia or complete hair loss.

### How long does recovery take after FUE hair transplant?

Most patients return to desk work within 3–5 days after FUE. Redness, scabbing, and mild swelling resolve within 7–14 days. Transplanted hairs shed in weeks 2–6 (shock loss), which is normal, and new hair growth begins from month 3–4. Full results are visible at 12–18 months.

### Are hair transplant results permanent in Singapore?

Surgical FUE results are permanent in the transplanted area because grafts are taken from the donor zone, which is genetically resistant to DHT-driven hair loss. However, hair loss can continue in non-transplanted areas, meaning some patients require additional procedures or medical management to maintain overall density over time.

### What is Regenera Activa and does it work?

Regenera Activa is a minimally invasive non-surgical treatment that harvests micro-grafts from a small punch biopsy behind the ear, then reinjects the concentrated progenitor cells into thinning areas of the scalp. Clinical studies show it can arrest hair loss progression and stimulate regrowth in patients with androgenetic alopecia. Results typically appear over 6–12 months and a single treatment can last 2–3 years. It is offered at The Clifford Clinic and M-Aesthetic Clinic on this list.
