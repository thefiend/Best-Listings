## How We Ranked These Commercial Aircon Services

Our ranking methodology for the best commercial aircon services in Singapore in 2026 covers six criteria. **BCA and NEA licensing:** contractors performing gas-related refrigerant work on commercial systems must hold a valid BCA Electrical Licence (EL) or Refrigeration and Air-conditioning (RAC) contractor registration; unregistered companies were excluded regardless of review score. **Commercial system capability:** providers with verified experience on VRV/VRF multi-zone networks, ceiling cassette units, and ducted systems ranked above those limited to residential split units. **Brand certification:** technicians certified by Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Panasonic, Trane, or Carrier scored higher on technical expertise dimensions. **Maintenance SLA depth:** companies offering structured service contracts with defined visit schedules, response time guarantees, and parts coverage ranked higher than those operating purely on a call-out basis. **Verified review volume and recency:** minimum 4.8 stars with at least 500 verified Google reviews, with review activity in the preceding 3 months confirming continued service quality. **Response time:** test enquiries were sent to each company and reply times were measured; companies responding within 2 hours on a business day scored full marks. BestThingReview.com evaluated over 50 providers before arriving at this shortlist of 10.

## What to Look for When Hiring a Commercial Aircon Service

**BCA licensing and refrigerant compliance.** Singapore's Building and Construction Authority mandates that contractors performing any gas-related work on refrigerant systems hold the appropriate BCA licence. Before signing a commercial maintenance SLA, request the contractor's BCA registration number and verify it on the BCA directory. Non-compliant work voids equipment warranties and may expose tenants or building owners to regulatory liability.

**Commercial system type compatibility.** Not all aircon contractors have the equipment or training to maintain VRV/VRF multi-zone systems, ducted fan coils, or precision cooling units. Confirm that the contractor has verifiable experience with your specific system type and brand. A contractor experienced on Daikin VRV systems may not have equivalent capability on Trane or Carrier ducted systems.

**Maintenance SLA structure.** A well-structured commercial maintenance SLA should specify: number of scheduled visits per year, scope of each visit (what is included and excluded), response time guarantee for emergency callouts, parts and refrigerant coverage terms, and the process for escalating faults beyond routine maintenance. Vague SLAs that describe "comprehensive servicing" without itemising scope are a risk.

**Review volume, recency, and commercial context.** A company with strong residential review volume may not have equivalent commercial capability. When reading reviews, filter for mentions of office, retail, F&B, industrial, or multi-unit contexts. Review recency within the past three months is a reliable indicator of continued operational quality, particularly for contractors that may have changed ownership or key staff.

**Response time for emergency callouts.** For commercial premises where system downtime has direct revenue consequences, confirm the contractor's actual emergency response time commitment and whether it is backed by a contractual SLA or is simply a best-effort estimate. Companies that offer 24/7 coverage with a documented 2-hour response window are meaningfully different from those offering "emergency service" with no defined response standard.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does commercial aircon servicing cost in Singapore in 2026?

Commercial aircon maintenance in Singapore typically costs SGD 80–200 per cassette or split unit per service visit, depending on unit type, system complexity, and scope. Annual maintenance contracts for small commercial premises (6–10 units) generally range from SGD 1,200–4,000 per year depending on visit frequency and whether parts and refrigerant are included. VRV/VRF system maintenance is priced separately and usually quoted per zone or per outdoor condensing unit, with annual contracts typically starting from SGD 3,000 for a standard 10-zone office system. Always request an itemised written quote before committing.

### What is the difference between commercial and residential aircon servicing?

Residential servicing typically covers standard split-unit fan coils and window units with limited diagnostic complexity. Commercial servicing involves more complex system types including ceiling cassette units, VRV/VRF multi-zone networks, ducted fan coils, precision air handling units, and chiller-based systems. Commercial jobs require BCA-licensed technicians for gas-related work, often involve coordinating access across multiple floors or units, and require structured service reporting for facility management records. Response time expectations and SLA structures are also fundamentally different: commercial downtime has direct business impact that justifies priority contracts.

### Do commercial aircon contractors in Singapore need to be BCA-licensed?

Yes. In Singapore, contractors performing any work involving refrigerant gas handling (including top-ups, leak detection, and recovery) on commercial systems must hold a valid BCA Electrical Licence or be registered under BCA's list of licensed Refrigeration and Air-conditioning contractors. This requirement covers any system using regulated refrigerants including R410A, R32, and R134a. Always verify a contractor's BCA licence status before signing a commercial maintenance agreement, and retain the verification record.

### How often should commercial aircon units be serviced in Singapore?

The standard recommendation for commercial premises in Singapore is monthly servicing for systems under high daily load (F&B kitchens, server rooms, retail with high footfall) and quarterly servicing for standard office environments. BCA guidelines and most manufacturer warranties require a minimum of four services per year for commercial systems. Environments with elevated contamination (grease, dust, or chemical exposure) should increase frequency to every 4–6 weeks to maintain cooling efficiency and indoor air quality compliance under NEA standards.

### What is a VRV or VRF system and why does it need specialist maintenance?

VRV (Variable Refrigerant Volume, a Daikin trademark) and VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow, the generic equivalent) are multi-zone refrigerant-based systems that allow a single outdoor condensing unit to serve multiple indoor units simultaneously, with independent temperature control per zone. They are common in mid-sized to large offices and retail spaces in Singapore. Maintenance requires specialist training because fault diagnosis involves reading system controller logs, understanding refrigerant circuit balance, and using brand-specific service software. Technicians without VRV/VRF certification frequently misdiagnose system faults, leading to unnecessary part replacements or failure to identify refrigerant circuit imbalances.

### What should a commercial aircon maintenance SLA include?

A well-structured commercial aircon maintenance SLA should specify: the number of scheduled service visits per year and their scope; a maximum response time for emergency callouts (typically 2–4 hours); whether refrigerant top-ups are included or charged separately; whether minor parts such as filters and drain pans are included; the reporting format (verbal or written service report per visit); and the escalation process for faults beyond routine maintenance. Any SLA that does not specify response time commitments or excludes gas-related work from scope should be renegotiated before signing.

### Can commercial aircon contractors handle new installations in Singapore offices?

Yes, the companies on this list offer commercial installation services for new office, retail, and F&B fit-outs. A full commercial installation covers system design (unit placement, zone planning, piping and electrical routing), equipment supply, installation, BCA documentation, and manufacturer warranty registration. For larger premises, a site survey and load calculation should be conducted before equipment is specified. Always confirm the contractor holds the appropriate BCA licence for installation work and obtain a detailed scope of works before committing.

### What are the red flags when hiring a commercial aircon service company?

Key red flags: no verifiable BCA licence for gas-related work; quoting a single flat price for "commercial servicing" without specifying system type or unit count; no written service reports provided after visits; inability to provide references from commercial clients in your industry sector; no defined emergency callout response time; and significantly below-market pricing with no transparency on what is included or excluded. For F&B and retail clients, also check that the contractor has experience with your specific system brand, as VRV/VRF and ducted systems require brand-specific training that not all contractors hold.

### How do I switch commercial aircon service providers in Singapore?

Review your existing SLA for the notice period (usually 30–60 days) and any exclusivity clauses. Request a handover service report from the outgoing contractor documenting current system condition, recent service history, and any outstanding faults. Commission a baseline inspection from the incoming contractor before the handover to independently verify system condition. Ensure the new contractor has access to manufacturer warranty documentation and any BCA-related service records for your premises.
