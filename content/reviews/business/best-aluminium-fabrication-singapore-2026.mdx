## What to Look for When Hiring an Aluminium Fabrication Company in Singapore

**Google reviews and recency.** A 4.5-star Google rating with at least 20 reviews is the baseline for aluminium fabrication contractors in Singapore. Recency matters more than the total average: a company with 100 reviews but nothing posted in the past 6 months may have changed ownership or declined in quality. Prioritise contractors with a steady stream of recent reviews that reference specific project types similar to yours, whether windows, doors, canopies, or custom commercial work.

**Project scope alignment.** Aluminium fabrication covers a wide range: residential window replacement, custom gates and grilles, commercial shopfronts, aluminium canopy structures, curtain wall systems, and full A&A works. Not every contractor can handle every scope. Match the contractor's listed services and review examples to your actual project before enquiring. A specialist in residential window installations may lack the equipment and permits for a large commercial canopy structure, and vice versa.

**In-house fabrication vs. reseller.** Contractors who fabricate in-house have direct control over quality, lead times, and custom specifications. Contractors who resell fabricated components from third parties introduce an additional quality control gap and often have less flexibility on non-standard dimensions. Ask directly whether the company fabricates on-site or outsources fabrication. In-house fabricators generally provide faster turnaround on custom work and clearer accountability when issues arise.

**Drawings and shop drawings before fabrication.** For any project beyond a simple window replacement, a credible aluminium fabrication contractor should provide shop drawings or fabrication drawings before work commences. These confirm dimensions, material specifications, finishes, and fixing details in advance. Contractors who skip this step and fabricate from verbal measurements alone introduce avoidable errors into custom work. Require drawings before approving any fabrication.

**Material specification and warranty.** Aluminium sections vary significantly in alloy grade, wall thickness, and surface finish (anodised, powder-coated, or mill finish). A professional contractor will specify the aluminium grade and finish in their quotation and should be able to provide product data sheets on request. Ask what warranty is provided on workmanship and what the process is if fabricated components fail or are damaged during installation. Reputable contractors provide written warranties of at least one year on workmanship.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does aluminium fabrication cost in Singapore?

Pricing varies widely by scope. Residential aluminium window replacement typically costs SGD 150 to SGD 600 per window depending on size, type, and glass specification. Aluminium gate fabrication and installation ranges from SGD 300 to SGD 1,500. Custom aluminium canopy structures start from SGD 3,000 and can reach SGD 30,000 or more for large commercial installations. Commercial shopfront systems typically range from SGD 5,000 to SGD 50,000 depending on area and specification.

### How do I choose an aluminium fabrication company in Singapore?

Start with Google Maps reviews: look for a 4.5-star or higher rating with at least 20 recent reviews from clients who describe projects similar to yours. Confirm whether the contractor fabricates in-house or outsources fabrication, ask for shop drawings before any fabrication begins, and request an itemised quotation that specifies material grade, finish, and dimensions. Compare at least two quotations before committing.

### What aluminium profiles and finishes are available in Singapore?

The most common residential finishes are anodised aluminium (silver or bronze tones) and powder-coated aluminium (available in any RAL colour). Mill finish is used in industrial applications. Common alloy grades are 6063-T5 and 6061-T6; 6063-T5 is standard for windows and doors while 6061-T6 is used for structural components requiring higher strength. Always confirm which grade and finish is included in the quotation.

### How long does aluminium fabrication and installation take?

Standard residential window and door replacements are typically measured, fabricated, and installed within 1 to 3 weeks. Custom aluminium gates and grilles take 2 to 4 weeks. Canopy structures and commercial shopfronts require 3 to 8 weeks depending on drawings, permit requirements, and scale. Larger structural aluminium projects can take 2 to 4 months.

### Do aluminium contractors in Singapore need permits?

Structural aluminium works, including canopy additions and A&A works on HDB and condominium properties, require permits from the relevant authority (HDB, BCA, or MCST depending on property type) before works commence. Standard window and door replacements on HDB flats are permitted within HDB's renovation permit framework. Always confirm with your contractor what permits are required for your specific project and verify that they will apply before works begin.

### What are red flags when hiring an aluminium fabrication contractor?

Red flags include: no physical business address or verifiable registration, requests for full payment before fabrication begins, inability or unwillingness to provide shop drawings or detailed quotations, no written warranty on workmanship, quotations with no material specification (grade, finish, wall thickness), and suspiciously low pricing without explanation. Any contractor who starts fabrication based on verbal measurements alone without a confirmed drawing is introducing unnecessary risk.

### What is the difference between aluminium extrusion and aluminium fabrication?

Aluminium extrusion is the manufacturing process that produces aluminium profiles in standard shapes by forcing aluminium alloy through a die. Aluminium fabrication refers to the cutting, joining, welding, drilling, and finishing of extruded profiles into functional products such as windows, doors, canopies, or enclosures. Most Singapore aluminium contractors buy extruded profiles and fabricate finished products. Companies like Chip Aik Aluminium manufacture their own profiles; most others source from extrusion suppliers.

### Can aluminium fabrication companies in Singapore handle HDB renovation projects?

Yes, but contractors performing structural works or window and door replacement on HDB flats must comply with HDB renovation permit requirements. Standard window replacement using HDB-approved aluminium frame types is permitted under a standard renovation permit. Structural canopy additions require a separate permit. Confirm with your contractor that they will obtain all required HDB permits before commencement, as non-compliant works may require costly reinstatement.

### What maintenance do aluminium fabricated products require?

Powder-coated and anodised aluminium require minimal maintenance: regular cleaning with mild soap and water, and periodic checking of seals, gaskets, and hardware for wear. Avoid abrasive cleaners or steel wool on coated surfaces. Sliding and folding door tracks should be cleaned and lubricated with a silicone-based lubricant annually. Hardware (hinges, locks, handles) should be checked for secure fixing every 12 months. Well-fabricated aluminium products have a service life of 20 to 30 years with basic maintenance.

### How do I compare quotations from different aluminium fabrication companies?

Ensure each quotation specifies: aluminium alloy grade and wall thickness, surface finish (anodised or powder-coated), glass type and thickness if applicable, hardware brand and model, fabrication and installation timeline, payment schedule, and warranty terms. Quotations that specify only dimensions and a total price make meaningful comparison impossible. Request itemised quotations from at least two contractors and flag any line items that differ significantly between quotes before making a decision.
