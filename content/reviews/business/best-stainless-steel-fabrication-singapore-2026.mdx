## What to Look for When Hiring a Stainless Steel Fabrication Company in Singapore

**Google reviews and verified track record.** A 4.5-star rating with 20 or more reviews is the minimum credibility threshold for stainless steel fabrication contractors in Singapore. Review recency matters as much as the aggregate score: a fabricator with 150 reviews but no recent activity may have changed ownership or scaled down capacity. Prioritise businesses with consistent, recent reviews mentioning specific project types similar to yours.

**Material grade and certification capability.** Stainless steel is not a single material. Grade 304 is standard for architectural and food-grade applications; grade 316 is required for marine, coastal, and chemically aggressive environments. Ask the fabricator to confirm the material grade they will use and to provide a mill certificate verifying it. For structural and pressure applications, weld procedure qualifications (WPS/PQR) and welder certifications are additionally required. Fabricators who cannot provide these documents should not be engaged for certified applications.

**Fabrication vs. supply-and-install distinction.** Some businesses on this list supply standard stainless steel items; others fabricate bespoke pieces; others do both. Confirm whether the firm fabricates in-house or outsources fabrication to a third party. In-house fabrication provides tighter quality control and faster turnaround for revisions. Ask to see their workshop or portfolio of completed custom pieces before committing.

**Scope match and specialist vs. generalist.** A fabricator who specialises in food-grade kitchen equipment is not automatically the right choice for architectural decorative metalwork or structural steelwork. Match the fabricator's demonstrated portfolio to your specific application. For structural projects touching BCA-regulated works, confirm the fabricator holds relevant contractor registration and can provide signed-off drawings and engineering endorsement where required.

**Response time and quoting process.** Send a test enquiry describing your project and note how long the fabricator takes to respond, whether they ask clarifying questions about material grade and finish requirements, and whether they provide an itemised quote or a lump-sum figure. Fabricators who respond with detailed, itemised quotations within 24 to 48 hours have the process infrastructure to manage your project reliably. Vague or verbal-only quotes are a consistent predictor of billing disputes during execution.

**BCA and NEA compliance for regulated applications.** For food premises, commercial kitchens, and food processing facilities, stainless steel equipment and surfaces must meet NEA hygiene standards and pass inspection. For structural steel elements in new buildings and A&A works, BCA contractor licensing and PE-endorsed drawings are mandatory. Verify the fabricator's registration status through the BCA directory before engagement on regulated projects.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does stainless steel fabrication cost in Singapore?

Custom stainless steel fabrication in Singapore ranges from SGD 200 to SGD 2,000 for small residential items such as gates, grilles, and handrails. Commercial kitchen equipment and countertops typically cost SGD 1,500 to SGD 15,000 depending on size and complexity. Large-scale structural or industrial stainless steel fabrication projects start from SGD 20,000 and can exceed SGD 200,000 for full fit-outs or complex process equipment. Material grade, surface finish specification, and weld certification requirements are the primary cost drivers beyond size.

### What is the difference between grade 304 and grade 316 stainless steel?

Grade 304 is the most common austenitic stainless steel, suitable for general architectural, food-grade, and indoor applications. Grade 316 contains molybdenum, which provides significantly higher corrosion resistance, making it the correct choice for marine environments, coastal locations, chemical processing, and pharmaceutical applications. In Singapore's coastal climate, grade 316 is recommended for any exposed external stainless steel installation. Always confirm grade specification in writing before fabrication commences.

### Do I need a permit for stainless steel structural works in Singapore?

Structural works involving stainless steel or mild steel that affect the structural integrity of a building require BCA-approved drawings, a PE-endorsed structural plan, and a building permit from URA or HDB depending on property type.
