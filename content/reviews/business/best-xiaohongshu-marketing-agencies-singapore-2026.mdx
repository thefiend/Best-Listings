## What to Look for When Hiring a Xiaohongshu Marketing Agency

Choosing the right XHS marketing agency in Singapore requires evaluating criteria that are specific to this platform, not generic digital marketing capabilities. The following factors separate agencies that can genuinely deliver results on Xiaohongshu from those that are selling a service they have repurposed from Instagram or Facebook management.

**Native Chinese-language content capability.** Xiaohongshu is a Chinese-language platform. Content that performs on XHS is written natively in Simplified or Traditional Chinese by strategists who understand the platform's cultural context, not translated from English by a tool or a junior executive. Ask specifically who writes the content, what their Chinese language proficiency is, and whether they can provide samples of XHS notes they have produced for other clients.

**Understanding of the XHS algorithm and note format.** The Xiaohongshu algorithm rewards content with high saves and shares over passive likes, prioritises detailed and visually rich notes over brief captions, and penalises content that includes external links or obvious commercial language in the note body. An agency that understands these mechanics will produce content structured to maximise algorithmic distribution: one that does not will produce content that gets posted but never discovered.

**KOL and KOC network depth.** The distinction between KOLs (Key Opinion Leaders: large influencers with 100,000-plus followers) and KOCs (Key Opinion Consumers: nano and micro creators with 1,000 to 50,000 followers) is critical on Xiaohongshu. KOCs generate content that users trust as authentic peer recommendation rather than paid endorsement. An agency with a genuine KOC network in Singapore across relevant categories (beauty, lifestyle, F&B, fashion, home) can deliver campaigns that perform organically in the discovery feed rather than running purely as paid placements.

**InfoFlow advertising credentials.** InfoFlow is Xiaohongshu's native paid advertising format. It requires a verified XHS business account, CNY-denominated advertising credits, and specific creative guidelines that differ from Meta or Google ad formats. Confirm that any agency you consider has direct experience running InfoFlow campaigns and can show you reporting examples from live campaigns rather than theoretical capability.

**Client outcome evidence.** Ask for case studies specific to XHS, not generic social media case studies. Evidence should include follower growth rates, note reach and save rates, enquiry volume attributed to XHS, and if relevant, conversion data tied to XHS traffic. Agencies that cannot produce XHS-specific outcome evidence are likely managing the platform as an afterthought within a broader social media package rather than as a primary specialisation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Xiaohongshu and why is it important for Singapore businesses?

Xiaohongshu, also called RED or Little Red Book, is a Chinese social media and e-commerce platform founded in Shanghai in 2013. It combines Instagram-style visual content with product reviews, lifestyle recommendations, and shopping features. As of 2026, Xiaohongshu has over 300 million monthly active users globally, with Singapore's user base concentrated in the Chinese-speaking population aged 18 to 35. For Singapore businesses targeting Chinese-speaking consumers in categories such as beauty, fashion, F&B, travel, home, and professional services, Xiaohongshu is now a primary discovery channel: users search for restaurant recommendations, product reviews, and service recommendations on XHS before making purchase decisions in the same way they use Google for English-language searches.

### How much does Xiaohongshu marketing cost in Singapore?

Xiaohongshu organic account management in Singapore typically starts from SGD 800 per month, covering account setup, content creation (4 to 8 notes per month), and community management. Full-service XHS campaigns including native Chinese content, KOL or KOC coordination, and InfoFlow paid advertising range from SGD 1,200 to SGD 5,000 per month depending on content volume, influencer tier, and advertising budget. KOL campaigns are quoted individually based on creator follower count and engagement rate. InfoFlow ad spend is billed separately through the platform and is typically denominated in CNY, with Singapore agencies managing the currency conversion and platform billing on behalf of clients. Enterprise-level XHS campaigns with high-volume KOL coordination can exceed SGD 15,000 per month.

### What is the difference between a KOL and a KOC on Xiaohongshu?

A KOL (Key Opinion Leader) is a Xiaohongshu creator with a large following, typically 100,000 followers or more, who is recognised as an authority or celebrity in their niche. KOLs generate high reach but their content is perceived by audiences as sponsored or commercial. A KOC (Key Opinion Consumer) is a regular user with a smaller but highly engaged audience, typically 1,000 to 50,000 followers, who creates authentic product reviews and lifestyle content based on personal experience. On Xiaohongshu specifically, KOC content is often more commercially effective than KOL content because the platform's community trusts peer recommendations over celebrity endorsements. Many Xiaohongshu marketing strategies combine KOC seeding for authentic reach with selective KOL campaigns for brand-building visibility.

### How long does it take to see results from Xiaohongshu marketing in Singapore?

Organic XHS account growth typically takes 3 to 6 months to reach a meaningful follower base and consistent discovery feed placement, as the platform's algorithm rewards consistent, high-quality posting rather than immediate viral content. Paid InfoFlow advertising campaigns can generate immediate reach and traffic from the first week, though conversion optimisation typically improves over the first 4 to 8 weeks as the algorithm learns the target audience. KOC seeding campaigns can generate measurable engagement and enquiry volume within 2 to 4 weeks of creator posting. Singapore businesses should plan for a 3-month minimum commitment to organic XHS management before evaluating results.

### Can Singapore businesses run Xiaohongshu InfoFlow ads without a Chinese entity?

Yes. Singapore-registered businesses can run InfoFlow ads on Xiaohongshu through a verified XHS business account. However, the platform's ad credits are denominated in CNY and the account verification process requires specific documentation. Most Singapore Xiaohongshu marketing agencies manage this on behalf of their clients: they hold the business account, manage the CNY credit top-up, and bill clients in SGD at a transparent exchange rate plus service fee. Ask any agency you consider exactly who holds the business account, who manages the ad credit, and what happens to the account if you end the engagement.

### What types of content perform best on Xiaohongshu for Singapore brands?

XHS content that performs strongly in Singapore consistently shares the following characteristics: it is written natively in Simplified Chinese (not translated English), it uses detailed first-person narrative or review format rather than brand broadcast language, it includes high-quality visuals with natural rather than heavily styled aesthetics, it answers a specific question or addresses a real purchase consideration, and the note title contains searchable keywords that match the terms Singapore XHS users are actually searching for. Categories with particularly strong XHS performance in Singapore include beauty and skincare reviews, F&B recommendations with specific dish recommendations and pricing, interior design and home renovation inspiration, travel itineraries covering Singapore and nearby destinations, and professional services reviews covering clinics, legal services, and financial advisors.

### Is Xiaohongshu effective for B2B marketing in Singapore?

XHS is primarily a B2C platform, but it has demonstrated effectiveness for certain B2B categories in Singapore where professional decision-makers are also active users on the platform for personal reasons. Professional services including accounting, legal, immigration consulting, and financial advisory have seen effective B2B lead generation from XHS in Singapore, as Chinese-speaking business owners and entrepreneurs use the platform for professional recommendations in the same way they use it for consumer discovery. For B2B businesses in these sectors, a well-executed XHS presence can generate qualified professional enquiries from an audience that is difficult to reach cost-effectively through Google Ads or LinkedIn. For traditional B2B verticals such as manufacturing, logistics, or enterprise software, XHS is unlikely to deliver commercially meaningful results.

### What questions should I ask a Xiaohongshu marketing agency before hiring them?

Ask these seven questions to evaluate any XHS agency in Singapore: (1) Who writes the content and what is their Mandarin language proficiency level? (2) Can you show me XHS accounts you currently manage and their analytics for the past three months? (3) Do you manage InfoFlow ad campaigns directly on the platform, and can you show me a sample campaign report? (4) What is your KOC network depth in Singapore, specifically in my product or service category? (5) Who owns the XHS business account: you or the client? (6) What does a typical month of content production include: number of notes, content types, posting frequency? (7) What results have you achieved for other Singapore businesses in my industry: specifically follower growth, note reach, and enquiry volume?

### How is Xiaohongshu different from Instagram for Singapore marketers?

Xiaohongshu and Instagram share a visual format but operate with fundamentally different user behaviours and content mechanics. XHS users actively search for specific recommendations using keywords within the platform: the search function is used more like Google than like Instagram's hashtag discovery. This means XHS content must be optimised for searchability within the platform, not just visual appeal. XHS's algorithm prioritises saves and shares over likes, rewarding content that is genuinely useful and referenced repeatedly over content that is aesthetically pleasing but shallow. The comment section on XHS is more substantive than Instagram: users ask detailed questions and expect answers, creating community engagement that is closer to a forum than a social feed. For Singapore marketers, this means XHS requires a content strategy built around answering real questions and providing genuine recommendations, not broadcasting brand messages.
