## What to Look for When Hiring an AI SEO Service

**1. Specific GEO and AEO methodology, not generic AI claims.** Any agency can put "AI SEO" on their website in 2026. The differentiator is whether they can explain, specifically, how they optimise for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT web citations, and Perplexity answers. Ask for their process on structured data, entity optimisation, and content authority building. Vague answers about "AI-powered tools" are a red flag.

**2. Schema and structured data implementation as a core service.** AI systems rely heavily on structured data to identify and classify content for citation. FAQ schema, HowTo schema, Article schema, and LocalBusiness schema are all citation-relevant. An agency that does not include structured data as a standard deliverable is not doing full AI SEO.

**3. Content authority measurement, not just keyword rankings.** GEO success is measured differently from traditional SEO. Ask how the agency measures AI citation frequency, share-of-voice in AI-generated answers, and brand mention rates in generative search results. If they only report keyword positions, they are not doing AI SEO.

**4. Track record of adapting to algorithm changes.** AI Overview expansion, ChatGPT web search launch, and Perplexity's growth have each shifted the visibility landscape. Agencies that have operated across multiple major search shifts and maintained client satisfaction through them are more likely to adapt successfully to the next change.

**5. Transparency on content ownership and deliverables.** AI SEO requires content creation as well as technical implementation. Ensure that all content produced is yours, that you retain access to all data and reporting platforms, and that you can exit the engagement without losing the work completed to date.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is GEO and AEO and why does it matter for Singapore businesses in 2026?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is the practice of optimising content and technical signals so that a business is cited and referenced by generative AI systems including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) is the closely related practice of structuring content to be selected as the definitive answer to specific questions by AI-powered search engines and voice assistants. For Singapore businesses, both matter because a growing share of online search journeys now begin or end with an AI-generated answer rather than a click on a traditional blue-link result. A business that ranks well in Google's traditional results but does not appear in AI Overviews is invisible to users who accept the AI-generated answer without scrolling further.

### How is AI SEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses primarily on getting pages to rank in Google's organic results through keyword optimisation, backlink acquisition, and technical site health. AI SEO, GEO, and AEO require an additional and distinct set of optimisations: entity clarity (ensuring AI systems correctly understand what your business is and what it does), structured data implementation (helping AI systems categorise and cite your content accurately), content depth and topical authority (AI systems favour sources that cover topics comprehensively rather than targeting isolated queries), and brand authority signals across multiple platforms (AI systems are more likely to cite brands that appear authoritatively across multiple trusted sources). Traditional SEO is a prerequisite for AI SEO, but doing traditional SEO well no longer guarantees AI visibility.

### How long does it take to see results from AI SEO and GEO optimisation?

The timeline for AI search visibility improvements varies based on starting point, competition, and the scope of changes made. Technical changes including structured data implementation and site architecture adjustments can influence AI Overview appearances within four to eight weeks in some cases. Content authority building is a longer process: establishing topical depth across a subject area typically takes three to six months before consistent AI citation patterns emerge. Brand authority signals including mentions in external publications, directories, and authoritative sources develop over six to twelve months. Businesses should treat AI SEO as a twelve-month minimum investment rather than a short-term campaign, with measurable interim milestones established at the outset.

### Can my business appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers if I am not already ranking in Google?

Appearing in ChatGPT web search and Perplexity citations requires being present in the web pages those systems index, which means Google ranking is a prerequisite for most businesses. ChatGPT's web browsing and Perplexity both pull from publicly indexed web content: a page that does not rank in Google will generally not be indexed or cited by these systems either. The correct sequence is to establish strong Google organic rankings first, then layer AI citation optimisation on top through structured data, authoritative content architecture, and brand authority signals. Businesses attempting to skip traditional SEO fundamentals and go straight to AI citation optimisation will find limited results.

### What industries in Singapore benefit most from GEO and AEO optimisation?

Industries with high informational search intent benefit most from GEO and AEO in Singapore. Professional services including law firms, accounting, and financial advisory benefit because clients research extensively before engaging, and being cited in AI answers for regulatory or advisory queries builds significant credibility. Healthcare and aesthetics businesses benefit because patients and clients use AI search to compare treatment options and find qualified providers. Education and training providers benefit because AI Overviews are frequently triggered by course comparison and qualification queries. B2B technology companies benefit because enterprise buyers use AI-generated research summaries during vendor shortlisting. Any business where the customer journey involves a meaningful research phase before conversion is a strong AI SEO candidate.

### How do Singapore agencies measure AI SEO success?

The most rigorous AI SEO agencies in Singapore measure three types of outcomes: AI citation frequency (how often a brand or specific page is cited in Google AI Overviews for target query sets), share-of-voice in generative results (what percentage of AI-generated answers for a topic category include the client brand versus competitors), and referral traffic from AI-assisted sessions (users who clicked through from an AI-generated answer or summary). Traditional metrics including keyword rankings, organic traffic, and conversion rates remain relevant but are insufficient on their own for measuring AI SEO performance. Agencies that cannot provide AI-specific reporting against these metrics are not delivering full GEO and AEO services.

### Should I hire a separate agency for AI SEO or expect my existing SEO agency to cover it?

For most Singapore businesses, the practical question is whether their existing SEO agency understands GEO and AEO well enough to incorporate them into the current engagement. Many established agencies have added AI SEO language to their service pages without substantially changing their methodology. The way to test this is to ask your existing agency: specifically, which schema types they implement for AI citation, how they measure AI Overview appearances, and what content architecture changes they are making for topical authority. If the answers are vague or if they redirect the conversation to traditional keyword metrics, engaging a specialist AI SEO agency alongside or instead of your current provider is worth considering.

### Is AI SEO worth the investment for a small Singapore business?

For small Singapore businesses with limited marketing budgets, AI SEO is worth the investment if two conditions are met: the business operates in a category where AI Overviews and generative answers are already appearing for relevant queries, and the business has a clear conversion path from organic traffic. The first condition is easy to test: simply Google the key questions your prospective clients would ask and observe whether AI Overviews are already appearing above the organic results. If they are, your competitors are already competing for that AI visibility real estate whether or not they are actively optimising for it. The second condition ensures that the investment has a measurable commercial return rather than generating awareness without enquiries.
