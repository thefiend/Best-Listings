## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the Aspire referral reward?

Both parties must meet specific criteria. The referrer must be an existing Aspire Singapore customer. The referred business must be incorporated in Singapore with a valid Singapore business registration certificate and must not have previously registered with Aspire. The referrer and the director of the referred business must be different individuals.

### How much is the Aspire referral reward?

Both the referrer and the referred business each receive SGD $250. The payout occurs once the referred business has successfully opened an account and made a minimum total spend of SGD $100 at qualified merchants using the Aspire Card.

### What counts as a qualified merchant spend?

Spend must be at one of the listed SaaS or online marketing merchants, charged directly from the merchant's official website or application. Third-party marketplace purchases such as those made via the App Store, Play Store, or resellers do not qualify. Common qualifying expenses include Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Shopify, Xero, Canva, and Google or Meta advertising.

### When will the SGD $250 be credited?

Aspire credits the SGD $250 to both the referrer and the referred business after the referred business has completed account verification and reached the SGD $100 minimum qualified spend threshold.

### Can I use the referral code if my business is not incorporated in Singapore?

No. The campaign is restricted to businesses incorporated in Singapore with a valid Singapore business registration certificate. Foreign-incorporated businesses are not eligible.

### What happens if I spend at a merchant not on the qualified list?

Only spend at the listed qualified merchants counts toward the SGD $100 minimum. Spending at non-listed merchants, topping up e-wallets, or paying payment service providers does not contribute to the qualifying threshold and will not trigger the reward.

### Is there a risk of disqualification?

Aspire monitors for fraudulent activity including creating multiple accounts, seeking refunds on qualifying transactions, or any circumvention of the campaign rules. Disqualified participants forfeit the reward. For questions, contact support@aspireapp.com.
